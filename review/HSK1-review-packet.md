## 2. Grammar lessons (26)

### 1. Word order: who does what

Pattern: Subject + Time / Place + Verb + Object

- Chinese sentences follow the same basic order as English: subject, verb, object. 我喝茶 means 'I drink tea'.
- Verbs never change. There is no past-tense ending, no plural and no 'he drinks' form. 我喝, 他喝 and 我们喝 all use the same 喝.
- Words that say when and where normally come before the verb, not after it. 我昨天在家 means 'I was at home yesterday'. 爸爸晚上在家工作 means 'Dad works at home in the evening'. Time usually comes first, then place. (There are a few exceptions you will meet later, such as 我住在学校 and how long something lasted, 我住了三天.)

Common mistakes taught:
- Do not put the place or the time after the verb the way English does. Say 我在家吃饭 (I eat at home), not 我吃饭在家.

Examples:

- 我喝茶。  Wǒ hē chá.  — I drink tea.
- 哥哥吃鸡蛋。  Gēge chī jīdàn.  — My older brother eats eggs.
- 我们学习汉语。  Wǒmen xuéxí Hànyǔ.  — We study Chinese.
- 爸爸晚上在家工作。  Bàba wǎnshang zài jiā gōngzuò.  — Dad works at home in the evening.

### 2. 是: A is B

Pattern: A + 是 + B

- 是 links two nouns: 他是我的朋友 means 'He is my friend'. 我是学生 means 'I am a student'. It works like 'is / am / are' with nouns.
- In a plain statement, do not put 是 before an adjective. Say 她很漂亮, not 她是很漂亮 (see the lesson on describing things). Later you will meet 是 with an adjective for emphasis, but do not use it for plain description.
- To say no, put 不 in front: 我不是老师。 To ask a yes/no question, add 吗: 你是学生吗？

Common mistakes taught:
- In a plain statement do not put 是 before an adjective: 今天很冷 is the normal way, 今天是很冷 is not.

Examples:

- 我是学生。  Wǒ shì xuésheng.  — I am a student.
- 他是我的朋友。  Tā shì wǒ de péngyou.  — He is my friend.
- 她不是老师。  Tā bú shì lǎoshī.  — She is not a teacher.
- 你是中国人吗？  Nǐ shì Zhōngguó rén ma?  — Are you Chinese?

### 3. Describing things: 很, 太, 真, 非常, 有点儿

Pattern: Subject + Degree word + Adjective

- An adjective can be the whole predicate. There is no 是: 今天很冷 means 'It is cold today'.
- Usually 很 goes before the adjective. In a plain statement it often means just 'is' rather than a strong 'very': 她很忙. A bare adjective with nothing in front, like 她忙, sounds like a comparison ('she is busy, but others are not').
- For stronger feeling use 非常 (very, extremely), 真 (really) or 太…了 (too, so): 今天太冷了！ 有点儿 means 'a bit' and is often used for something you do not like: 今天有点儿冷.
- To say not, put 不 before the adjective and leave out 很: 这个房间不大。

Common mistakes taught:
- For plain description do not use 是 with an adjective: 她很漂亮, not 她是很漂亮.
- 太…了 can mean 'too' (太贵了, too expensive) or 'so / how' (太好了！ That is great!).

Examples:

- 今天很冷。  Jīntiān hěn lěng.  — It is very cold today.
- 这个房间不大。  Zhège fángjiān bú dà.  — This room is not big.
- 你的衣服真漂亮！  Nǐ de yīfu zhēn piàoliang!  — Your clothes are really pretty!
- 今天太冷了！  Jīntiān tài lěng le!  — It is so cold today!
- 我今天有点儿忙。  Wǒ jīntiān yǒudiǎnr máng.  — I am a bit busy today.

### 4. Yes/no questions with 吗

Pattern: Statement + 吗 + Yes/no question

- Say a normal statement and add 吗 at the end: 你是学生吗？ The word order does not change.
- Answer with the verb: 你喝茶吗？ — 喝。 or 不喝。 For 是 questions, answer 是 or 不是.
- A plain verb + 吗 question asks about a fact or a habit. 你喝茶吗？ means 'Do you drink tea?' It is not an offer. To offer something or ask what someone would like, use 想: 你想喝茶吗？ (see the lesson on 想 and 要).
- Do not use 吗 in a question that is already asked by a question word: say 你叫什么名字？ (not 你叫什么名字吗？). (A question word hidden inside a bigger sentence can still take 吗: 你知道他在哪里吗？ means 'Do you know where he is?')

Common mistakes taught:
- If 什么, 谁 or 哪儿 is already asking the question, leave 吗 off.
- 你喝茶吗？ asks whether you drink tea in general. To offer tea, say 你想喝茶吗？

Examples:

- 你是老师吗？  Nǐ shì lǎoshī ma?  — Are you a teacher?
- 他喜欢茶吗？  Tā xǐhuan chá ma?  — Does he like tea?
- 你想喝水吗？  Nǐ xiǎng hē shuǐ ma?  — Would you like some water?
- 你认识他吗？  Nǐ rènshi tā ma?  — Do you know him?

### 5. Question words: 什么, 谁, 哪儿, 几, 多少, 怎么

Pattern: Subject + Verb + Question word

- The question word goes where the answer would go. 你吃什么？ is answered with 我吃米饭. 她是谁？ is answered with 她是我的老师.
- Common ones: 什么 (what), 谁 (who), 哪儿 or 哪里 (where), 哪 + measure word (which: 哪个, 哪本书), 几 (how many, small numbers, with a measure word), 多少 (how many, how much), 怎么 (how: 这个字怎么读), 怎么样 (how about it, how is it), 什么时候 (when).
- Do not add 吗 to these questions when the question word is the thing being asked.

Common mistakes taught:
- Use 几 for small numbers and put a measure word after it (几口人, 几岁). Use 多少 for bigger or unknown amounts, like 多少钱.

Examples:

- 你想吃什么？  Nǐ xiǎng chī shénme?  — What do you want to eat?
- 她是谁？  Tā shì shéi?  — Who is she?
- 你家在哪儿？  Nǐ jiā zài nǎr?  — Where is your home?
- 你家有几口人？  Nǐ jiā yǒu jǐ kǒu rén?  — How many people are in your family?
- 这个多少钱？  Zhège duōshǎo qián?  — How much is this?
- 这个字怎么读？  Zhège zì zěnme dú?  — How do you read this character?
- 今天天气怎么样？  Jīntiān tiānqì zěnmeyàng?  — How is the weather today?

### 6. Saying no: 不, 没(有), 不要

Pattern: Subject + 不 / 没 + Verb

- 不 negates most things: 是, adjectives, habits, plans and wanting. 我不是老师。我不喝茶。这个不大。
- 没(有) negates 有 and things that did not happen: 我没有钱。小明今天没上学。Never say 不有. 'Have' is always negated with 没.
- 不要 is a command, 'don't!': 不要说话！ It can also be a firm refusal. For a plain 'I don't want to', 不想 is the safe choice: 我不想吃。
- Tone change: 不 is said bú before a fourth-tone syllable. 不是 is bú shì, and 不要 is búyào.

Common mistakes taught:
- Do not say 我不有钱. 'Have' is negated with 没: 我没有钱.
- 不要 is 'don't!'. For 'I don't want to', say 我不想…

Examples:

- 我不是老师。  Wǒ bú shì lǎoshī.  — I am not a teacher.
- 我没有钱。  Wǒ méiyǒu qián.  — I don't have any money.
- 小明今天没上学。  Xiǎomíng jīntiān méi shàngxué.  — Xiaoming did not go to school today.
- 不要在这儿说话。  Búyào zài zhèr shuōhuà.  — Don't talk here.

### 7. 有: having something

Pattern: Subject + 有 + Thing

- 有 means 'to have': 我有一个哥哥。我有两本书。
- The negative is 没有, never 不有: 我没有手机。
- Ask with 吗, or with 有没有: 你有手机吗？ or 你有没有手机？
- 有 is also used for 'there is / there are'. See the lesson on places and positions.

Common mistakes taught:
- Do not add 吗 to 有没有: say 你有没有手机？ or 你有手机吗？, not both.

Examples:

- 我有一个哥哥。  Wǒ yǒu yí ge gēge.  — I have an older brother.
- 我没有手机。  Wǒ méiyǒu shǒujī.  — I don't have a phone.
- 你有时间吗？  Nǐ yǒu shíjiān ma?  — Do you have time?
- 她有没有男朋友？  Tā yǒu méiyǒu nánpéngyou?  — Does she have a boyfriend?

### 8. Pronouns and 们

Pattern: Pronoun + 们 + Plural

- 我 I, 你 you, 他 he, 她 she, 它 it. Plurals: 我们 we, 你们 you (more than one), 他们 they (people, or a mixed group), 她们 they (all women), 它们 they (things or animals). He, she and it all sound like tā.
- 您 is a polite 'you' for one person: 老师，您好！ 大家 means 'everyone'.
- 们 makes plurals for people: 同学们, 孩子们. Do not use 们 after a number: 三个学生, not 三个学生们.
- 小 in front of a name makes it friendly, like 小明.

Common mistakes taught:
- Do not add 们 after a number: 三个学生 is right.

Examples:

- 我们都是学生。  Wǒmen dōu shì xuésheng.  — We are all students.
- 他们在学校。  Tāmen zài xuéxiào.  — They are at school.
- 老师，您好！  Lǎoshī, nín hǎo!  — Hello, teacher!
- 同学们好！  Tóngxuémen hǎo!  — Hello, classmates!
- 大家好！  Dàjiā hǎo!  — Hello, everyone!

### 9. 的: belonging and describing

Pattern: Owner or description + 的 + Noun

- 的 links an owner or a describing phrase to a noun: 我妈妈的手机 means 'my mom's phone'. 好吃的米饭 means 'tasty rice'.
- With family and close groups, 的 is usually left out: 我妈妈, 我家, 我们学校. For things, 的 is the normal choice: 我的书, 我的手机. (In casual speech it is sometimes dropped.)
- The noun can be dropped when it is clear: 这是我的。 means 'This is mine.'
- A short adjective often joins the noun directly: 新手机 (a new phone). With 很 or a longer phrase, add 的: 很好吃的米饭 (very tasty rice).

Common mistakes taught:
- 我的妈妈 is not wrong, but in everyday speech people say 我妈妈.

Examples:

- 这是我的书。  Zhè shì wǒ de shū.  — This is my book.
- 这是我妈妈的手机。  Zhè shì wǒ māma de shǒujī.  — This is my mother's phone.
- 我妈妈在家。  Wǒ māma zài jiā.  — My mom is at home.
- 这是很好吃的米饭。  Zhè shì hěn hǎochī de mǐfàn.  — This is very tasty rice.

### 10. 这, 那 and measure words

Pattern: 这 / 那 + Number + Measure word + Noun

- 这 means this and 那 means that. Before a noun they need a measure word: 这个人 (this person), 那本书 (that book). 这个 and 那个 can also stand alone: 我喜欢这个。 Plurals are 这些 and 那些. Places are 这儿 / 这里 (here) and 那儿 / 那里 (there).
- Chinese counts with a measure word between the number and the noun: 三个苹果, 两本书. The all-purpose one is 个.
- Some nouns want a specific one: 本 for books, 件 for clothes (and for matters), 只 (zhī) for small animals, 口 for people in a family, 家 for shops and companies, 块 or 元 for money, and 杯 for cups of drinks.
- Ask how many with 几 + measure word: 你有几本书？

Common mistakes taught:
- Use the right measure word: 三本书, not 三个书. Use 个 only when no special one fits.

Examples:

- 这个人是我的朋友。  Zhège rén shì wǒ de péngyou.  — This person is my friend.
- 我有两本书。  Wǒ yǒu liǎng běn shū.  — I have two books.
- 我想买一件新衣服。  Wǒ xiǎng mǎi yí jiàn xīn yīfu.  — I want to buy a new item of clothing.
- 我家有四口人。  Wǒ jiā yǒu sì kǒu rén.  — There are four people in my family.
- 我想喝一杯茶。  Wǒ xiǎng hē yì bēi chá.  — I would like a cup of tea.

### 11. Numbers, 两, money, age and 第

Pattern: Number + Measure word / unit

- 一 二 三 四 五 六 七 八 九 十. Then 十一 (11), 二十 (20), 二十一 (21). 一百 is 100 and 一百零五 is 105 (零 fills the gap). 一千 is 1,000.
- There are two words for two: 二 for counting, numbers and dates (二十, 二月), and 两 before a measure word (两个, 两本书).
- 半 means half: 一半 (half of it). Money uses 块 (spoken) or 元 (written), often with 钱: 三块钱, 三十元. One and a half yuan is 一块半.
- 第 makes an ordinal number: 第一天 means 'the first day'.
- Age uses 岁 and needs no 是: 我八岁。

Common mistakes taught:
- Say 两个人, not 二个人. But 二 is right in numbers and dates: 二十, 二月二号.

Examples:

- 这个手机三千块。  Zhège shǒujī sān qiān kuài.  — This phone is 3,000 yuan.
- 我有两个朋友。  Wǒ yǒu liǎng ge péngyou.  — I have two friends.
- 他八岁。  Tā bā suì.  — He is eight years old.
- 这个包子一块半。  Zhège bāozi yí kuài bàn.  — This bun is one and a half yuan.
- 今天是我学汉语的第一天。  Jīntiān shì wǒ xué Hànyǔ de dì yī tiān.  — Today is my first day of learning Chinese.

### 12. Time, dates and days

Pattern: Subject + Time + Verb

- Clock time is number + 点 (o'clock), then + 分 (minutes) or 半 (half): 七点, 八点零五分, 七点半. Say 两点 for 2 o'clock.
- Dates go from big to small: year, month, day. Say 月 and 号 (spoken) or 日 (written): 十月三十号. Weekdays are 星期 + number: 星期一 to 星期六. Sunday is 星期天 or 星期日. Ask with 几: 今天星期几？ 现在几点？
- A point in time goes before the verb, either right after the subject or at the start of the sentence: 我七点起床 or 七点我起床. Not after the verb. (How long something lasted is different and comes after the verb: 我住了三天.)
- A time or date does not need a verb: 现在七点半。 With 是 it also works: 现在是八点零五分。

Common mistakes taught:
- Say 两点 for 2 o'clock, not 二点.
- A point in time goes before the verb: 我七点起床, not 我起床七点.

Examples:

- 我七点起床。  Wǒ qī diǎn qǐchuáng.  — I get up at seven o'clock.
- 现在是八点零五分。  Xiànzài shì bā diǎn líng wǔ fēn.  — It is 8:05 now.
- 今天是十月三十号。  Jīntiān shì shí yuè sānshí hào.  — Today is October 30th.
- 今天星期几？  Jīntiān xīngqī jǐ?  — What day of the week is it today?
- 我星期天在家。  Wǒ xīngqītiān zài jiā.  — I am at home on Sunday.

### 13. 在: where something happens

Pattern: Subject + 在 + Place + Verb

- 在 means 'at / in'. 在 + place usually goes before the verb: 我在家吃饭 means 'I eat at home'. (A few verbs take it after, like 住在学校 'live at school'.)
- 在 can also be a verb, 'to be at': 我在家。他不在学校。
- Ask where with 在哪儿 or 在哪里: 你在哪儿工作？

Common mistakes taught:
- The place goes before the verb: 我在家吃饭, not 我吃饭在家.

Examples:

- 我在家。  Wǒ zài jiā.  — I am at home.
- 他们在家吃饭。  Tāmen zài jiā chī fàn.  — They eat at home.
- 你在哪儿工作？  Nǐ zài nǎr gōngzuò?  — Where do you work?
- 我在学校学习汉语。  Wǒ zài xuéxiào xuéxí Hànyǔ.  — I study Chinese at school.

### 14. Positions and where things are

Pattern: Thing + 在 + Place

- 上 (on) and 里 (inside) go right after the noun: 桌子上, 房间里. For under, outside, in front and behind, add 边 (side): 桌子下边, 房间外边, 学校前边, 学校后边. 这边 and 那边 work the same way.
- Thing + 在 + place says where the thing is: 书在桌子上。
- Place + 有 + thing says what is there: 桌子上有一本书。
- Place + 是 + thing identifies what is there: 我家外边是一家商店 (What is outside my home is a shop). 有 only says that something exists there.

Common mistakes taught:
- Do not use 在 for 'there is': 桌子上有一本书, not 桌子上在一本书.

Examples:

- 书在桌子上。  Shū zài zhuōzi shàng.  — The book is on the table.
- 杯子里有水。  Bēizi lǐ yǒu shuǐ.  — There is water in the cup.
- 桌子上有一本书。  Zhuōzi shàng yǒu yì běn shū.  — There is a book on the table.
- 我家外边是一家商店。  Wǒ jiā wàibian shì yì jiā shāngdiàn.  — Outside my home there is a shop.
- 学校在那边。  Xuéxiào zài nàbiān.  — The school is over there.

### 15. 想 and 要: wanting and offering

Pattern: Subject + 想 / 要 + Verb

- 想 + verb means 'want to' or 'would like to': 我想喝茶。 It is soft and polite, so it is the word to use when you offer or ask what someone wants: 你想喝茶吗？ (Would you like some tea?) 你想吃什么？ (What would you like to eat?)
- 要 also means 'want', but it is more direct. It works with a noun, which is how you order or buy: 我要一个包子。 With a verb: 我要休息。
- A plain verb question is not an offer. 你喝茶吗？ means 'Do you drink tea?' and asks about a habit. To ask 'Would you like tea?', add 想: 你想喝茶吗？
- The negative of 想 is 不想: 我不想吃。 不要 is 'don't!' or a firm refusal.

Common mistakes taught:
- 你喝茶吗？ is not an offer. It asks whether you drink tea. Offer with 你想喝茶吗？
- 不想 is 'do not want to'. 不要 is 'don't!'

Examples:

- 我想去中国。  Wǒ xiǎng qù Zhōngguó.  — I want to go to China.
- 你想喝什么？  Nǐ xiǎng hē shénme?  — What would you like to drink?
- 你想喝茶吗？  Nǐ xiǎng hē chá ma?  — Would you like some tea?
- 我要一个包子。  Wǒ yào yí ge bāozi.  — I'll have a bun.
- 我不想吃东西。  Wǒ bù xiǎng chī dōngxi.  — I don't want anything to eat.

### 16. 会, 能, 可以: can

Pattern: Subject + 会 / 能 / 可以 + Verb

- 会: you know how to, a skill you learned. 我会做饭。你会开车吗？
- 能: you are able to, or the situation allows it. 我明天能来。你能来吗？
- 可以: you are allowed to. It is how you ask for permission: 我可以坐这儿吗？ To say yes, answer 可以.
- They overlap: 能 is also used to ask permission. The negatives are 不会 (don't know how), 不能 (can't) and 不可以 (not allowed).

Common mistakes taught:
- Do not use 会 to ask permission: 我可以坐这儿吗？, not 我会坐这儿吗？

Examples:

- 我会说一点儿汉语。  Wǒ huì shuō yìdiǎnr Hànyǔ.  — I can speak a little Chinese.
- 你会开车吗？  Nǐ huì kāichē ma?  — Can you drive?
- 你明天能来吗？  Nǐ míngtiān néng lái ma?  — Can you come tomorrow?
- 我可以坐这儿吗？  Wǒ kěyǐ zuò zhèr ma?  — May I sit here?

### 17. 都, 也, 还, 再 and joining sentences

Pattern: Subject + 都 / 也 / 还 / 再 + Verb

- These adverbs go after the subject and before the verb or adjective: 我们都是学生。我也是学生。
- 都 means all or both: 我们都很好。 With 不, it means none of us: 我们都不喝茶。
- 也 means also or too. It goes before 不: 我也不是学生 means 'I am not a student either'. 我也是 means 'Me too'. Do not put 也 at the end of a sentence.
- 还 means also, else or still: 你还想吃什么？ (What else would you like to eat?)
- 再 means again, for something that has not happened yet: 明天再来。
- Two short sentences can sit side by side with a comma and no 'and': 爸爸是老师，妈妈也是老师。

Common mistakes taught:
- 也 goes before the verb: 我也喜欢茶, not 我喜欢茶也.
- 都 goes after the subject: 他们都在学校, not 都他们在学校.

Examples:

- 我们都是学生。  Wǒmen dōu shì xuésheng.  — We are all students.
- 我也是学生。  Wǒ yě shì xuésheng.  — I am a student too.
- 你还想吃什么？  Nǐ hái xiǎng chī shénme?  — What else would you like to eat?
- 明天再来。  Míngtiān zài lái.  — Come again tomorrow.
- 爸爸是老师，妈妈也是老师。  Bàba shì lǎoshī, māma yě shì lǎoshī.  — Dad is a teacher, and Mom is a teacher too.

### 18. A-not-A questions

Pattern: Subject + Verb + 不 + Verb + Object

- Another way to ask a yes/no question: say the verb, then 不, then the verb again. 你是不是老师？ 你去不去学校？ (Are you going to school or not?)
- For 有, use 没: 你有没有手机？
- Do not add 吗 to an A-not-A question.
- With two-syllable verbs the first verb is often shortened in speech: 你喜欢不喜欢茶？ can also be 你喜不喜欢茶？, and 你认识不认识他？ can be 你认不认识他？ Both are fine.

Common mistakes taught:
- No 吗 here: 你去不去？, not 你去不去吗？

Examples:

- 她是不是你们的老师？  Tā shì bú shì nǐmen de lǎoshī?  — Is she your teacher?
- 你去不去学校？  Nǐ qù bú qù xuéxiào?  — Are you going to school?
- 你有没有手机？  Nǐ yǒu méiyǒu shǒujī?  — Do you have a phone?
- 你喜欢不喜欢茶？  Nǐ xǐhuan bù xǐhuan chá?  — Do you like tea?

### 19. 吧 and 呢

Pattern: Sentence + 吧 / 呢

- 吧 softens a sentence. After a suggestion it means 'let's' or 'how about': 我们去吃饭吧。 It also makes a polite invitation gentler: 请坐吧 (Please, have a seat).
- 吧 can also check what you already think is true, like 'right?': 你没事吧？ (You are all right, aren't you?)
- 呢 asks 'and what about…?' by bouncing a question back: 我很好，你呢？ (I am fine, and you?)
- 呢 can also ask where something is: 你的手机呢？ (Where is your phone?)

Common mistakes taught:
- Use 吗 when you really want a yes/no answer. 吧 suggests or checks, 吗 asks.

Examples:

- 我们去吃饭吧。  Wǒmen qù chī fàn ba.  — Let's go and eat.
- 请坐吧。  Qǐng zuò ba.  — Please, have a seat.
- 我很好，你呢？  Wǒ hěn hǎo, nǐ ne?  — I am fine, and you?
- 你的手机呢？  Nǐ de shǒujī ne?  — Where is your phone?
- 你没事吧？  Nǐ méishì ba?  — Are you all right?

### 20. 在, 正在 and 呢: happening now

Pattern: Subject + 在 / 正在 + Verb + 呢

- Put 在 or 正在 before a verb to say it is happening right now: 爸爸在做饭。他正在看电视。
- 呢 at the end is optional and sounds lively: 他正在看书呢。 You can even use 呢 alone: 小明睡觉呢。
- Be careful: 在 + place means 'at' (我在家), and 在 + verb means 'in the middle of' (我在看书).
- For habits, use the plain verb: 我早上喝茶 (I drink tea in the morning). Putting 在 in front, 我在喝茶, changes the meaning to 'right now'.

Common mistakes taught:
- For a habit, use the plain verb: 我早上喝茶. 我在喝茶 would mean 'I am drinking tea right now'.

Examples:

- 爸爸在做饭，妈妈在看书。  Bàba zài zuòfàn, māma zài kàn shū.  — Dad is cooking and Mom is reading.
- 他正在看电视。  Tā zhèngzài kàn diànshì.  — He is watching TV right now.
- 哥哥正在看书呢。  Gēge zhèngzài kàn shū ne.  — My older brother is reading right now.
- 小明睡觉呢。  Xiǎomíng shuìjiào ne.  — Xiaoming is sleeping.

### 21. 了 after a verb: it's done

Pattern: Subject + Verb + *了 + Amount + Object

- 了 right after a verb says the action is finished: 我买了一本书。 (I bought a book.)
- It is not a past-tense marker. It marks 'completed'. Do not use it for habits (我早上喝茶, not 我早上喝了茶), although one particular morning is fine (我今天早上喝了茶), or for plain facts about a state (我昨天在家).
- A short sentence like 我买了书 can sound unfinished. Add an amount or a describing word: 我买了一本书, 妈妈喝了一杯茶. Or put 了 at the end: 我买书了。
- To say it did not happen, use 没(有) and drop 了: 我没买书。
- Ask with 吗: 你吃饭了吗？

Common mistakes taught:
- Do not add 了 to habits: 我早上喝茶 (not 我早上喝了茶). For one particular morning, 了 is fine: 我今天早上喝了茶.
- Do not keep 了 in the negative: 我没买书 (not 我没买了书).

Examples:

- 我买了一本书。  Wǒ mǎi le yì běn shū.  — I bought a book.
- 妈妈喝了一杯茶。  Māma hē le yì bēi chá.  — Mom drank a cup of tea.
- 我看见他了。  Wǒ kànjiàn tā le.  — I saw him.
- 我没买书。  Wǒ méi mǎi shū.  — I did not buy a book.
- 你吃饭了吗？  Nǐ chī fàn le ma?  — Have you eaten?

### 22. 了 at the end: something has changed

Pattern: Situation + 了

- 了 at the end of a sentence says 'now it is like this': a change or a new situation. 天冷了 means 'It has turned cold'. 下雨了 means 'It is raining now'. 我八岁了 means 'I am eight now'.
- A sentence like this does not need a subject: 下雨了！ 下课了！
- Compare 今天很冷 (a plain fact, it is cold today) with 天冷了 (news: it has turned cold).
- 太…了 is a separate pattern: 太好了！ (That is great!)

Examples:

- 天冷了。  Tiān lěng le.  — It has turned cold.
- 下雨了！  Xiàyǔ le!  — It is raining now!
- 现在八点了。  Xiànzài bā diǎn le.  — It is eight o'clock now.
- 他病了。  Tā bìng le.  — He has gotten sick.
- 下课了，我们去吃饭。  Xiàkè le, wǒmen qù chī fàn.  — Class is over, let's go and eat.

### 23. 和 and 对

Pattern: A + 和 + B

- 和 joins nouns and pronouns, like 'and': 我和爸爸, 老师和学生。 To link two whole sentences, use a comma instead.
- With 都, put 都 after the whole group: 我和他都是学生。
- 对 + person + verb means 'to / toward someone': 他对我很好。我对他说：'你好！'
- 对 also means 'right, correct': 对，我是学生。

Common mistakes taught:
- 都 comes after the group: 我和他都是学生, not 我都和他是学生.

Examples:

- 我和他是同学。  Wǒ hé tā shì tóngxué.  — He and I are classmates.
- 我和爸爸都喜欢看书。  Wǒ hé bàba dōu xǐhuan kàn shū.  — Dad and I both like reading.
- 他对我很好。  Tā duì wǒ hěn hǎo.  — He is very nice to me.
- 对，我是学生。  Duì, wǒ shì xuésheng.  — Yes, that's right, I am a student.

### 24. Two actions in a row, and giving

Pattern: Subject + Action 1 + Action 2

- Chinese lines up verbs in the order they happen: 我去商店买水果 means 'I go to the shop to buy fruit'. The second action is the purpose of the first.
- Sometimes the first action is how you do the second: 爸爸开车去公司 (Dad drives to work), 我坐火车回家 (I take the train home).
- Giving and asking can take two objects, the person first: 爸爸给小明一本书 (Dad gives Xiaoming a book). 我问老师一个问题 (I ask the teacher a question).
- 给 + person before a verb means 'for / to someone': 我给妈妈打电话 (I call my mom).

Common mistakes taught:
- Keep the order of events: 我去商店买水果, not 我买水果去商店.

Examples:

- 我去商店买水果。  Wǒ qù shāngdiàn mǎi shuǐguǒ.  — I go to the shop to buy fruit.
- 爸爸开车去公司。  Bàba kāichē qù gōngsī.  — Dad drives to work.
- 爸爸给小明一本书。  Bàba gěi Xiǎomíng yì běn shū.  — Dad gives Xiaoming a book.
- 我给妈妈打电话。  Wǒ gěi māma dǎ diànhuà.  — I call my mom.

### 25. 请, 一下 and 喂

Pattern: 请 + Verb

- 请 + verb means 'please': 请坐。请喝茶。 请问 means 'may I ask…?' and opens a question: 请问，学校在哪儿？
- Verb + 一下 means 'for a moment' or 'a little': 我问一下老师。我想休息一下。 It also makes a request softer.
- 喂 is how you answer or start a phone call: 喂，你好！ 喂，请问小明在家吗？
- Some verbs already include an object (睡觉, 上课, 说话). Put 了 after the whole word: 他们上课了。 When another action follows, 了 may move inside: 下了课，我们去吃饭。

Common mistakes taught:
- 请 comes before the verb: 请坐, not 坐请.

Examples:

- 请喝茶。  Qǐng hē chá.  — Please have some tea.
- 请问，学校在哪儿？  Qǐngwèn, xuéxiào zài nǎr?  — Excuse me, where is the school?
- 我想休息一下。  Wǒ xiǎng xiūxi yíxià.  — I want to rest for a bit.
- 喂，你好！  Wèi, nǐ hǎo!  — Hello! (answering the phone)

### 26. Some and a little: 一些, 一点儿, 有些, 有的, 有点儿

Pattern: 一些 / 一点儿 + Noun

- 一些 means 'some' or 'a few' and goes before a noun: 我想买一些水果。
- 一点儿 means 'a little' or 'a bit' of something: 我会说一点儿汉语。
- 有些 and 有的 mean 'some (of them)' and start a sentence about part of a group: 有些学生不喜欢读书。有的人喜欢喝茶，有的人喜欢喝牛奶。
- 有点儿 is different. It goes before an adjective and means 'a bit', often about something you do not like: 今天有点儿冷。

Common mistakes taught:
- 有点儿 goes before an adjective: 今天有点儿冷. Say 我会说一点儿汉语 for 'a little Chinese'.

Examples:

- 我想买一些水果。  Wǒ xiǎng mǎi yìxiē shuǐguǒ.  — I want to buy some fruit.
- 我会说一点儿汉语。  Wǒ huì shuō yìdiǎnr Hànyǔ.  — I can speak a little Chinese.
- 有些学生不喜欢读书。  Yǒuxiē xuésheng bù xǐhuan dúshū.  — Some students don't like studying.
- 今天有点儿冷。  Jīntiān yǒudiǎnr lěng.  — It is a bit cold today.

## 3. Speaking scripts

### Order a drink

- Speaker: 你好！你想喝什么？  — Hello! What would you like to drink?
- Learner: 我想喝茶。  — I would like some tea.
- Speaker: 好的。你想喝热茶吗？  — Okay. Would you like hot tea?
- Learner: 是的，我想喝热茶。  — Yes, I'd like hot tea.
- Speaker: 好的。你想吃东西吗？  — Okay. Would you like something to eat?
- Learner: 我不想吃东西，谢谢。  — I don't want anything to eat, thanks.
- Speaker: 好。十块钱。  — Okay. Ten yuan.
- Learner: 好的，谢谢！  — Okay, thank you!
- Speaker: 不客气！再见！  — You're welcome! Goodbye!

### Introduce yourself

- Speaker: 你好！你叫什么名字？  — Hello! What is your name?
- Learner: 你好，我叫小明。  — Hello, my name is Xiaoming.
- Speaker: 认识你，我很高兴。你在哪儿工作？  — I am very glad to meet you. Where do you work?
- Learner: 我在公司工作。你呢？  — I work at a company. And you?
- Speaker: 我是医生。你喜欢你的工作吗？  — I am a doctor. Do you like your work?
- Learner: 我很喜欢。我也喜欢学习汉语。  — I like it a lot. I also like studying Chinese.
- Speaker: 你的汉语很好！  — Your Chinese is very good!
- Learner: 谢谢！再见！  — Thank you! Goodbye!
- Speaker: 再见！  — Goodbye!

### Ask the price

- Speaker: 你好！你想买什么？  — Hello! What would you like to buy?
- Learner: 我想买一件衣服。  — I want to buy an item of clothing.
- Speaker: 这件很好看，一百块。  — This one looks very nice. It is 100 yuan.
- Learner: 太贵了。有便宜的吗？  — That is too expensive. Is there a cheaper one?
- Speaker: 这件八十块。  — This one is 80 yuan.
- Learner: 好，我要这件。  — Good, I'll take this one.
- Speaker: 好的。谢谢！  — Okay. Thank you!
- Learner: 谢谢，再见！  — Thank you, goodbye!
- Speaker: 再见！  — Goodbye!

### Ask where something is

- Speaker: 你好！你想去哪儿？  — Hello! Where would you like to go?
- Learner: 请问，医院在哪儿？  — Excuse me, where is the hospital?
- Speaker: 医院在学校那边。  — The hospital is over by the school.
- Learner: 我可以坐出租车去吗？  — Can I take a taxi there?
- Speaker: 可以。你坐出租车吧。  — Yes, you can. Take a taxi.
- Learner: 好，谢谢你！  — Okay, thank you!
- Speaker: 不客气！再见！  — You're welcome! Goodbye!
- Learner: 再见！  — Goodbye!
- Speaker: 再见！  — Goodbye!

### Talk about your family

- Speaker: 你好！你家有几口人？  — Hello! How many people are in your family?
- Learner: 我家有四口人。  — There are four people in my family.
- Speaker: 你有哥哥吗？  — Do you have an older brother?
- Learner: 有，我有一个哥哥。  — Yes, I have an older brother.
- Speaker: 你哥哥做什么工作？  — What does your older brother do?
- Learner: 他在医院工作，他是医生。  — He works at a hospital. He is a doctor.
- Speaker: 你们家人都很好吧？  — Your family are all well, right?
- Learner: 是的，我们都很好。  — Yes, we are all fine.
- Speaker: 太好了！再见！  — Great! Goodbye!

### Make a plan

- Speaker: 你好！你明天有时间吗？  — Hello! Do you have time tomorrow?
- Learner: 有，我明天下午有时间。  — Yes, I have time tomorrow afternoon.
- Speaker: 我们去看电影吧！  — Let's go and watch a movie!
- Learner: 好，几点去？  — Good, what time shall we go?
- Speaker: 下午三点，怎么样？  — How about three o'clock in the afternoon?
- Learner: 可以。在哪儿见？  — That works. Where shall we meet?
- Speaker: 在电影院见吧。  — Let's meet at the cinema.
- Learner: 好，明天见！  — Okay, see you tomorrow!
- Speaker: 明天见！  — See you tomorrow!

### Make a phone call

- Speaker: 喂，你好！你是谁？  — Hello! Who are you?
- Learner: 喂，我是小明。你妈妈在家吗？  — Hello, I am Xiaoming. Is your mom at home?
- Speaker: 她不在家，她在公司工作。  — She is not at home. She is at work.
- Learner: 她几点下班？  — What time does she finish work?
- Speaker: 她六点下班。  — She finishes work at six.
- Learner: 好，我六点再打电话。谢谢！  — Okay, I'll call again at six. Thank you!
- Speaker: 不客气！再见！  — You're welcome! Goodbye!
- Learner: 再见！  — Goodbye!
- Speaker: 再见！  — Goodbye!

### Talk about the weather

- Speaker: 你好！今天天气怎么样？  — Hello! How is the weather today?
- Learner: 今天很冷，也下雨了。  — It is very cold today, and it is raining too.
- Speaker: 对，太冷了！你想喝热茶吗？  — Yes, it is so cold! Would you like some hot tea?
- Learner: 好，我想喝热茶，谢谢。  — Yes, I'd like some hot tea, thank you.
- Speaker: 你喜欢冷的天气吗？  — Do you like cold weather?
- Learner: 我不太喜欢冷的天气，我喜欢热的天气。  — I don't like cold weather much. I like hot weather.
- Speaker: 我也是！明天天气很好。  — Me too! The weather will be very nice tomorrow.
- Learner: 太好了！明天见！  — Great! See you tomorrow!
- Speaker: 明天见！  — See you tomorrow!

## 4. Listening clips and what to listen for

1. 你好，我叫小明。  Nǐ hǎo, wǒ jiào Xiǎomíng.  — Hello, my name is Xiaoming.
   - 你好 is said ní hǎo: two third tones in a row, so the first one rises.
   - 叫 jiào falls sharply, then 小明 is said as one name.
2. 你想喝什么？  Nǐ xiǎng hē shénme?  — What do you want to drink?
   - Two third tones in a row: 你 nǐ sounds like ní before 想 xiǎng.
   - 什么 shénme is said quickly, and the 么 gets very light.
3. 今天天气很好。  Jīntiān tiānqì hěn hǎo.  — The weather is very nice today.
   - 很 hěn before 好 hǎo: two third tones, so 很 sounds like hén.
   - 今天 and 天气 are short and run together at normal speed.
4. 我想买一个杯子。  Wǒ xiǎng mǎi yí ge bēizi.  — I want to buy a cup.
   - 一 becomes yí before the fourth tone 个.
   - 个 is often said short and light, almost like ge.
5. 你在哪儿工作？  Nǐ zài nǎr gōngzuò?  — Where do you work?
   - 哪儿 nǎr is one beat: the 儿 merges into the syllable.
   - Listen for 在 zài as a quick falling tone between two longer words.
6. 现在几点？  Xiànzài jǐ diǎn?  — What time is it now?
   - 几点 jǐ diǎn: two third tones in a row, so 几 sounds like jí.
7. 我七点起床。  Wǒ qī diǎn qǐchuáng.  — I get up at seven.
   - Before a second-tone syllable, a third tone stays low and does not rise: 起 qǐ in 起床 qǐchuáng.
8. 明天你去学校吗？  Míngtiān nǐ qù xuéxiào ma?  — Are you going to school tomorrow?
   - 吗 is very light and short at the end of the question.
9. 我不是老师。  Wǒ bú shì lǎoshī.  — I am not a teacher.
   - 不 changes to bú before the fourth tone 是 shì: bú shì.
10. 这是我妈妈的手机。  Zhè shì wǒ māma de shǒujī.  — This is my mother's phone.
   - 的 is very light: de.
   - 妈妈 māma: the second 妈 is light and short.
11. 谢谢你！  Xièxie nǐ!  — Thank you!
   - 谢谢 xièxie: the second 谢 is light and short.
12. 对不起，我不知道。  Duìbuqǐ, wǒ bù zhīdào.  — Sorry, I don't know.
   - 对不起 duìbuqǐ: 不 is light here.
   - In 不知道, 不 is bù before the first tone 知 zhī.
13. 我们去吃饭吧。  Wǒmen qù chī fàn ba.  — Let's go and eat.
   - 吧 is light at the end: ba.
14. 你有时间吗？  Nǐ yǒu shíjiān ma?  — Do you have time?
   - 你 nǐ and 有 yǒu are both third tones, so 你 sounds like ní.
15. 我喜欢喝茶。  Wǒ xǐhuan hē chá.  — I like drinking tea.
   - 我 wǒ before 喜 xǐ: two third tones, so 我 sounds like wó.
   - 喜欢 xǐhuan: the 欢 is light.
16. 她在学校学习汉语。  Tā zài xuéxiào xuéxí Hànyǔ.  — She studies Chinese at school.
   - Listen for 学校 xuéxiào versus 学习 xuéxí: the first syllable is the same, the second is different.
17. 一个苹果三块钱。  Yí ge píngguǒ sān kuài qián.  — One apple costs three yuan.
   - 一 becomes yí before the fourth tone 个.
   - 三块钱 sān kuài qián: the numbers run together at normal speed.
18. 我今年二十岁。  Wǒ jīnnián èrshí suì.  — I am 20 years old this year.
   - 二十 èrshí is said as one word, so 十 is short.
19. 请问，学校在哪儿？  Qǐngwèn, xuéxiào zài nǎr?  — Excuse me, where is the school?
   - 请问 qǐngwèn: the third tone 请 stays low before the fourth tone 问.
20. 我买了一本书。  Wǒ mǎi le yì běn shū.  — I bought a book.
   - 了 is light and quick: le.
   - 一 becomes yì before the third tone 本.
21. 你吃饭了吗？  Nǐ chī fàn le ma?  — Have you eaten?
   - 了 and 吗 are both light: le ma.
22. 天冷了。  Tiān lěng le.  — It has turned cold.
   - 冷 lěng is a third tone, and 了 is light and short after it.
23. 我们在饭店吃饭。  Wǒmen zài fàndiàn chī fàn.  — We eat at a restaurant.
   - 饭 fàn appears twice (饭店, 吃饭). The tone is the same: falling.
24. 你的汉语很好。  Nǐ de Hànyǔ hěn hǎo.  — Your Chinese is very good.
   - 很 hěn before 好 hǎo: two third tones, so 很 sounds like hén.

## 5. Word webs

### Shared-character groups (the small glosses under each word)

**学** (xué, to study, to learn)

- 学习 = 学 (study) + 习 (practice)
- 学生 = 学 (study) + 生 (student)
- 学校 = 学 (study) + 校 (school)
- 同学 = 同 (same) + 学 (study)
- 大学 = 大 (big) + 学 (study)
- 中学 = 中 (middle) + 学 (study)

### Opposite pairs and their notes

- 大 / 小: Both work with 很: 很大, 很小.
- 多 / 少: Put them together and you get 多少 (duōshao), 'how many' or 'how much'.
- 冷 / 热: Both describe weather and drinks: 很冷, 热茶.
- 早上 / 晚上: Same 上 ending. 早 (early) and 晚 (late) are what change.
- 上午 / 下午: Same 午 (noon). 上 (before) and 下 (after) give before and after noon.
- 来 / 去: 来 comes toward the speaker, 去 goes away from them.
- 买 / 卖: Same sound shape, different tones: 买 mǎi (buy, third tone) and 卖 mài (sell, fourth tone).
- 上 / 下: 上 is up or on, 下 is down or under. They also start and end things: 上班 and 下班, 上课 and 下课.
- 前 / 后: Front and back. Add 边 after the noun: 学校前边, 学校后边.
- 里 / 外: Inside and outside. 里 goes right after the noun, 外 takes 边: 房间里, 房间外边.
- 男 / 女: They build words too: 男朋友 and 女朋友, 女儿, 女士.
- 早 / 晚: Early and late. They are also in 早上 / 晚上 and 早饭 / 晚饭.
- 上班 / 下班: 上 starts it, 下 finishes it.
- 上课 / 下课: 上 starts it, 下 finishes it.
- 有 / 没有: 没 is how you say 'not' with 有. Never 不有.
- 贵 / 便宜: Expensive and cheap. They do not share a character, so learn them as a pair.
- 昨天 / 明天: Same 天 (day). 昨 is the past and 明 is next.
- 去年 / 明年: Same 年 (year). 去 is the past and 明 is next.
- 哥哥 / 弟弟: Older brother and younger brother. 哥哥 and 弟弟 both repeat a character.
- 姐姐 / 妹妹: Older sister and younger sister.
- 儿子 / 女儿: Son and daughter. Both contain 儿 (child).
- 这里 / 那里: Here and there. 这 is near and 那 is far.
- 这边 / 那边: This side and that side. 这 is near and 那 is far.

### Topic sentences

- Greetings and thanks: 你好！谢谢你，再见！  Nǐ hǎo! Xièxie nǐ, zàijiàn!  — Hello! Thank you, goodbye!
- Family and people: 我家有四口人，爸爸、妈妈、哥哥和我。  Wǒ jiā yǒu sì kǒu rén, bàba, māma, gēge hé wǒ.  — There are four people in my family: Dad, Mom, my older brother and me.
- Who: pronouns: 我们都是学生，他们是老师。  Wǒmen dōu shì xuésheng, tāmen shì lǎoshī.  — We are all students, and they are teachers.
- This, that, which, where: 这是我的书，那是你的书。  Zhè shì wǒ de shū, nà shì nǐ de shū.  — This is my book, and that is your book.
- Numbers: 这个手机三千零五十块。  Zhège shǒujī sān qiān líng wǔshí kuài.  — This phone is 3,050 yuan.
- Amounts and measure words: 我想买一些水果。多少钱？  Wǒ xiǎng mǎi yìxiē shuǐguǒ. Duōshǎo qián?  — I want to buy some fruit. How much is it?
- Time of day: 我早上七点起床，晚上十点睡觉。  Wǒ zǎoshang qī diǎn qǐchuáng, wǎnshang shí diǎn shuìjiào.  — I get up at seven in the morning and go to bed at ten in the evening.
- Days and dates: 今天是星期天，我在家。  Jīntiān shì xīngqītiān, wǒ zài jiā.  — Today is Sunday, and I am at home.
- At school: 学生在学校学习汉语。  Xuésheng zài xuéxiào xuéxí Hànyǔ.  — The students study Chinese at school.
- Work and places: 我爸爸在公司工作，我妈妈在医院工作。  Wǒ bàba zài gōngsī gōngzuò, wǒ māma zài yīyuàn gōngzuò.  — My dad works at a company and my mom works at a hospital.
- Food and drink: 我想喝水，不想吃米饭。  Wǒ xiǎng hē shuǐ, bù xiǎng chī mǐfàn.  — I want to drink water, and I don't want to eat rice.
- Shopping and money: 这件衣服很贵，那件很便宜。  Zhè jiàn yīfu hěn guì, nà jiàn hěn piányi.  — This item of clothing is expensive, and that one is cheap.
- At home: 我的手机在桌子上，电脑在房间里。  Wǒ de shǒujī zài zhuōzi shàng, diànnǎo zài fángjiān lǐ.  — My phone is on the table and my computer is in the room.
- Getting around: 我坐出租车去学校，他坐火车回家。  Wǒ zuò chūzūchē qù xuéxiào, tā zuò huǒchē huí jiā.  — I take a taxi to school and he takes the train home.
- Weather: 今天下雨，天气很冷。  Jīntiān xiàyǔ, tiānqì hěn lěng.  — It is raining today and the weather is cold.
- Fun and feelings: 我喜欢看电影，也喜欢听歌。  Wǒ xǐhuan kàn diànyǐng, yě xǐhuan tīng gē.  — I like watching movies, and I like listening to songs too.
- Health and daily routine: 我弟弟生病了，他想休息一下。  Wǒ dìdi shēngbìng le, tā xiǎng xiūxi yíxià.  — My younger brother is sick, and he wants to rest for a bit.
- Describing things: 这个手机很新，非常好看。  Zhège shǒujī hěn xīn, fēicháng hǎokàn.  — This phone is very new and really nice-looking.
- Everyday verbs: 我觉得这个菜很好吃，你觉得呢？  Wǒ juéde zhège cài hěn hǎochī, nǐ juéde ne?  — I think this dish is very tasty. What do you think?
- Little words that build sentences: 我也想去，你呢？  Wǒ yě xiǎng qù, nǐ ne?  — I want to go too. What about you?
