### Pairs and opposites
- 东 / 西: 东 is east and 西 is west. Put them together and you get 东西 (dōngxi), which means "things".
- 南 / 北: 南 is south and 北 is north. 南方 and 北方 are the south and the north of a country.
- 东方 / 西方: The East and the West. Both end in 方 (side).
- 南方 / 北方: The south and the north of a country. 中国的北方很冷，南方很热。
- 东北 / 西南: Northeast and southwest. 东 swaps with 西 and 北 swaps with 南.
- 东南 / 西北: Southeast and northwest, the other pair of corners.
- 胖 / 瘦: Fat and thin. Be careful: saying 你很胖 to a person is rude.
- 饿 / 饱: Hungry and full: 我饿了, 我饱了.
- 容易 / 难: Easy and hard. Both go with 很: 很容易, 很难.
- 开心 / 难过: Happy and sad. Both tell how you feel: 我很开心, 我很难过.
- 干净 / 脏: Clean and dirty: 房间很干净, 手很脏.
- 老 / 年轻: Old and young, for people: 老人 and 年轻人.
- 开机 / 关机: 开 turns it on and 关 turns it off. Both end in 机 (machine).
- 住院 / 出院: Go into hospital and leave hospital. 院 stays the same. 住 is to stay and 出 is to go out.
- 以前 / 以后: Before and after. Both start with 以.
- 以上 / 以下: Above (or more than) and below (or less than). Both start with 以.
- 前天 / 后天: The day before yesterday and the day after tomorrow. 前 is before and 后 is after, both with 天 (day).
- 前年 / 后年: The year before last and the year after next. 前 is before and 后 is after, both with 年 (year).
- 夏天 / 冬天: Summer is hot and winter is cold. Both end in 天.
- 春天 / 秋天: Spring and autumn, the two mild seasons.
- 男人 / 女人: Man and woman. Both end in 人 (person).
- 男生 / 女生: Boy and girl students. Both end in 生 (student).
- 发 / 收: 发 sends and 收 receives: 发邮件, 收邮件.
- 得到 / 丢: To get something and to lose it.
- 记 / 忘记: To remember and to forget. 忘记 has 记 inside it, and 忘 means to lose from the mind.
- 担心 / 放心: To worry and to set your mind at rest. Both end in 心 (heart): 担 carries the worry and 放 puts it down.
- 一样 / 不同: The same and different. 不 turns 同 (same) into 不同 (not the same).
- 好久 / 不久: 好久 is a long time and 不久 is not long. You also hear 好久不见 when you meet an old friend.
- 阿姨 / 叔叔: 阿姨 is a woman about your mother's age and 叔叔 is a man about your father's age.
