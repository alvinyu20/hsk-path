你好！我正在做一个 HSK 3 学习网站（学习者已学过 HSK 1 和 HSK 2 的约 500 个词，现在学 HSK 3 的 491 个新词，总共约 1000 个词）。请你以中国母语者和汉语老师的身份，检查下面的练习对话、常用句和听力短文。
**只报告有问题的地方**，不要夸奖，也不要重复没问题的内容。对每个问题，请写：编号 / 原文 / 哪里不好（简单中文，再加一句英文）/ 更自然的说法（只用 HSK 1、HSK 2 和 HSK 3 的词）。
需要特别注意：1) 不自然或不地道的说法；2) 语法错误；3) 英文翻译和中文意思不一致；4) 书面语被当成口语教；5) 对 HSK 3 来说太难的词；6) 多音字的拼音是否正确。
如果某一部分全部没问题，请回答“没有发现问题”。

Hello! I am building an HSK 3 study site (learners know the ~500 words of HSK 1 and 2 and are now learning the 491 new HSK 3 words, about 1,000 in all). Please act as a native speaker and Chinese teacher and check the items below.
**Report only the problems**, no praise and no repeating good items. For each problem give: number / the item / what is wrong (simple Chinese plus one line of English) / a more natural version (using only HSK 1, 2 and 3 words).
Please watch for: 1) unnatural or non-idiomatic wording; 2) grammar mistakes; 3) English that does not match the Chinese; 4) written style taught as spoken; 5) words too hard for HSK 3; 6) wrong pinyin for words with more than one reading.
If a part has no problems, answer "No problems found".

---

Also check: do the practice conversations sound like something people really say (cafe, hotel, doctor, directions ...)? Would a native speaker reply in these words? Are the "what to listen for" notes true?

## Practice scripts (8 conversations the learner reads aloud)
### Check in at a hotel (Hotel, 5 turns)
- 对方：你好！欢迎来到我们宾馆。你想住几天？  /  Hello! Welcome to our hotel. How many days would you like to stay?
- 学习者：我想住三天。  /  I would like to stay three days.
- 对方：好的。请把你的护照给我。  /  Okay. Please give me your passport.
- 学习者：好的，给你。  /  Okay, here you are.
- 对方：谢谢。你的房间在八楼，坐电梯就可以上去。  /  Thank you. Your room is on the eighth floor; you can go up by the elevator.
- 学习者：好的，早饭在几楼？  /  Okay, which floor is breakfast on?
- 对方：在一楼，早上六点半开始。  /  On the first floor. It starts at half past six in the morning.
- 学习者：好的，谢谢你。  /  Okay, thank you.
- 对方：不客气！有问题可以找我。  /  You are welcome! You can come to me if you have any problem.
### See a doctor (Health, 5 turns)
- 对方：你好，你哪里不舒服？  /  Hello, where do you feel unwell?
- 学习者：我头疼，还有点发烧。  /  I have a headache and a bit of a fever.
- 对方：从什么时候开始的？  /  Since when?
- 学习者：从昨天晚上开始的。  /  Since yesterday evening.
- 对方：你可能感冒了。你要多喝水，多休息。  /  You may have a cold. You need to drink more water and rest more.
- 学习者：我需要吃药吗？  /  Do I need to take medicine?
- 对方：要，这个药一天吃三次，吃完饭再吃。  /  Yes. Take this medicine three times a day, after you finish eating.
- 学习者：好的，谢谢医生。  /  Okay, thank you, doctor.
- 对方：不客气。如果明天还发烧，你再来。  /  You are welcome. If you still have a fever tomorrow, come again.
### Plan the weekend (Plans, 5 turns)
- 对方：这个周末你有什么打算？  /  What are your plans for this weekend?
- 学习者：我还没有打算，你呢？  /  I have no plan yet. What about you?
- 对方：天气很好，我们一起去爬山吧。  /  The weather is nice; let us go climbing together.
- 学习者：好啊！我们几点出发？  /  Sure! What time do we set out?
- 对方：早上七点吧。我们在地铁站见面。  /  Seven in the morning. We meet at the subway station.
- 学习者：太早了，八点可以吗？  /  That is too early; is eight okay?
- 对方：可以。那你带点吃的和喝的，好吗？  /  Okay. Then will you bring something to eat and drink?
- 学习者：好的，我带面包和水。  /  Okay, I will bring bread and water.
- 对方：太好了，星期天见！  /  Great, see you on Sunday!
### Move to a new home (Housing, 5 turns)
- 对方：我听说你要搬家，是真的吗？  /  I heard you will move. Is it true?
- 学习者：是的，我下个月搬到新小区。  /  Yes, next month I am moving to a new neighbourhood.
- 对方：新家在哪里？附近方便吗？  /  Where is the new home? Is it handy around there?
- 学习者：很方便，附近有地铁站和银行。  /  Very handy; there is a subway station and a bank nearby.
- 对方：太好了！你什么时候搬？  /  Great! When are you moving?
- 学习者：下个月五号，你可以来帮我吗？  /  On the fifth of next month. Can you come and help me?
- 对方：当然可以！我需要带东西吗？  /  Of course you can! Do I need to bring anything?
- 学习者：不用，谢谢你，你来就行。  /  No need, thank you. Just come.
- 对方：好的，到时候见！  /  Okay, see you then!
### Take the high-speed train (Travel, 5 turns)
- 对方：你好！需要帮助吗？  /  Hello! Do you need help?
- 学习者：请问，高铁几点出发？  /  Excuse me, what time does the high-speed train leave?
- 对方：十点二十。可是今天晚点了，要等半个小时。  /  Twenty past ten. But today it is late, so you have to wait half an hour.
- 学习者：好的，我的行李可以带上去吗？  /  Okay. Can I take my luggage up with me?
- 对方：可以。你先把票给我看看。  /  Yes, you can. First show me your ticket.
- 学习者：好的，给你。我在哪里检票？  /  Okay, here you are. Where do I have my ticket checked?
- 对方：在那边，然后坐电梯上去。  /  Over there, and then go up by the elevator.
- 学习者：谢谢你。  /  Thank you.
- 对方：不客气，再见！  /  You are welcome, goodbye!
### Talk about school (Study, 5 turns)
- 对方：这次考试你的成绩怎么样？  /  How is your result in this exam?
- 学习者：不太好，我的数学很差。  /  Not so good; my maths is poor.
- 对方：没关系。你每天都做作业吗？  /  Never mind. Do you do your homework every day?
- 学习者：做，可是我常常忘记复习。  /  I do, but I often forget to review.
- 对方：那你要每天复习一点，不要太着急。  /  Then you should review a little every day and not worry too much.
- 学习者：好的，我会努力的。你有什么好办法？  /  Okay, I will work hard. What good ideas do you have?
- 对方：多练习，也可以问老师。  /  Practise more, and you can ask the teacher too.
- 学习者：好，我明天就去问老师。  /  Okay, I will go and ask the teacher tomorrow.
- 对方：很好，我相信你的成绩一定可以提高！  /  Good. I am sure your grades can improve!
### Change a shirt (Shopping, 5 turns)
- 对方：你好！你需要帮助吗？  /  Hello! Do you need help?
- 学习者：我想换这件衬衫。  /  I would like to change this shirt.
- 对方：好的。这件有问题吗？  /  Okay. Is there a problem with this one?
- 学习者：大小不合适，有点小。  /  The size is not right; it is a bit small.
- 对方：那你要大一点的吗？  /  Then do you want a bigger one?
- 学习者：要，还有蓝的吗？  /  Yes. Do you have a blue one?
- 对方：有。你可以试一下。  /  Yes. You can try it on.
- 学习者：好的，这件很合适，我买这件。  /  Okay, this one fits well. I will buy this one.
- 对方：好的，谢谢你！  /  Okay, thank you!
### Call a friend abroad (Friends, 5 turns)
- 对方：好久不见！你在国外生活得怎么样？  /  Long time no see! How is your life abroad?
- 学习者：很好，可是我有点想家。  /  Very well, but I miss home a bit.
- 对方：你习惯那里的天气了吗？  /  Have you got used to the weather there?
- 学习者：还没有，那里的冬天很冷。  /  Not yet; the winter there is very cold.
- 对方：你要注意身体，多穿一点。  /  Take care of yourself and wear a bit more.
- 学习者：好的。你最近怎么样？  /  Okay. How have you been lately?
- 对方：我很忙，可是很开心。我们周末再聊天吧。  /  I am busy but happy. Let us chat again at the weekend.
- 学习者：好啊，周末见！  /  Sure, see you at the weekend!
- 对方：好，再见！  /  Okay, goodbye!

## Useful phrases
- 请再说一遍。  /  Please say it once more.
- 我没听清楚，请再说一遍。  /  I did not hear it clearly; please say it once more.
- 你可以说得慢一点吗？  /  Can you speak a little slower?
- 这个句子是什么意思？  /  What does this sentence mean?
- 我不太明白，你可以再讲一遍吗？  /  I do not quite understand; can you explain it again?
- 我想查一下这个词。  /  I want to look this word up.
- 你能帮我吗？  /  Can you help me?
- 对不起，我忘记了。  /  Sorry, I forgot.

## Listening clips: the passage, the English and the "what to listen for" notes
- 我昨天在路口遇到了一个老同事。  /  I ran into an old colleague at the crossing yesterday.
    note: 遇到 yùdào: both syllables fall.
    note: 老同事 lǎo tóngshì: 老 is a third tone before a rising tone, so it only dips.
- 请问，我怎么去图书馆？  /  Excuse me, how do I get to the library?
    note: 图书馆 túshūguǎn: a rise, a high tone, then a dip.
    note: 怎么 zěnme: 么 is light and short. 怎么去 asks how to get somewhere.
- 刚才下雨了，你带伞了吗？  /  It just rained; did you bring an umbrella?
    note: 刚才 gāngcái means "a moment ago": high and flat, then rising.
    note: 带伞 dài sǎn: a fall, then a dip. The last two sounds, 了吗, are both light.
- 我的手机没电了，只能关机。  /  My phone has no battery left now, so I can only turn it off.
    note: 没电 méi diàn: a rise, then a fall.
    note: 只能 zhǐnéng: 只 is a third tone before a rising tone, so it only dips.
- 比赛结束以后，我们一起去吃饭吧。  /  After the match ends, let us go and eat together.
    note: 结束 jiéshù: a rise, then a fall.
    note: 以后 yǐhòu: 以 is low and short before the fourth tone 后. 比赛结束以后 means "after the match ends".
- 这个问题很难，我想了半天才明白。  /  This question is hard; I thought for a long time before I understood.
    note: 半天 bàntiān: a fall, then high and flat. Here it means a long time, not half a day.
    note: 才 cái sits before the verb and tells you it took longer than expected: 才明白.
- 我的自行车被朋友借走了，我只能坐地铁。  /  My bike was borrowed by a friend, so I can only take the subway.
    note: 被 bèi comes before the person who did it: 被朋友借走.
    note: 借 jiè (to borrow) falls; 街 jiē (street) is high and flat.
- 他一边走路，一边听音乐。  /  He listens to music while he walks.
    note: 一边 yìbiān: 一 is yì before the high tone 边. You hear it twice: 一边…一边….
    note: 音乐 yīnyuè: high and flat, then falling.
- 我对中国文化很感兴趣。  /  I am very interested in Chinese culture.
    note: 感兴趣 gǎn xìngqù: 兴 is xìng (fourth tone) here, not xīng.
    note: 对 comes first and 感兴趣 later: 对…感兴趣 means to be interested in ….
- 老师让我们先复习，然后再做作业。  /  The teacher has us review first and then do the homework.
    note: 复习 fùxí: a fall, then a rise.
    note: 先…然后…: when you hear 先 (first), listen for 然后 (then) later.
- 今天比昨天凉快多了。  /  Today is much cooler than yesterday.
    note: 凉快 liángkuài: a rise, then a fall. 快 kuài (fast) is the same sound.
    note: 多了 duō le after an adjective means "much more": 凉快多了.
- 他长得又高又瘦，很像他爸爸。  /  He is tall and thin and looks a lot like his dad.
    note: 长 zhǎng (to grow) is a third tone; 长 cháng (long) rises. Here it is zhǎng.
    note: 像 xiàng (to look like) and 向 xiàng (towards) sound the same.
- 他决定明年去国外留学。  /  He has decided to study abroad next year.
    note: 决定 juédìng: a rise, then a fall.
    note: 留学 liúxué: both syllables rise.
- 我妈妈担心我晚上骑车不安全。  /  My mother worries that riding at night is not safe for me.
    note: 担心 dānxīn: both syllables are high and flat.
    note: 不安全 bù ānquán: 不 stays bù before the high tone 安; it changes only before a fourth tone.
- 请把你的护照给我看看。  /  Please show me your passport.
    note: 护照 hùzhào: both syllables fall.
    note: 把 bǎ puts the object first: 把护照给我看看. Listen for 把 and then the verb at the end.
- 他刚到家，就接到了朋友的电话。  /  He had only just got home when he got a call from a friend.
    note: 刚…就…: 刚 (just) and 就 (then) come as a pair.
    note: 接到 jiēdào: high and flat, then falling.
- 学校附近新开了一家银行。  /  A new bank has opened near the school.
    note: 附近 fùjìn: both syllables fall.
    note: 银行 yínháng: here 行 is háng (a business), not xíng.
- 春天来了，公园里的花都开了。  /  Spring has come, and the flowers in the park have all opened.
    note: 春天 chūntiān: both syllables are high and flat.
    note: 公园 gōngyuán: a high tone, then a rise. 里 is light in 公园里.
- 我一直在等你，你怎么现在才来？  /  I have been waiting for you all along; why do you only come now?
    note: 一直 yìzhí: 一 is yì before the rising tone 直.
    note: 才 cái here shows it is later than expected.
- 听说下个月学校要开运动会。  /  It is said that the school will hold a sports meeting next month.
    note: 听说 tīngshuō: both syllables are high and flat.
    note: 运动会 yùndònghuì: all three syllables fall.
- 你应该多锻炼，身体才会健康。  /  You should exercise more; only then will you be healthy.
    note: 应该 yīnggāi: both syllables are high and flat.
    note: 锻炼 duànliàn: both syllables fall.
- 如果你不舒服，就请假回家休息吧。  /  If you do not feel well, ask for leave and go home to rest.
    note: 如果 rúguǒ starts the "if" part and 就 starts the "then" part. Listen for the pair.
    note: 请假 qǐngjià: a dip, then a fall. 请假回家 is a chain of actions: ask for leave, go home.
- 雨越下越大，我们别出去了。  /  The rain is getting heavier and heavier, so let us not go out.
    note: 越 yuè is said twice, and both fall: 越下越大 means "the more it rains, the heavier".
    note: 别出去了 bié chūqu le: 别 rises, and the last two syllables are light.
- 对不起，我迟到了，路上车太多了。  /  Sorry, I am late; there were too many cars on the road.
    note: 迟到 chídào: a rise, then a fall.
    note: 对不起 duìbuqǐ: the 不 in the middle is light and short.
