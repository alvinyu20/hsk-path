## Practice scripts (8 conversations the learner reads aloud)
### Return a faulty product (Shopping, 5 turns)
- 对方：你好，请问我可以帮你做什么？  /  Hello, what can I do to help you?
- 学习者：你好，我上周在这里买了一部手机，可是它坏了。  /  Hello, I bought a phone here last week, but it is broken.
- 对方：真对不起。具体是什么问题？  /  Very sorry about that. What exactly is the problem?
- 学习者：它昨天突然不能开机了，我什么办法都试过了。  /  It suddenly could not be turned on yesterday, and I have tried every method.
- 对方：好的。你还留着买东西时的小票吗？  /  Okay. Have you kept the receipt from when you bought it?
- 学习者：我留着，小票在这里。  /  I have, and the receipt is here.
- 对方：我们可以给你换一个新的，也可以把钱退给你。你想怎么办？  /  We can give you a new one, or we can give you your money back. What would you like?
- 学习者：我想退货，因为这部手机的质量不太好。  /  I want to return it, because the quality of this phone is not very good.
- 对方：好的，请你在这里签一下名，钱很快就会退给你。  /  Okay, please sign here, and the money will be returned to you soon.
### See a doctor (Health, 5 turns)
- 对方：你好，请坐。你哪里不舒服？  /  Hello, please sit down. Where do you feel unwell?
- 学习者：医生，我一直咳嗽，晚上睡不好。  /  Doctor, I keep coughing, and I do not sleep well at night.
- 对方：咳嗽几天了？有没有发烧？  /  How many days have you been coughing? Have you had a fever?
- 学习者：已经三天了，我昨天晚上有点发烧。  /  It has been three days, and last night I had a bit of a fever.
- 对方：我先给你检查一下。不太严重，可能是感冒了。你对药过敏吗？  /  Let me examine you first. It is not very serious; it may be a cold. Are you allergic to any medicine?
- 学习者：我对药不过敏。需要打针吗？  /  I am not allergic to medicine. Do I need an injection?
- 对方：不用打针，我给你开三天的药，每天吃三次。  /  You do not need an injection. I will give you three days of medicine, to be taken three times a day.
- 学习者：好的，谢谢医生。我回家以后应该注意什么？  /  Okay, thank you, doctor. What should I pay attention to when I get home?
- 对方：要多休息，多喝水。三天以后再来检查一下。  /  You must rest more and drink more water. Come back for a check in three days.
### Open a bank account (Money, 5 turns)
- 对方：你好，请问你要办理什么业务？  /  Hello, what business would you like to do?
- 学习者：你好，我想开一个新的银行账户。  /  Hello, I want to open a new bank account.
- 对方：好的。请把你的身份证给我，再填一下这张表格。  /  Okay. Please give me your ID card and fill in this form.
- 学习者：好的。这张表格我应该怎么填？  /  Okay. How should I fill in this form?
- 对方：这里写名字和电话。写好以后，请你想一个六位数的密码。  /  Write your name and phone number here. After that, please think of a six-digit password.
- 学习者：好了，我想好密码了。现在可以输入吗？  /  Done, I have thought of a password. Can I enter it now?
- 对方：可以，手续已经办好了。你的银行卡三天以后就能拿到。  /  Yes. The procedure has already been done. You can get your bank card in three days.
- 学习者：请问，我每个月要付手续费吗？  /  Excuse me, do I have to pay a service charge every month?
- 对方：不用，存款超过一百块就不收费。  /  No. If your deposit is over a hundred yuan, there is no charge.
### Share an idea at a meeting (Meetings, 5 turns)
- 对方：大家好，今天我们来讨论一下新产品的宣传方案。谁先说？  /  Hello, everyone. Today we will discuss the publicity plan for the new product. Who will speak first?
- 学习者：我先说。我觉得我们可以在网上做宣传，因为现在很多人都在网上买东西。  /  I will go first. I think we can advertise online, because many people shop online now.
- 对方：这个想法不错。你有具体的方案吗？  /  That is a good idea. Do you have a concrete plan?
- 学习者：有。我们可以请一些有名的人介绍产品，这样更容易吸引顾客。  /  Yes. We can ask some famous people to introduce the product, which makes it easier to attract customers.
- 对方：不过这样会花很多钱。你有没有考虑过成本？  /  But this will cost a lot of money. Have you thought about the cost?
- 学习者：我考虑过了。我们可以先少做一点，如果效果好，再多做一些。  /  I have. We can do a little first, and if the results are good, do more.
- 对方：好，我同意先试一下。其他人还有什么意见？  /  Good, I agree to try it first. What other opinions does anyone have?
- 学习者：我也同意。不过我们要定一个时间表，这样才能按时完成。  /  I agree too. But we need to set a schedule, so that we can finish on time.
- 对方：好，那就这样决定。谢谢大家。  /  Good, let us decide it that way. Thank you, everyone.
### Report a problem in your flat (Housing, 5 turns)
- 对方：你好，这里是物业，请问有什么事？  /  Hello, this is the property office. What is the matter?
- 学习者：你好，我住在三号楼。我家的空调坏了，一点也不冷。  /  Hello, I live in building three. The air conditioner at my home is broken and does not cool at all.
- 对方：好的。请问是什么时候开始坏的？  /  Okay. When did it start to go wrong?
- 学习者：昨天晚上就不行了，现在家里热得我睡不好。  /  It stopped working last night, and now it is so hot at home that I cannot sleep well.
- 对方：真不好意思。我们马上安排师傅去看一下。你什么时候在家？  /  I am really sorry. We will arrange for a repairman to take a look right away. When are you at home?
- 学习者：我下午三点以后都在家，请师傅那个时候来吧。  /  I am at home after three in the afternoon, so please ask the repairman to come then.
- 对方：好的，师傅四点左右到。如果要换新的，可能需要你付一点钱，可以吗？  /  Okay, the repairman will arrive at about four. If a new part is needed, you may have to pay a little. Is that all right?
- 学习者：可以。请问师傅来之前，会先给我打电话吗？  /  That is fine. Will the repairman call me first, before coming?
- 对方：会的，他到楼下的时候会给你打电话。  /  Yes, he will call you when he gets downstairs.
### Ask a tour guide (Travel, 5 turns)
- 对方：大家好，我是你们的导游。今天我们会去三个景点，请大家跟着我。  /  Hello, everyone. I am your tour guide. Today we will visit three sights, so please follow me.
- 学习者：导游，请问今天的路线是怎么样的？  /  Guide, excuse me, what is today's route like?
- 对方：上午我们参观古城，中午吃饭，下午爬山。  /  In the morning we visit the old town, at noon we eat, and in the afternoon we climb the mountain.
- 学习者：旅行费用包括门票吗？  /  Does the cost of the trip include the tickets?
- 对方：古城的门票包括在内，山上的要自己买，一个人五十块。  /  The ticket for the old town is included, but you must buy the one for the mountain yourselves, fifty yuan each.
- 学习者：好的。下午我们几点在哪里集合？  /  Okay. At what time and where do we meet in the afternoon?
- 对方：下午两点，我们在山下的停车场集合。请大家一定要准时。  /  At two in the afternoon, we meet at the car park at the foot of the mountain. Everyone must be on time, please.
- 学习者：如果我不小心迷路了，应该怎么联系你？  /  If I get lost by accident, how should I contact you?
- 对方：请记下我的电话。有问题就给我打电话，我会马上去找你。  /  Please note down my phone number. If there is a problem, call me and I will come and find you at once.
### Sign up as a volunteer (Community, 5 turns)
- 对方：你好，欢迎来到社区服务中心。请问你有什么事？  /  Hello, welcome to the community service centre. What brings you here?
- 学习者：你好，我想报名当志愿者。  /  Hello, I would like to sign up as a volunteer.
- 对方：太好了！你对哪种活动比较有兴趣？我们有照顾老人、教孩子学习，还有整理大家捐来的旧书。  /  Great! Which kind of activity are you more interested in? We have looking after old people, teaching children, and sorting the old books people have donated.
- 学习者：我对教孩子学习比较有兴趣，因为我会说两种语言。  /  I am more interested in teaching children, because I can speak two languages.
- 对方：那太合适了。你每个星期有多少时间？  /  That is just right. How much time do you have each week?
- 学习者：我每个星期六上午都有空，可以吗？  /  I am free every Saturday morning. Is that all right?
- 对方：可以。这个活动是免费的，你只需要填一下这张报名表。  /  That is fine. The activity is free, and you only need to fill in this sign-up form.
- 学习者：好的。请问我还需要带什么东西？  /  Okay. Excuse me, what else do I need to bring?
- 对方：不用，你自己来就行。下个星期六见！  /  No need, just come yourself. See you next Saturday!
### Sign a work contract (Contracts, 5 turns)
- 对方：你好，欢迎加入我们公司。这是你的工作合同，请你先看一下。  /  Hello, welcome to our company. This is your work contract, so please take a look at it first.
- 学习者：谢谢。请问，试用期是多长时间？  /  Thank you. Excuse me, how long is the trial period?
- 对方：试用期是三个月。试用期的工资是正式工资的一半。  /  The trial period is three months. The pay during the trial period is half of the full pay.
- 学习者：好的。那公司有保险吗？  /  Okay. And does the company provide insurance?
- 对方：有。公司会给你买保险，你不用自己付钱。  /  Yes. The company will buy insurance for you, and you do not have to pay yourself.
- 学习者：明白了。请问我平时需要加班吗？加班的话，公司会多给钱吗？  /  I see. Do I need to work overtime normally? If I do, will the company pay more?
- 对方：有时候需要加班，不过加班的话，我们会多付工资。  /  Sometimes you will need to work overtime, but if you do, we will pay you more.
- 学习者：好的，我没有问题了。我现在可以签名吗？  /  Okay, I have no more questions. Can I sign now?
- 对方：可以，请在最后一页签上你的名字，再写上今天的日期。  /  You may. Please sign your name on the last page and write today's date.

## Useful phrases
- 不好意思，我没听清楚，能请你再说一遍吗？  /  Sorry, I did not hear clearly. Could you say it once more, please?
- 你的意思是不是说，我们明天再讨论？  /  Do you mean that we will discuss it again tomorrow?
- 请你说得慢一点，我想记下来。  /  Please speak a little more slowly; I want to write it down.
- 这个词我不太了解，你能解释一下吗？  /  I do not quite understand this word. Could you explain it?
- 我觉得你说得很有道理，不过我有不同的看法。  /  I think what you say makes a lot of sense, but I see it differently.
- 我先说一下我的想法，请大家提意见。  /  Let me say my thoughts first, and please give your opinions, everyone.
- 如果有时间的话，我们可以再讨论一下细节。  /  If there is time, we can discuss the details again.
- 谢谢你的耐心，我学到了很多。  /  Thank you for your patience; I have learned a lot.

## Listening clips: the passage, the English and the "what to listen for" notes
- 这件事很重要，我们必须认真对待。  /  This matter is very important; we must treat it seriously.
    note: 必须 bìxū: a fall, then a high flat tone.
    note: 认真 rènzhēn: a fall, then a high flat tone.
- 我们要重视这个问题，重新检查一遍。  /  We must take this problem seriously and check it again.
    note: 重视 zhòngshì: 重 is said zhòng (heavy, serious) here, a fall.
    note: 重新 chóngxīn: 重 is said chóng (again) here, a rise.
- 我感觉有点累，想早点睡觉。  /  I feel a bit tired and want to go to bed early.
    note: 感觉 gǎnjué: 觉 is said jué (a rise) in this word; 感 only dips.
    note: 睡觉 shuìjiào: 觉 is said jiào (a fall) in this word.
- 他还没有把书还给图书馆。  /  He has not yet returned the book to the library.
    note: 还没有 hái méiyǒu: 还 is said hái (still, yet), a rise.
    note: 还给 huán gěi: 还 is said huán (give back), also a rise.
- 我去银行取钱，顺便买了一些水果。  /  I went to the bank to take out money and bought some fruit on the way.
    note: 银行 yínháng: 行 is said háng (a rise) when it means a bank.
    note: 水果 shuǐguǒ: two third tones in a row, so 水 rises (say shuíguǒ).
- 我的爱好很多，比如爬山、游泳和旅游。  /  I have many hobbies, such as climbing mountains, swimming and travelling.
    note: 爱好 àihào: 好 is said hào (a fall) when it means to like.
    note: 比如 bǐrú: 比 only dips before the rising 如.
- 他不但不生气，反而笑了。  /  Not only was he not angry, he laughed instead.
    note: 不但 búdàn: 不 is said bú before the falling 但.
    note: 不生气 bù shēngqì: 不 stays bù before a high flat tone.
- 我一直觉得这两个办法一样好。  /  I have always thought these two methods are equally good.
    note: 一直 yìzhí: 一 is said yì (a fall) before the rising 直.
    note: 一样 yíyàng: 一 is said yí (a rise) before the falling 样.
- 请你管理好自己的时间，不要浪费。  /  Please manage your time well and do not waste it.
    note: 管理 guǎnlǐ: two third tones in a row, so 管 rises (say guánlǐ).
    note: 浪费 làngfèi: both syllables fall.
- 这个地方的水果又新鲜又便宜，老板也很热情。  /  The fruit in this place is both fresh and cheap, and the boss is friendly too.
    note: 地方 dìfang: the second syllable is light and short.
    note: 老板 lǎobǎn: two third tones in a row, so 老 rises (say láobǎn).
- 这次旅游的计划已经决定了，大家都很期待。  /  The plan for this trip has been decided, and everyone is looking forward to it.
    note: 旅游 lǚyóu: 旅 has the rounded ü sound and only dips before the rising 游.
    note: 期待 qīdài: a high flat tone, then a fall.
- 老师支持我们参加比赛，还鼓励我们认真准备。  /  The teacher supports us in taking part in the competition and also encourages us to prepare seriously.
    note: 支持 zhīchí: both syllables start with the curled-tongue sounds zh and ch.
    note: 鼓励 gǔlì: 鼓 only dips before the falling 励.
- 他选择了一个全新的职业，希望积累更多的经验。  /  He chose a completely new career and hopes to gain more experience.
    note: 选择 xuǎnzé: x is a soft hiss made with the tongue tip behind the lower teeth.
    note: 职业 zhíyè: a rise, then a fall.
- 孩子们高兴地跑进了教室。  /  The children ran into the classroom happily.
    note: 高兴地 gāoxìng de: 地 is light (de) and joins the adjective to the verb.
    note: 教室 jiàoshì: both syllables fall.
- 这么多作业，你一个晚上做得了吗？  /  Can you finish so much homework in one evening?
    note: 做得了 zuò de liǎo: 了 is said liǎo here, meaning 'able to'.
    note: 晚上 wǎnshang: the second syllable is light and short.
- 暑假的时候，我回老家看了爷爷奶奶。  /  During the summer holiday, I went back to my hometown to see my grandparents.
    note: 暑假 shǔjià: 假 is said jià (holiday), a fall; 暑 only dips.
    note: 爷爷 yéye: the second syllable is light and short.
- 听音乐能让人放松，也能让人感到快乐。  /  Listening to music can help people relax and can also make people feel happy.
    note: 音乐 yīnyuè: 乐 is said yuè (music), a fall.
    note: 快乐 kuàilè: 乐 is said lè (happy), a fall.
- 我儿子长得很快，已经比我高了。  /  My son is growing fast and is already taller than me.
    note: 长得 zhǎng de: 长 is said zhǎng (to grow), a dip; 得 is light.
    note: 比我 bǐ wǒ: two third tones in a row, so 比 rises (say bíwǒ).
- 我希望你能理解我的想法。  /  I hope you can understand my thinking.
    note: 理解 lǐjiě: two third tones in a row, so 理 rises (say líjiě).
    note: 希望 xīwàng: a high flat tone, then a fall.
- 办这个手续有点麻烦，需要很多材料。  /  Doing this procedure is a bit troublesome and requires many materials.
    note: 麻烦 máfan: the second syllable is light and short.
    note: 手续 shǒuxù: 手 only dips before the falling 续.
- 由于下大雨，比赛被推迟到了下个星期。  /  Because of the heavy rain, the match was put off until next week.
    note: 由于 yóuyú: both syllables rise.
    note: 推迟 tuīchí: a high flat tone, then a rise.
- 他的关心让我感到非常温暖。  /  His concern makes me feel very warm.
    note: 温暖 wēnnuǎn: a high flat tone, then a dip.
    note: 感到 gǎndào: 感 only dips before the falling 到.
- 会议将在下午三点半准时开始。  /  The meeting will start on time at half past three this afternoon.
    note: 三点半 sān diǎn bàn: 点 only dips before the falling 半.
    note: 开始 kāishǐ: a high flat tone, then a dip.
- 他说话的声音很轻，我几乎听不清楚。  /  He speaks in a very soft voice, and I can hardly hear clearly.
    note: 几乎 jīhū: both syllables are high and flat.
    note: 清楚 qīngchu: the second syllable is light and short.
- 他每天都坚持锻炼身体，所以很少生病。  /  He keeps exercising every day, so he rarely gets sick.
    note: 坚持 jiānchí: a high flat tone, then a rise.
    note: 锻炼 duànliàn: both syllables fall.
- 我打算明年去国外留学，现在正在准备材料。  /  I plan to go abroad to study next year, and I am preparing the documents now.
    note: 打算 dǎsuàn: 打 only dips before the falling 算.
    note: 留学 liúxué: both syllables rise.
- 这家餐厅的服务很好，菜也很好吃。  /  The service in this restaurant is very good, and the food is tasty too.
    note: 餐厅 cāntīng: both syllables are high and flat.
    note: 服务 fúwù: a rise, then a fall.
- 你的汉语说得越来越好了。  /  Your Chinese is getting better and better.
    note: 越来越 yuè lái yuè: a fall, a rise, then a fall.
    note: 汉语 Hànyǔ: a fall, then a dip.
- 我们应该保护环境，不要乱扔垃圾。  /  We should protect the environment and not throw rubbish about.
    note: 保护 bǎohù: 保 only dips before the falling 护.
    note: 环境 huánjìng: a rise, then a fall.
- 请把声音调小一点，孩子已经睡着了。  /  Please turn the volume down a little; the child has already fallen asleep.
    note: 调小 tiáoxiǎo: 调 is said tiáo (to adjust), a rise.
    note: 睡着 shuìzháo: 着 is said zháo here (the action worked), a rise.
