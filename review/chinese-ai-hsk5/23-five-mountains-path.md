The HSK 5 Learn path is a pilgrimage up the Five Great Mountains (五岳 Wǔ Yuè): Mount Tai in the east, Mount Hua in the west, Mount Heng in the south (衡山, Hunan), Mount Heng in the north (恒山, Shanxi) and Mount Song in the centre. Each unit is one stop on a trail; the level exam is the summit. The stop names are real places or sights on each mountain (an archway, a temple, a pavilion, a rock); the short story line for each unit is mine.

Path title: The Five Great Mountains | tag: 五岳 wǔ yuè | blurb: For more than two thousand years, pilgrims have climbed the Five Great Mountains of China. You climb them in turn, from Mount Tai in the east to Mount Song in the centre. Each unit is one stop on a trail, and the level exam is the summit.
Mountain 东: Mount Tai · East · everyday life — Start where the sun comes up. The first mountain is a long climb of ordinary words: people, numbers, time, food, home and shops.
Mountain 西: Mount Hua · West · town, travel and work — The steepest path of the five. Cities, roads, jobs, money and rules, with a chain to hold on to.
Mountain 南: Mount Heng, south · South · body and heart — Green hills and quiet temples: your body, your feelings, what you think and what you enjoy. This Héng is written 衡.
Mountain 北: Mount Heng, north · North · learning and the world — The northern mountain has a temple on a cliff. School, science, nature and how things are made. This Héng is written 恒, and sounds the same as the southern one.
Mountain 中: Mount Song · Centre · ideas and joining them — The last mountain stands in the middle. The new patterns tie everything together, and the level exam waits at the top.
