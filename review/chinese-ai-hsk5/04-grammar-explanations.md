Lessons:

### Lesson 1: 一旦…就…, 因而 and 从而: once, so, and so
- 一旦 … 就 … means 'once, if it ever happens': as soon as the condition is true, the result follows. It is used for something that is not certain but, if it happens, matters a lot: 一旦下雨，比赛就会停 'Once it rains, the match will stop'. It often points to a worry or a rule, and it sounds more serious than 要是.
- 因而 'so, for that reason' and 从而 'and so, thereby' join a result to what came before, mostly in writing. 因而 gives the result of a reason: 他经常运动，因而身体很好 'He often exercises, so his health is good'. 从而 adds a second step that follows from the first action, often a good result: 多读书可以提高水平，从而找到更好的工作 'Reading more can raise one's level, and so help one find a better job'.
- (common mistake) 一旦 suggests that the condition is serious or unlikely. For an everyday 'if', use 如果 or 要是: 如果你有空，就来看我。
- (common mistake) 从而 needs a first action before it. Do not use it after 因为: 因为他很忙，从而没来 is wrong; say 因为他很忙，所以没来 or 他很忙，因而没来.
- example: 一旦下雨，比赛就会停。 / Once it rains, the match will stop.
- example: 你一旦开始，就不能放弃。 / Once you start, you cannot give up.
- example: 他经常运动，因而身体很好。 / He often exercises, so his health is good.
- example: 多读书可以提高水平，从而找到更好的工作。 / Reading more can raise one's level, and so help one find a better job.

### Lesson 2: 或是…或是…, …也好…也好 and 加上: either, whether, plus
- 或是 … 或是 … means 'either … or …', listing choices that all work: 周末我或是看书，或是看电影 'At the weekend I either read or watch films'. The first 或是 can be dropped: 你可以喝茶或是咖啡 'You can have tea or coffee'. 或是 is the same as 或者, a little more written. It is for statements; in a question use 还是.
- … 也好，… 也好 means 'whether … or …': the result is the same whichever one you pick. Put 也好 after each choice and use 都 in the result: 买也好，不买也好，我都没意见 'Whether to buy or not, I have no opinion'.
- 加上 'on top of that, plus' adds one more reason: 天气不好，加上时间太晚，我们就不去了 'The weather is bad and, on top of that, it is too late, so we are not going'. 以及 'and, as well as' joins things of the same kind, often the last two in a list: 苹果、香蕉以及西瓜 'apples, bananas and watermelons'.
- (common mistake) Use 或是 in statements and 还是 in questions: 你喝茶还是咖啡？ is right; 你喝茶或是咖啡？ is wrong.
- (common mistake) 也好 needs a result that is the same either way, so put 都 in the result: 去也好，不去也好，我都没意见.
- example: 周末我或是看书，或是看电影。 / At the weekend I either read or watch films.
- example: 买也好，不买也好，我都没意见。 / Whether to buy or not, I have no opinion.
- example: 天气不好，加上时间太晚，我们就不去了。 / The weather is bad and, on top of that, it is too late, so we are not going.
- example: 桌子上有苹果、香蕉以及西瓜。 / There are apples, bananas and watermelons on the table.

### Lesson 3: 再…也… and 没有…就没有…: however, without
- 再 … 也 … means 'however …, still …': whatever the degree, the result does not change. 再 goes before an adjective or a verb and 也 before the result: 再累，我也要完成工作 'However tired I am, I still need to finish the work'; 这个东西再贵，他也想买 'However expensive this thing is, he still wants to buy it'. Here 再 does not mean 'again'.
- 没有 … 就没有 … means 'without …, there is no …': the first thing is the base of the second. 没有水，就没有生命 'Without water, there is no life'; 没有你的帮助，就没有我的今天 'Without your help, there would be no me today'.
- (common mistake) Keep 也 in the result. 再累，我要完成工作 loses the meaning 'however'; say 再累，我也要完成工作.
- (common mistake) 再 already means 'however', so do not add 很 after it: 再冷 is right, 再很冷 is wrong.
- example: 再累，我也要完成工作。 / However tired I am, I still need to finish the work.
- example: 这个东西再贵，他也想买。 / However expensive this thing is, he still wants to buy it.
- example: 没有水，就没有生命。 / Without water, there is no life.
- example: 没有你的帮助，就没有我的今天。 / Without your help, there would be no me today.

### Lesson 4: 为的是, 以便 and 以…为…: in order to, take … as …
- 为的是 + purpose says why someone does something that you have just mentioned: 他每天跑步，为的是身体健康 'He runs every day in order to be healthy'. The action comes first, the purpose second. 以便 'so that' also puts the purpose second but is more formal and the purpose becomes possible because of the action: 请留下电话号码，以便我们找你 'Please leave your phone number so that we can find you'.
- 以 … 为 … means 'take … as …'. 以 … 为中心 is 'with … as the centre': 以学校为中心，附近有很多商店 'With the school as the centre, there are many shops nearby'. 以 … 为目标 is 'with … as the goal': 他以考上大学为目标，每天都很努力 'He takes getting into university as his goal and works hard every day'. 为 is read wéi here.
- (common mistake) 为的是 comes after the action. 为的是身体健康，他每天跑步 is back to front in this pattern; say 他每天跑步，为的是身体健康.
- (common mistake) 为的是 already means 'in order to', so do not add 为了 after it. In 以 … 为 …, 为 is read wéi: say 以学校为中心, not 以学校是中心.
- example: 他每天跑步，为的是身体健康。 / He runs every day in order to be healthy.
- example: 请留下电话号码，以便我们联系你。 / Please leave your phone number so that we will contact you.
- example: 以学校为中心，附近有很多商店。 / With the school as the centre, there are many shops nearby.
- example: 他以考上大学为目标，每天都很努力。 / He takes getting into university as his goal and works hard every day.

### Lesson 5: 反而, 不然 / 要不 and 毕竟: on the contrary, otherwise, after all
- 反而 'on the contrary, instead' shows a result that is the opposite of what you would expect: 雨不但没停，反而更大了 'The rain did not stop; on the contrary, it got heavier'. It goes before the verb or adjective in the second half and often follows 不但没 / 没有.
- 不然 and 要不 'otherwise, or else' warn what will happen if the first thing is not done: 快一点，不然我们会迟到 'Be quick, or we will be late'. 要不 also suggests another idea: 要不我们明天再去吧？ 'Or shall we go again tomorrow?'. 否则 (HSK 4) does the same job and sounds more formal.
- 毕竟 'after all' reminds the listener of a basic fact that explains or excuses something: 他毕竟还是个孩子，别生气 'He is only a child after all; do not be angry'. It goes before the verb or the noun phrase.
- (common mistake) Use 反而 only when the result goes against what you expect. 吃了药，他反而好了 is odd; say 吃了药，他反而更不舒服了.
- (common mistake) 不然 comes after the thing you should do and before the bad result: 快一点，不然会迟到. Do not put the warning first.
- example: 雨不但没停，反而更大了。 / The rain did not stop; on the contrary, it got heavier.
- example: 快一点，不然我们会迟到。 / Be quick, or we will be late.
- example: 要不我们明天再去吧？ / Or shall we go again tomorrow?
- example: 他毕竟还是个孩子，别生气。 / He is only a child after all; do not be angry.

### Lesson 6: 果然, 居然 and 似乎: as expected, unexpectedly, it seems
- 果然 'sure enough, as expected' says that something turned out just as you thought: 我说今天会下雨，果然下雨了 'I said it would rain today, and sure enough it rained'. 居然 'unexpectedly, actually' is the opposite: you did not expect it. 他居然忘了自己的生日 'He actually forgot his own birthday'. 居然 is like 竟然 (HSK 4) and a little more common in speech.
- 似乎 'it seems, seemingly' makes a careful guess from what you see: 他今天似乎不太高兴 'He seems not very happy today'. 仿佛 'as if' compares, and often ends with 似的: 她仿佛没听见似的，继续看书 'She keeps reading as if she did not hear'.
- (common mistake) 果然 is for what you expected. If you are surprised, use 居然 or 竟然: 他居然没来 'He actually did not come'.
- (common mistake) 似乎 is for a guess, not for a fact you know. Do not say 他似乎是我的老师 when you know he is.
- example: 我说今天会下雨，果然下雨了。 / I said it would rain today, and sure enough it rained.
- example: 他居然忘了自己的生日。 / He actually forgot his own birthday.
- example: 他今天似乎不太高兴。 / He seems not very happy today.
- example: 她仿佛没听见似的，继续看书。 / She keeps reading as if she did not hear.

### Lesson 7: 从…来看, 拿…来说 and 在…看来: judging from, for example, in one's view
- 从 … 来看 'judging from …' gives the basis for a judgement. Put what you look at between 从 and 来看: 从天气来看，明天会下雨 'Judging from the weather, it will rain tomorrow'; 从他的成绩来看，他学得很好 'Judging from his results, he is studying well'.
- 拿 … 来说 'take … for example, as for …' picks one case to show a general point: 拿我来说，我每天都跑步 'Take me, for example: I run every day'. The case after 拿 is a person or a thing, and the main sentence says something about it.
- 在 … 看来 'in … view, as … sees it' gives an opinion. What follows 在 is a person or a group: 在我看来，这个计划太难了 'In my view, this plan is too hard'. 在 … 看来 is a person's opinion; 从 … 来看 is about the evidence you look at.
- (common mistake) 来看 closes the 从 phrase: 从天气来看 is right; 从来看天气 is wrong.
- (common mistake) 在 … 看来 needs a person or a group: 在老师看来 'in the teacher's view'. Do not put a thing there: 在天气看来 is wrong.
- example: 从天气来看，明天会下雨。 / Judging from the weather, it will rain tomorrow.
- example: 从他的成绩来看，他学得很好。 / Judging from his results, he is studying well.
- example: 拿我来说，我每天都跑步。 / Take me, for example: I run every day.
- example: 在我看来，这个计划太难了。 / In my view, this plan is too hard.

### Lesson 8: 由此, 总之 and 到…为止: from this, in short, up to
- 由此 'from this, hence' leads to a conclusion drawn from what was just said, mostly in writing. 由此可见 'from this it can be seen' is the usual set phrase: 他每天学习五个小时，由此可见，他很努力 'He studies five hours a day; from this it can be seen that he works hard'. 总之 'in short, anyway' sums up several points and starts the closing sentence: 总之，我们要努力学习 'In short, we must study hard'.
- 到 … 为止 'up to …, until …' marks an end point. The thing after 到 is a time or a stage: 到今天为止，我已经学了一百个词 'Up to today, I have learned one hundred words'; 活动到下周五为止 'The event runs until next Friday'. 为 is read wéi here.
- (common mistake) 由此 and 总之 are more written than 所以. In a casual chat, 所以 sounds more natural.
- (common mistake) 为止 only goes with 到: say 到今天为止, not 从今天为止.
- example: 他每天学习五个小时，由此可见，他很努力。 / He studies five hours a day; from this it can be seen that he works hard.
- example: 总之，我们要努力学习。 / In short, we must study hard.
- example: 到今天为止，我已经学了一百个词。 / Up to today, I have learned one hundred words.
- example: 活动到下周五为止。 / The event runs until next Friday.

### Lesson 9: 凭, 依据 and 靠 / 依靠: relying on
- 凭 'on the strength of, by' + noun says what makes something possible: 他凭努力考上了大学 'He got into university by working hard'. 凭借 is the formal form: 他凭借自己的经验解决了问题 'He solved the problem by means of his own experience'. 凭什么 asks 'on what grounds?' and sounds sharp: 你凭什么这样说？ 'What gives you the right to say this?'
- 依据 'on the basis of' + noun is written: 依据这个规定，大家都要早点来 'According to this rule, everyone must come early'. 依据 is also a noun, 'basis': 这个办法没有依据 'This method has no basis'. 根据 (HSK 3) is the everyday word.
- 靠 and 依靠 'rely on, depend on' say whom or what one depends on: 他靠写文章生活 'He lives by writing articles'; 孩子需要依靠父母 'Children need to rely on their parents'. 靠 also means 'to lean on': 靠在墙上 'lean on the wall'.
- (common mistake) 凭 goes before the verb, with what it rests on right after it: 他凭努力考上了大学. 他考上了大学凭努力 is wrong.
- (common mistake) 凭什么 is a sharp 'on what grounds?'. For a polite question, use 为什么.
- example: 他凭努力考上了大学。 / He got into university by working hard.
- example: 他凭借自己的经验解决了问题。 / He solved the problem by means of his own experience.
- example: 依据这个规定，大家都要早点来。 / According to this rule, everyone must come early.
- example: 他靠写文章生活。 / He lives by writing articles.

### Lesson 10: 将, 由 and 所…的: formal 把, by, what someone …
- 将 is a formal 把 in writing: 请将书放在桌子上 'Please put the book on the table'; 他将这件事告诉了大家 'He told everyone about this'. The pattern is the same as 把: 将 + thing + verb + something extra after the verb.
- 由 'by, up to' tells who is responsible: 这件事由他来做 'He will take care of this'; 由你决定 'It is up to you'. It also names who does a job in a formal sentence: 这次会议由经理主持 'This meeting is chaired by the manager'.
- 所 + verb + 的 turns a verb into 'what someone …': 这是我所知道的 'This is what I know'; 他所说的都是真的 'Everything he says is true'. 所 is formal; in speech say 我知道的 or 他说的.
- (common mistake) 将 is for writing and formal speech. In daily talk, 把 is more natural: 请把书放在桌子上.
- (common mistake) Do not use 由 for transport. Say 坐火车 'go by train', not 由火车.
- example: 请将书放在桌子上。 / Please put the book on the table.
- example: 这件事由他来做。 / He will take care of this.
- example: 这次会议由经理主持。 / This meeting is chaired by the manager.
- example: 他所说的都是真的。 / Everything he says is true.

### Lesson 11: 令, 使 and 让: make someone feel
- 令 + person + feeling means 'make someone feel …', in writing: 这个消息令大家很高兴 'This news makes everyone very happy'; 他的话令我很感动 'His words move me very much'. 使 (HSK 4) works the same way and is formal too: 这个结果使老师很满意 'This result makes the teacher very satisfied'. 让 is the everyday word: 这个消息让我很高兴.
- 令人 + adjective is a fixed way to say 'it makes people …': 令人吃惊 'surprising', 令人满意 'satisfying', 令人担心 'worrying'. The person who feels it must come after 令, 使 or 让.
- (common mistake) 令 is for writing. In speech use 让: 这个消息让我很高兴.
- (common mistake) A person must come after 令, 使 or 让. 这个消息令很高兴 is wrong, because it does not say who is happy.
- example: 这个消息令大家很高兴。 / This news makes everyone very happy.
- example: 他的话令我很感动。 / His words move me very much.
- example: 这件事令人吃惊。 / This matter is surprising.
- example: 这个结果使老师很满意。 / This result makes the teacher very satisfied.

### Lesson 12: 彼此 and 如此: each other, so
- 彼此 'each other, one another' shows that both sides do the same thing to each other: 我们彼此了解 'We understand each other'; 同学们彼此帮助 'The classmates help each other'. It is like 互相 (HSK 4) but more formal. 对方 (HSK 4) points to one person on the other side: 我了解对方 'I understand the other person'.
- 如此 'so, like this' stands for a degree or way that was just mentioned: 他如此努力，一定会成功 'He works so hard that he will surely succeed'; 事情就是如此 'That is how things are'. 如此 is written; in speech say 这么 or 这样.
- (common mistake) 彼此 already means 'each other', so do not add 互相: 彼此互相帮助 repeats the idea.
- (common mistake) 如此 is formal. In daily speech say 这么 or 这样: 他这么努力.
- example: 我们彼此了解。 / We understand each other.
- example: 同学们彼此帮助。 / The classmates help each other.
- example: 他如此努力，一定会成功。 / He works so hard that he will surely succeed.
- example: 事情就是如此。 / That is how things are.

### Lesson 13: 过于, 稍, 够…的 and 可: too, a little, quite, really
- 过于 'too, excessively' goes before an adjective or a verb and says that something goes past the right amount: 这个问题过于简单 'This question is too simple'; 你不要过于担心 'Do not worry too much'. It is more formal than 太, and it usually sounds like a complaint, so do not use it for praise.
- 稍 'a little, slightly' goes before a verb or an adjective: 请稍等一下 'Please wait a moment'; 这件衣服稍大一点 'This piece of clothing is a little big'. With an adjective, add 一点 or 一些 to show the amount. 稍 is mostly written; 稍微 (HSK 4) is the everyday form.
- 够 … 的 'quite, rather' puts an adjective between 够 and 的 and adds a feeling, often surprise, sympathy or complaint: 今天够冷的 'It is quite cold today'; 这个问题够难的 'This question is quite hard'. 够 alone just means 'enough'.
- 可 'really, indeed' adds force in speech to a warning, a promise or an opinion: 你可要小心啊 'You must be careful'; 这可是个好机会 'This really is a good chance'; 这件事可不容易 'This really is not easy'. This 可 is not the 可 that means 'but'. It often comes with 要, 别, 是, 不 or 真.
- (common mistake) 过于 sounds like a complaint. For something good, say 非常 or 很: 非常好, not 过于好.
- (common mistake) Do not stack two degree words. 过于太 is wrong; use one of them.
- example: 这个问题过于简单。 / This question is too simple.
- example: 请稍等一下。 / Please wait a moment.
- example: 今天够冷的，多穿一点吧。 / It is quite cold today; wear a bit more.
- example: 你可要小心啊！ / You must be careful!

### Lesson 14: 将要, 时常, 时刻, 立刻 and 立即: about to, often, always, at once
- 将要 'be about to, will soon' goes before a verb: 比赛将要开始 'The match is about to start'; 我们将要搬到新家 'We will move to a new home soon'. 将要 is formal and usually does not end with 了. In speech, say 快要 … 了 or 就要 … 了 (HSK 3).
- 时常 'often' goes before the verb: 他时常去公园跑步 'He often goes to the park to run'. 经常 (HSK 3) is the everyday word. 时刻 as an adverb means 'at every moment, always', and stresses doing something all the time: 我们要时刻注意安全 'We must pay attention to safety at all times'.
- 立刻 and 立即 'at once, immediately' go before the verb: 听到电话响，他立刻跑了过去 'Hearing the phone ring, he ran over at once'; 请立即回家 'Please go home at once'. 立即 is more formal and is common in orders and notices. In speech, 马上 (HSK 3) is the usual word.
- (common mistake) 将要 does not usually take 了 at the end. For 'is about to start', say 比赛快要开始了.
- (common mistake) 立刻 and 立即 go before the verb, not after it: 他立刻跑了过去, not 他跑了过去立刻.
- example: 比赛将要开始。 / The match is about to start.
- example: 他时常去公园跑步。 / He often goes to the park to run.
- example: 我们要时刻注意安全。 / We must pay attention to safety at all times.
- example: 听到电话响，他立刻跑了过去。 / Hearing the phone ring, he ran over at once.

### Lesson 15: 渐渐, 始终 and 连续: gradually, all along, in a row
- 渐渐 and 逐渐 'gradually, little by little' show a change that comes slowly. They go before the verb or adjective and often end with 了: 天渐渐黑了 'It is getting dark'; 他的汉语逐渐好起来了 'His Chinese has gradually got better'. 逐步 'step by step' is for planned progress: 公司逐步扩大了 'The company has gradually grown bigger'.
- 始终 'all along, from start to finish' says that something never changed: 他始终没有告诉我这件事 'He never told me about this'; 她始终相信自己会成功 'She always believes she will succeed'. It often goes with 没 or 不 for 'never at any point'. 一直 (HSK 3) is the everyday word.
- 连续 'in a row' goes with a number of days, times or years: 他连续三天没有睡觉 'He did not sleep for three days in a row'. 持续 'go on, last' stresses one stretch that does not stop: 雨持续下了一个星期 'The rain went on for a week'. 连续 counts events one after another; 持续 is a single long stretch.
- (common mistake) 渐渐 and 逐渐 need a change. 天渐渐黑了 is right; for a fixed fact, 他渐渐是老师 is wrong.
- (common mistake) 持续 does not go with a count of separate events. Say 他连续三次来我家, not 他持续三次来我家.
- example: 天渐渐黑了。 / It is getting dark.
- example: 他的汉语逐渐好起来了。 / His Chinese has gradually got better.
- example: 她始终相信自己会成功。 / She always believes she will succeed.
- example: 他连续三天没有睡觉。 / He did not sleep for three days in a row.

### Lesson 16: 再次, 随后, 此后, 先后 and 陆续: again, afterwards, one after another
- 再次 'once more, again' goes before the verb. It is more formal than 又 or 再, and it can look back or ahead: 我再次见到了老朋友 'I saw my old friend again'; 请再次检查 'Please check again'.
- 随后, 此后 and 此时 tie an event to a point in time. 随后 'soon after, then' follows straight after something: 他先吃了饭，随后就去上班了 'He ate first and then went to work'. 此后 'from then on, after that' starts a stretch of time after that point: 此后，他每天都来这里 'After that, he comes here every day'. 此时 'at this moment' points to one moment: 此时，天已经黑了 'At this moment, it was already dark'.
- 先后 and 陆续 'one after another' need more than one person, thing or time. 先后 gives the order, often with a number: 他先后去过三个国家 'He has been to three countries, one after another'. 陆续 stresses a steady stream, not all at once: 客人们陆续到了 'The guests arrived one after another'.
- (common mistake) 随后 follows straight after the first event. For a gap of weeks or years, 后来 is better.
- (common mistake) 先后 and 陆续 need more than one. 客人们陆续到了 is right; 他陆续到了 is wrong, because one person cannot arrive 'one after another'.
- example: 我再次见到了老朋友。 / I saw my old friend again.
- example: 他先吃了饭，随后就去上班了。 / He ate first and then went to work.
- example: 此时，天已经黑了。 / At this moment, it was already dark.
- example: 客人们陆续到了。 / The guests arrived one after another.

### Lesson 17: 简直, 绝对, 根本 and 难以: simply, absolutely, at all, hard to
- 简直 'simply, practically' adds strong feeling to an adjective or a verb. The speaker sounds amazed or is exaggerating: 今天简直太热了 'Today is simply too hot'; 这里的风景简直像画一样 'The scenery here is simply like a painting'. It shows a feeling, not a fact, so it goes with words like 太, 像 and 是.
- 绝对 'absolutely' marks something as certain. With a negative it means 'never, under no circumstances': 这件事绝对不能告诉别人 'This absolutely cannot be told to anyone'. With a positive, it means 'for sure': 你绝对可以相信他 'You can absolutely trust him'. 这个答案绝对没有错 'There is absolutely no mistake in this answer'.
- 根本 'at all' goes before 不 or 没 and denies in full: 我根本不知道这件事 'I do not know about this at all'; 他根本没来 'He did not come at all'. In a positive sentence, 根本 is not 'completely'; as a noun it means 'root, basis'.
- 难以 'hard to' goes before a verb of two syllables: 这个问题难以解决 'This problem is hard to solve'; 那次旅行让我难以忘记 'That trip is hard for me to forget'. It is more formal than 很难 and is used for things like understanding, believing and accepting, not for plain physical actions.
- (common mistake) 根本 needs a negative. 我根本不知道 is right; 我根本知道 is wrong.
- (common mistake) 难以 goes before a verb of two syllables. Say 难以理解 but 很难学, not 难以学.
- example: 今天简直太热了。 / Today is simply too hot.
- example: 这件事绝对不能告诉别人。 / This absolutely cannot be told to anyone.
- example: 我根本不知道这件事。 / I do not know about this at all.
- example: 这个问题难以解决。 / This problem is hard to solve.

### Lesson 18: 尽量, 尽力 and 万一: as far as possible, do your best, in case
- 尽量 'as much as possible, to the fullest extent' goes before a verb and sets a goal for how much or how well: 请尽量早点来 'Please come as early as possible'; 我会尽量少吃甜的 'I will eat as little sweet food as possible'. 尽力 'do all one can' is about how hard someone tries: 我会尽力帮你 'I will do my best to help you'. 尽量 is about the degree you reach; 尽力 is about the effort you give.
- 万一 'in case, if by any chance' brings in something unlikely, and usually unwelcome, that might happen. A plan or a request follows, often with 就: 万一下雨，我们就在家看电影 'In case it rains, we will watch a movie at home'; 万一你没找到我，就给我打电话 'In case you do not manage to find me, call me'.
- (common mistake) 尽量 and 尽力 are not swapped freely. Say 尽量少吃, 'eat as little as possible', not 尽力少吃.
- (common mistake) 万一 is for something unlikely. For an ordinary 'if', use 如果: 如果明天有空，我们去看电影.
- example: 请尽量早点来。 / Please come as early as possible.
- example: 我会尽量少吃甜的。 / I will eat as little sweet food as possible.
- example: 我会尽力帮你。 / I will do my best to help you.
- example: 万一下雨，我们就在家看电影。 / In case it rains, we will watch a movie at home.

### Lesson 19: 再也不 / 没 and 怎么都 / 也 + 不 / 没: never again, however hard I try
- 再也不 + verb 'not any more, never again' says what someone will not do from now on: 我再也不会相信他了 'I will not trust him any more'; 我再也不吃那家店的菜了 'I will not eat the food from that restaurant any more'. 再也没(有) + verb says that something has not happened again since a past time: 他走了以后，再也没回来过 'After he left, he never came back again'. 不 is for a decision or a state; 没 is for what did not happen.
- 怎么 + 都 / 也 + verb + 不 / 没 'however hard one tries, still not' shows that effort does not work: 这个字我怎么也写不好 'However hard I try, I do not manage to write this character well'; 我怎么都想不起来他的名字 'However hard I try, I do not manage to think of his name'. It usually goes with a 不 complement (写不好, 打不开, 想不起来). 怎么都 and 怎么也 mean the same.
- (common mistake) 再也 must be followed by 不 or 没. 再也吃 is wrong. 再 alone, as in 再吃, means 'again', which is the opposite.
- (common mistake) With a 不 complement, 怎么也打不开 means 'try but fail'. With 不 before the verb, 怎么也不打开 means 'refuse, however much'. They are not the same.
- example: 我再也不会相信他了。 / I will not trust him any more.
- example: 他走了以后，再也没回来过。 / After he left, he never came back again.
- example: 这个字我怎么也写不好。 / However hard I try, I do not manage to write this character well.
- example: 我怎么都想不起来他的名字。 / However hard I try, I do not manage to think of his name.

### Lesson 20: A来A去, A着A着 and 说A就A: again and again, while …, the moment you say so
- A来A去 repeats a verb to show that an action goes on again and again, or back and forth: 他在房间里走来走去 'He walks back and forth in the room'; 我想来想去，还是决定去了 'After thinking it over and over, I still decided to go'. The result or decision follows, often with 还是 or 也.
- A着A着 'while doing A, something happens' shows an action that goes on until a new, often unexpected thing comes in: 他看着看着就睡着了 'He fell asleep while watching'; 我们走着走着，下起雨来了 'We were walking when it began to rain'. The second part needs a new event.
- 说A就A 'do A the moment one says so' repeats the verb to show that someone acts at once, with no delay: 他说走就走，没有等我们 'He left the moment he said so, and did not wait for us'; 说干就干，我们现在就开始吧 'Let us get to it right away; let us start now'; 他说哭就哭 'He cries at the drop of a hat'.
- (common mistake) A来A去 uses the same verb twice: 走来走去, 想来想去. Two different verbs do not make this pattern.
- (common mistake) A着A着 needs a second event. 他看着看着 alone sounds unfinished; add 就睡着了 or something similar.
- example: 他在房间里走来走去。 / He walks back and forth in the room.
- example: 我想来想去，还是决定去了。 / After thinking it over and over, I still decided to go.
- example: 他看着看着就睡着了。 / He fell asleep while watching.
- example: 他说走就走，没有等我们。 / He left the moment he said so, and did not wait for us.

### Lesson 21: 有A有B, X也不是Y也不是 and 一来…二来…: both, stuck either way, for one thing
- 有A有B 'both A and B' joins two things of the same kind that both exist or both happen. With nouns it shows what there is: 这家饭店有吃有喝，还有音乐 'This restaurant has food and drink, and music too'. With verbs, it shows two actions at the same time: 他们有说有笑地走进了教室 'They walked into the classroom talking and laughing'.
- X也不是，Y也不是 'whichever way, it is not right' describes being stuck in an awkward spot, where neither choice works: 他坐也不是，站也不是，不知道该怎么办 'He does not know whether to sit or stand, and does not know what to do'; 我说也不是，不说也不是，真难办 'I do not know whether to speak or keep quiet; it is really awkward'.
- 一来…二来… 'for one thing …, for another …' lists two reasons or two aims in order: 今天我不去了，一来天气不好，二来我有点累 'I am not going today: first, the weather is bad; second, I am a bit tired'; 我想去中国，一来学汉语，二来看朋友 'I want to go to China: first to learn Chinese, second to see my friends'.
- (common mistake) 有A有B needs the same kind of word on both sides: 有说有笑 joins two verbs. 有说有好 is wrong.
- (common mistake) 一来 and 二来 come as a pair. 一来 with no 二来 sounds unfinished.
- example: 这家饭店有吃有喝，还有音乐。 / This restaurant has food and drink, and music too.
- example: 他们有说有笑地走进了教室。 / They walked into the classroom talking and laughing.
- example: 他坐也不是，站也不是，不知道该怎么办。 / He does not know whether to sit or stand, and does not know what to do.
- example: 今天我不去了，一来天气不好，二来我有点累。 / I am not going today: first, the weather is bad; second, I am a bit tired.

### Lesson 22: 看你…的, X什么X and 什么…不…的: look how, why bother, never mind
- 看你 + adjective or verb + 的 'look how …' shows how someone's state strikes the speaker, with surprise, pity or teasing: 看你急的 'Look how worried you are!'; 看你累的，快去休息吧 'Look how tired you are; go and rest'. 看你说的 'Come on, what a thing to say' pushes back on what someone said: 看你说的，我怎么会不帮你呢？'Come on, why would I not help you?'
- X什么X 'why bother …, no need to …' repeats a verb or adjective with 什么 in the middle to tell someone to stop: 急什么急？时间还早呢 'Why the hurry? It is still early'; 怕什么怕？有我在呢 'What is there to fear? I am here'. It sounds blunt, so use it with people you know well.
- 什么 + A + 不 + A + 的 'never mind (whether) …' waves aside the other person's worry: 什么钱不钱的，我们是朋友 'Never mind money; we are friends'; 什么麻烦不麻烦的，都是小事 'Never mind trouble; it is all small things'. The word in the middle is the thing the other person has just worried about.
- (common mistake) X什么X is blunt and can sound rude to a stranger. To be polite, say 别急 or 不要急.
- (common mistake) 看你 … 的 needs a state or an action: 看你急的 is right. A plain noun does not fit, so 看你书的 is wrong.
- example: 看你累的，快去休息吧。 / Look how tired you are; go and rest quickly.
- example: 看你说的，我怎么会不帮你呢？ / Come on, why would I not help you?
- example: 急什么急？时间还早呢。 / Why the hurry? It is still early.
- example: 什么钱不钱的，我们是朋友！ / Never mind money; we are friends!

### Lesson 23: 不得了, 极了 and 得很: extremely
- Adjective or feeling + 得不得了 'extremely, terribly' shows a degree with no limit: 他高兴得不得了 'He is extremely happy'. 不得了 can also stand alone as a comment, 'This is serious!': 这可不得了！ It can be good or bad.
- 极了 'extremely' follows an adjective or a feeling at the end of the clause: 这里的风景美极了 'The scenery here is extremely beautiful'. 极了 already carries the degree, so do not add 很 or 非常 before the adjective.
- 得很 'very' follows an adjective and is more casual: 他累得很，想早点睡觉 'He is very tired and wants to go to bed early'. In formal writing, 非常 is better. As with 极了, no 很 goes before the adjective.
- (common mistake) 极了 and 得很 already carry the degree. Do not add 很 or 非常: 很美极了 is wrong; say 美极了.
- (common mistake) 得很 is spoken and casual. In an essay or a notice, use 非常 instead.
- example: 他高兴得不得了。 / He is extremely happy.
- example: 这里的风景美极了。 / The scenery here is extremely beautiful.
- example: 他累得很，想早点睡觉。 / He is very tired and wants to go to bed early.
- example: 这可不得了！ / This is serious!

### Lesson 24: V得 + clause and 得了 / 不了: so … that, manage to or not
- 得 after a verb or an adjective can bring in a whole clause that shows how far the action or state went: 他高兴得跳了起来 'He was so happy that he jumped up'; 她哭得眼睛都红了 'She cried until her eyes were red'; 他忙得没有时间吃饭 'He was so busy that he had no time to eat'. The result is a full phrase, not a single word.
- 得了 and 不了, read liǎo, after a verb tell whether someone can manage the action, mostly about amount or ability. 吃得了 'can finish eating' and 吃不了 'cannot finish eating'. 这么多菜，我们吃得了吗？ 'Will we manage to finish so many dishes?'; 这么多菜，我吃不了 'There is so much food that I will not manage to finish it'; 明天我有事，去不了 'I have something on tomorrow, so I will not make it'. Do not mix it up with 不得了 'extremely'.
- (common mistake) After 得 + clause, do not add 很 between: 他忙得没有时间吃饭 is right; 他忙得很没有时间 is wrong.
- (common mistake) 吃不了 means 'cannot finish', not 'will not eat'. To refuse, say 不吃.
- example: 他高兴得跳了起来。 / He was so happy that he jumped up.
- example: 她哭得眼睛都红了。 / She cried until her eyes were red.
- example: 这么多菜，我们吃得了吗？ / Will we manage to finish so many dishes?
- example: 明天我有事，去不了。 / I have something on tomorrow, so I will not make it.

### Lesson 25: 起来, 下去, 下来 and 上: beyond the direction
- 起来 does more than 'rise'. After an adjective it shows a change that begins and keeps growing: 天气热起来了 'The weather is getting hot'. After 看, 吃 or 说 it gives your impression from one angle: 这个菜看起来很好吃 'This dish looks very tasty'; 说起来容易，做起来难 'It is easy to say but hard to do'.
- 下去 'keep on' carries an action on from now into the future: 请你说下去 'Please go on talking'; 不管多难，我们都要坚持下去 'However hard it is, we must keep going'.
- 下来 'settle down' is for something that quiets, slows or is fixed in place: 请你安静下来 'Please calm down'; 请把电话号码写下来 'Please write the phone number down'. 写下来 is to write something so that it stays; 写下去 is to keep writing.
- 上 after a verb shows that someone reaches a goal or starts a state: 他爱上了这座城市 'He fell in love with this city'; 她考上了大学 'She got into university'.
- (common mistake) 下来 and 下去 are not the same: 写下来 means 'write it down', 写下去 means 'keep writing'.
- (common mistake) 看起来, 吃起来 and 说起来 give an impression, and the verb stays right before 起来. The adjective comes after: 看起来很好吃.
- example: 天气热起来了。 / The weather is getting hot.
- example: 这个菜看起来很好吃。 / This dish looks very tasty.
- example: 请把电话号码写下来。 / Please write the phone number down.
- example: 她考上了大学。 / She got into university.

### Lesson 26: 有着 and 把…当作…: have, treat as
- 有着 is a formal 有 and goes with an abstract noun such as history, experience, effect or meaning: 这座城市有着很长的历史 'This city has a very long history'; 他有着丰富的经验 'He has rich experience'. 着 is read zhe. For a plain thing, say 有: 我有一本书, not 我有着一本书.
- 把 + noun + 当作 / 当成 / 看作 'treat as, regard as' says what someone takes something to be: 他把我当作朋友 'He treats me as a friend'; 我们把他看作自己的老师 'We regard him as our own teacher'. 变成 'turn into' can sit in the same pattern: 他们把这个地方变成了公园 'They turned this place into a park'. Longer forms such as 翻译成 are for a later level.
- (common mistake) 有着 is for abstract things. 我有着一本书 is wrong; say 我有一本书.
- (common mistake) In 把 … 当作 …, the thing that is treated comes right after 把. If you switch the two nouns, the meaning changes: 他把我当作朋友 and 他把朋友当作我 are not the same.
- example: 这座城市有着很长的历史。 / This city has a very long history.
- example: 他有着丰富的经验。 / He has rich experience.
- example: 他把我当作朋友。 / He treats me as a friend.
- example: 他们把这个地方变成了公园。 / They turned this place into a park.

### Lesson 27: 饭做好了 and 来了一位新同学: no 被, arrivals and losses
- Chinese does not need 被 when it is clear that the thing receives the action. 饭做好了 'The meal has been cooked'; 衣服洗干净了 'The clothes have been washed clean'; 问题解决了 'The problem has been solved'. The thing comes first, a verb with a result follows (好, 干净, 完), and nobody is named because it does not matter or is already clear.
- For arrivals and losses, a new thing comes after the verb: 我们班来了一位新同学 'A new classmate has joined our class'; 前面来了一辆车 'A car has come from ahead'. The place or group comes first, then 来了 or 丢了, then the thing, usually with a number and a measure word. A thing the listener already knows comes before the verb: 我的手机丢了 'My phone is lost'.
- (common mistake) Do not add 被 when the meaning is already clear. 饭做好了 is natural; 饭被做好了 sounds odd, as if someone suffered. 被 is for something that happened to someone, or when you name the doer.
- (common mistake) In 我们班来了一位新同学, the place comes first. 来了一位新同学我们班 is wrong.
- example: 饭做好了。 / The meal has been cooked.
- example: 问题解决了。 / The problem has been solved.
- example: 我们班来了一位新同学。 / A new classmate has joined our class.
- example: 我的手机丢了。 / My phone is lost.

### Lesson 28: Time, place, manner and serial verbs: word order before the verb
- Time, place and manner words all go before the verb, in this order: who, when, where, how, what. 我们明天下午在学校门口见面 'We will meet at the school gate tomorrow afternoon'; 他每天早上在公园里认真地跑步 'Every morning he runs seriously in the park'. A manner word ending in 地 sits right before the verb: 孩子们在院子里高兴地玩 'The children play happily in the yard'.
- Serial verbs put two or more verbs one after another, in the order they happen, with one subject and nothing between them. 我去商店买东西 'I go to the shop to buy things'; 他坐飞机去上海 'He goes to Shanghai by plane'; 他打开门走了出去 'He opened the door and walked out'. The first verb often shows the way or the means, and the last shows the aim. 了 goes after the verb that ends the chain.
- (common mistake) The place goes before the verb in Chinese: 我在公园里跑步, not 我跑步在公园里.
- (common mistake) Do not join serial verbs with 和: 我去商店买东西, not 我去商店和买东西.
- example: 我们明天下午在学校门口见面。 / We will meet at the school gate tomorrow afternoon.
- example: 他每天早上在公园里认真地跑步。 / Every morning he runs seriously in the park.
- example: 我去商店买东西。 / I go to the shop to buy things.
- example: 他坐飞机去上海。 / He goes to Shanghai by plane.

### Lesson 29: 跟…相比 and 比 + amount: compared with, three years older
- 跟 … 相比 'compared with …' puts the thing you compare against first, then says how the main subject differs: 跟去年相比，今年的雨多了 'Compared with last year, there is more rain this year'; 跟他相比，我的汉语差多了 'Compared with him, my Chinese is much worse'. 与 and 和 can replace 跟; 与 is the most formal.
- In a 比 sentence, the amount goes after the adjective to say by how much: 他比我大三岁 'He is three years older than me'; 这本书比那本贵很多 'This book is much more expensive than that one'; 我比他高一点 'I am a bit taller than him'; 这条路比那条长五公里 'This road is five kilometres longer than that one'. Do not put 很 before the adjective in a 比 sentence.
- (common mistake) 相比 needs 跟, 与 or 和 before the thing compared: 跟去年相比 is right; 去年相比 is wrong.
- (common mistake) Do not put 很 before the adjective in a 比 sentence: 他比我很大 is wrong. Put the amount after it: 他比我大三岁.
- example: 跟去年相比，今年的雨多了。 / Compared with last year, there is more rain this year.
- example: 跟他相比，我的汉语差多了。 / Compared with him, my Chinese is much worse.
- example: 他比我大三岁。 / He is three years older than me.
- example: 这本书比那本贵很多。 / This book is much more expensive than that one.

### Lesson 30: 朵, 幅, 颗, 匹, 扇, 册 and 届: new measure words
- Each noun has its own measure word, so learn the pair together. 朵 is for flowers and clouds: 一朵花 'a flower'. 幅 is for paintings, pictures and cloth: 一幅画 'a painting'. 颗 is for small round things such as stars, hearts and sweets: 一颗星星 'a star'. 匹 is for horses: 一匹马 'a horse'. 扇 is for doors and windows: 一扇门 'a door'.
- 册 is for volumes and copies of books: 第二册课本 'the second volume of the textbook'. 届 is for sessions, such as a competition, a meeting or a graduating class, and goes with 第 and a number: 第三届运动会 'the third sports meeting'. The sentence 墙上挂着一幅画 'A painting hangs on the wall' shows the usual order: place, verb, number, measure word, noun.
- (common mistake) Measure words are not interchangeable: a door is 一扇门, not 一朵门. Learn each noun with its measure word.
- (common mistake) 届 is only for sessions and classes (第三届), not for ordinary times: say 三次, not 三届, for 'three times'.
- example: 桌子上有一朵花。 / There is a flower on the table.
- example: 墙上挂着一幅画。 / A painting hangs on the wall.
- example: 他骑着一匹马。 / He is riding a horse.
- example: 那扇门是开着的。 / That door is open.

### Lesson 31: 性, 本, 之一 and 是否: -ness, this, one of, whether
- 性 after an adjective or a noun makes an abstract noun, like '-ness' or '-ity': 重要性 'importance', 安全性 'safety', 积极性 'enthusiasm', 可能性 'possibility'. 这件事的重要性不用多说 'The importance of this matter needs no more words'; 这个办法的可能性很小 'The chance of this method working is very small'.
- 本 'this, our' before a noun is formal and points to the speaker's own group or the current time: 欢迎来到本公司 'Welcome to our company'; 本月我们很忙 'This month we are very busy'.
- 之一 'one of' follows the group: 上海是中国最大的城市之一 'Shanghai is one of China's biggest cities'; 他是我最好的朋友之一 'He is one of my best friends'. The group before 之一 often has 最 or a number.
- 是否 'whether or not' is the formal form of 是不是 or 有没有 in an indirect question: 我不知道他是否同意 'I do not know whether he agrees'; 请告诉我你是否有空 'Please tell me whether you are free'. Do not add 吗 after it.
- (common mistake) 是否 already asks the question, so do not add 吗: 我不知道他是否同意 is right; 我不知道他是否同意吗 is wrong.
- (common mistake) 之一 comes after the whole group. Say 上海是中国最大的城市之一, not 上海是之一中国最大的城市.
- example: 这件事的重要性不用多说。 / The importance of this matter needs no more words.
- example: 欢迎来到本公司。 / Welcome to our company.
- example: 他是我最好的朋友之一。 / He is one of my best friends.
- example: 我不知道他是否同意。 / I do not know whether he agrees.

### Lesson 32: Words you know, with a new meaning
- Some words you already know come back in HSK 5 with another meaning or another sound. The character is the same; the words around it tell you which one it is.
- A new sound. 看 kān means 'to look after, to guard': 看家 'guard the house', 看门 'keep the door'; 看 kàn is 'to look'. 当 dàng means 'to treat as': 当作 'treat as', 上当 'be fooled'; 当 dāng is 'when, to be'. 倒 dǎo means 'to fall over': 摔倒 'fall down'; 倒 dào is 'to pour' or 'backwards'. 数 shǔ means 'to count': 数数 'count'; 数 shù is 'a number': 数字. 背 bèi means 'the back' and 'to recite': 背书 'recite a lesson', 背后 'behind'; 背 bēi is 'to carry on the back': 背书包. 好 hào means 'to like': 爱好 'hobby', 好奇 'curious'; 好 hǎo is 'good'. 行 háng means 'a row, a line of work': 行业 'industry', 银行 'bank'; 行 xíng is 'OK'. 量 liàng means 'amount': 数量 'quantity'; 量 liáng is 'to measure'. 为 wéi means 'to be, as': 成为 'become', 作为 'as'; 为 wèi is 'for'. 头 is neutral, tou, as a suffix: 木头 'wood', 石头 'stone', 里头 'inside'; on its own, 头 tóu is 'head'.
- The same sound, a new meaning. 才 cái also means 'talent': 人才 'talented person', 天才 'genius'. 本 běn means 'this, one's own': 本月 'this month', 本地 'local'. 别 bié also means 'to part': 告别 'say goodbye', 分别 'part'. 喂 wèi means 'to feed': 喂鱼 'feed the fish'. 省 shěng means 'to save': 省钱 'save money'. 面 miàn means 'surface, side': 水面 'the water surface', 方面 'aspect'.
- Five more come in pairs. 称 is chēng 'to weigh, to call' and chèn 'to match': 对称 'symmetrical'. 处 is chǔ 'to be in, to deal with' and chù 'a place, a point': 好处 'benefit', 到处 'everywhere'. 调 is diào 'to transfer, to investigate' and tiáo 'to adjust': 空调 'air conditioner'. 系 is jì 'to fasten' and xì 'a system, a department': 中文系 'the Chinese department'. 批 is pī 'a batch' and 'to approve'.
- (common mistake) The sound is decided by the words around it: 看家 is kān but 看书 is kàn; 摔倒 is dǎo but 倒水 is dào; 数数 is shǔ but 数字 is shù.
- (common mistake) 头 after 木, 石 or 里 is neutral: 木头 mùtou. On its own, or in 头发, it is tóu, 'head'.
- example: 我们家的狗会看家。 / Our dog can guard the house.
- example: 请你数数这里有几本书。 / Please count how many books there are here.
- example: 你要小心，别上当。 / You must be careful; do not be fooled.
- example: 他摔倒了。 / He fell down.
