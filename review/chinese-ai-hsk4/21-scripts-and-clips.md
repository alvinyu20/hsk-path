**只报告有问题的地方**，不要夸奖，也不要重复没问题的内容。对每个问题，请写：编号 / 原文 / 哪里不好（简单中文，再加一句英文）/ 更自然的说法（只用 HSK 1 到 HSK 4 的词）。
需要特别注意：1) 不自然或不地道的说法；2) 语法错误；3) 英文翻译和中文意思不一致；4) 书面语被当成口语教（或口语被当成书面语）；5) 对 HSK 4 来说太难的词；6) 多音字和轻声的拼音是否正确。
如果某一部分全部没问题，请回答“没有发现问题”。

Hello! I am building an HSK 4 study site (learners know the ~1,000 words of HSK 1 to 3 and are now learning the 990 new HSK 4 words, about 2,000 in all). Please act as a native speaker and Chinese teacher and check the items below.
**Report only the problems**, no praise and no repeating good items. For each problem give: number / the item / what is wrong (simple Chinese plus one line of English) / a more natural version (using only HSK 1 to 4 words).
Please watch for: 1) unnatural or non-idiomatic wording; 2) grammar mistakes; 3) English that does not match the Chinese; 4) written style taught as spoken (or the reverse); 5) words too hard for HSK 4; 6) wrong pinyin for words with more than one reading and for neutral tones.
If a part has no problems, answer "No problems found".

---

Also check: do the practice conversations sound like something people really say (a job interview, renting a flat, sending a parcel, saying sorry for being late, booking a table, a visa office, a coach, cheering up a friend)? Would a native speaker reply in these words? Are the "what to listen for" notes true?

## Practice scripts (8 conversations the learner reads aloud)
### Go to a job interview (Work, 5 turns)
- 对方：你好，请坐。你是来应聘的吗？  /  Hello, please sit down. Are you here to apply for the job?
- 学习者：是的，我想应聘这个工作。  /  Yes, I want to apply for this job.
- 对方：你有工作经验吗？  /  Do you have work experience?
- 学习者：我有两年的经验。  /  I have two years of experience.
- 对方：很好。你为什么想来我们公司？  /  Good. Why do you want to come to our company?
- 学习者：我对这个工作很有兴趣。  /  I am very interested in this work.
- 对方：好的，我们会通知你。你对工资有什么想法？  /  Okay, we will notify you. What do you think about the pay?
- 学习者：我希望工资不要太低。  /  I hope the pay is not too low.
- 对方：好的，谢谢你来面试。  /  Okay, thank you for coming to the interview.
### Rent a flat (Housing, 5 turns)
- 对方：你好，我是房东。你想租这个房子吗？  /  Hello, I am the landlord. Do you want to rent this flat?
- 学习者：对，我想租一年。  /  Yes, I want to rent it for a year.
- 对方：好的。房租一个月三千块。  /  Okay. The rent is 3,000 yuan a month.
- 学习者：房租可以便宜一点吗？  /  Can the rent be a little cheaper?
- 对方：可以，两千八百块怎么样？  /  Okay, how about 2,800 yuan?
- 学习者：好的。房子里有家具吗？  /  Okay. Is there furniture in the flat?
- 对方：有，床和桌子都有，还有冰箱。  /  Yes, there are a bed and a table, and also a fridge.
- 学习者：太好了，我什么时候可以搬进来？  /  Great, when can I move in?
- 对方：下个星期一就可以。  /  You can move in next Monday.
### Send a parcel (Post, 5 turns)
- 对方：你好，你要寄什么？  /  Hello, what do you want to send?
- 学习者：我想寄一个快递。  /  I want to send a parcel.
- 对方：请把地址写在这里。  /  Please write the address here.
- 学习者：好的，我写好了。  /  Okay, I have written it.
- 对方：这个有点重，三十块。  /  This is a bit heavy: 30 yuan.
- 学习者：能快一点到吗？  /  Can it arrive a bit faster?
- 对方：可以，明天就能到。  /  Yes, it can arrive tomorrow.
- 学习者：好的，请问我怎么付钱？  /  Okay, how do I pay?
- 对方：你可以用手机付钱。  /  You can pay with your phone.
### Say sorry for being late (Manners, 5 turns)
- 对方：你怎么现在才来？我们等了你半个小时。  /  Why have you only come now? We waited for you for half an hour.
- 学习者：对不起，路上现在堵车了。  /  Sorry, there is a traffic jam on the road now.
- 对方：你为什么没有提前给我打电话？  /  Why did you not call me in advance?
- 学习者：我的手机没电了，真抱歉。  /  My phone has no battery left. I am really sorry.
- 对方：好吧，这次我原谅你。  /  All right, I forgive you this time.
- 学习者：谢谢你的理解，下次我一定准时。  /  Thank you for understanding; next time I will certainly be on time.
- 对方：好，我们快点进去吧。  /  Okay, let us go in quickly.
- 学习者：对了，今天的活动几点开始？  /  By the way, what time does today's event begin?
- 对方：现在就要开始了，快进去吧。  /  It is about to begin now; hurry in.
### Book a table for a group (Food, 5 turns)
- 对方：你好，这里是餐厅。你要订座位吗？  /  Hello, this is the restaurant. Do you want to book seats?
- 学习者：对，我想订一些座位。  /  Yes, I want to book some seats.
- 对方：好的，几个人？什么时候来？  /  Okay, how many people? When are you coming?
- 学习者：十个人，星期六晚上六点半。  /  Ten people, Saturday evening at half past six.
- 对方：星期六晚上很多人聚餐，七点以后才有座位。  /  Many people have group meals on Saturday evenings; there are seats only after seven.
- 学习者：那就七点吧，你们可以打折吗？  /  Then seven it is; can you give a discount?
- 对方：可以，我们给你们打折。  /  Yes, we will give you a discount.
- 学习者：太好了，谢谢你！  /  Great, thank you!
- 对方：不客气，星期六见。  /  You are welcome. See you on Saturday.
### Ask about a visa (Travel, 5 turns)
- 对方：你好，请问你想办什么？  /  Hello, what do you want to do?
- 学习者：我想申请签证。  /  I want to apply for a visa.
- 对方：好的。请把护照和照片给我。  /  Okay. Please give me your passport and photo.
- 学习者：好的，这是我的护照。我还需要什么材料？  /  Okay, here is my passport. What other materials do I need?
- 对方：你还要在表格上写好你的地址。  /  You must also write your address on the form.
- 学习者：好的。签证要多长时间？  /  Okay. How long will the visa take?
- 对方：一般一个星期。通过以后，我们会给你打电话。  /  Usually one week. After it is approved, we will call you.
- 学习者：好的，非常感谢。  /  Okay, thank you very much.
- 对方：不客气，再见。  /  You are welcome. Goodbye.
### Talk to your coach (Sports, 5 turns)
- 对方：你好，我是你的教练。你想什么时候开始锻炼？  /  Hello, I am your coach. When do you want to start exercising?
- 学习者：我想从这个星期开始。  /  I want to start this week.
- 对方：好。你现在的体重是多少？  /  Good. What is your weight now?
- 学习者：我的体重是七十公斤。  /  My weight is 70 kilograms.
- 对方：你每天可以跑三十分钟，然后放松一下。  /  You can run for thirty minutes every day and then relax a bit.
- 学习者：我工作很忙，压力很大，会不会太累？  /  I am busy at work and under great pressure; will it be too tiring?
- 对方：不会的，运动可以让人放松。  /  No, it will not; exercise can help people relax.
- 学习者：好的，我会坚持的。  /  Okay, I will keep at it.
- 对方：太好了，加油！  /  Great, keep it up!
### Cheer up a friend (Friends, 5 turns)
- 对方：明天有考试，我很紧张。  /  There is an exam tomorrow, and I am very nervous.
- 学习者：别紧张，你一直很努力。  /  Do not be nervous; you have always worked hard.
- 对方：可是我还是担心，压力很大。  /  But I still worry, and the pressure is great.
- 学习者：你要有信心，你一定能考好。  /  You must have confidence; you can certainly do well.
- 对方：谢谢你的鼓励。我今天晚上应该怎么复习？  /  Thank you for your encouragement. How should I review this evening?
- 学习者：早点复习完，然后早点睡觉，放松一下。  /  Finish reviewing early, then go to bed early and relax a bit.
- 对方：好的，我听你的。考完以后一起吃饭吧。  /  Okay, I will listen to you. After the exam, let us eat together.
- 学习者：好啊，我请你吃饭，加油！  /  Great, I will invite you to a meal. Come on!
- 对方：谢谢你，我会努力的。  /  Thank you, I will work hard.

## Useful phrases
- 能不能请你再说一遍？  /  Could you please say it once more?
- 这个词在这里是什么意思？  /  What does this word mean here?
- 请你说得清楚一点，好吗？  /  Please speak a little more clearly, all right?
- 我不太了解这个词，可以解释一下吗？  /  I do not quite know this word; can you explain it?
- 你能给我举个例子吗？  /  Can you give me an example?
- 对不起，我没听懂，请再说一遍。  /  Sorry, I did not understand; please say it once more.
- 请你把它写下来，好吗？  /  Please write it down, all right?
- 我想问一下，你的意思是这个吗？  /  I want to ask: is this what you mean?

## Listening clips: the passage, the English and the "what to listen for" notes
- 他一直很努力，所以成绩提高得很快。  /  He has always worked hard, so his grades improve very quickly.
    note: 一直 yìzhí: 一 is said yì (falling) before the rising 直.
    note: 提高 tígāo: a rise, then a high flat tone.
- 我对这个城市的交通不太满意。  /  I am not very satisfied with the traffic in this city.
    note: 交通 jiāotōng: both syllables are high and flat.
    note: 不太 bú tài: 不 is said bú (rising) before a falling tone.
- 老师批评了他，但是也表扬了他的努力。  /  The teacher criticised him, but also praised his effort.
    note: 批评 pīpíng: a high tone, then a rise.
    note: 表扬 biǎoyáng: 表 only dips before the rising 扬.
- 这家餐厅的味道很特别，价格也不高。  /  The flavour at this restaurant is special, and the prices are not high either.
    note: 味道 wèidào: both syllables fall.
    note: 特别 tèbié: a fall, then a rise.
- 如果你有困难，可以给我打电话。  /  If you have a difficulty, you can call me.
    note: 困难 kùnnan: the second syllable is light and short.
    note: 打电话 dǎ diànhuà: a dip, then fall, fall.
- 他的汉语说得越来越流利了。  /  His Chinese is getting more and more fluent.
    note: 越来越 yuè lái yuè: fall, rise, fall.
    note: 流利 liúlì: a rise, then a fall.
- 为了保护环境，我们应该少用塑料。  /  To protect the environment, we should use less plastic.
    note: 保护 bǎohù: 保 only dips before the falling 护.
    note: 塑料 sùliào: both syllables fall.
- 经理让大家下午三点准时开会。  /  The manager asked everyone to have the meeting at three o'clock sharp this afternoon.
    note: 准时 zhǔnshí: 准 only dips before the rising 时.
    note: 开会 kāihuì: a high tone, then a fall.
- 昨天的演出非常精彩，观众们都笑了。  /  Yesterday's performance was wonderful, and the audience all laughed.
    note: 精彩 jīngcǎi: a high tone, then a dip.
    note: 演出 yǎnchū: 演 only dips before the high 出.
- 我的手机突然没电了，所以无法联系你。  /  My phone suddenly ran out of battery, so I could not contact you.
    note: 突然 tūrán: a high tone, then a rise.
    note: 无法 wúfǎ: a rise, then a dip.
- 这个城市的交通比以前方便多了。  /  The traffic in this city is much more convenient than before.
    note: 方便 fāngbiàn: a high tone, then a fall.
    note: 以前 yǐqián: 以 only dips before the rising 前.
- 他故意不说话，让大家有点担心。  /  He deliberately does not speak, which makes everyone a bit worried.
    note: 故意 gùyì: both syllables fall.
    note: 担心 dānxīn: both syllables are high and flat.
- 请把这些材料整理好，下午交给我。  /  Please sort these materials out and hand them to me this afternoon.
    note: 整理 zhěnglǐ: two third tones in a row, so 整 rises (say zhéngli).
    note: 材料 cáiliào: a rise, then a fall.
- 虽然他很累，但是仍然坚持工作。  /  Although he is tired, he still keeps on working.
    note: 仍然 réngrán: both syllables rise.
    note: 坚持 jiānchí: a high tone, then a rise.
- 我们要互相帮助，共同完成这个任务。  /  We must help each other and complete this task together.
    note: 互相 hùxiāng: a fall, then a high tone.
    note: 共同 gòngtóng: a fall, then a rise.
- 无论天气怎么样，我都要去跑步。  /  No matter what the weather is like, I will go running.
    note: 无论 wúlùn: a rise, then a fall.
    note: 怎么样 zěnmeyàng: 么 is light and short.
- 他不但会说汉语，而且会写汉字。  /  He can not only speak Chinese but also write characters.
    note: 不但 búdàn: 不 is said bú before the falling 但.
    note: 而且 érqiě: a rise, then a dip.
- 请问，附近有银行或者超市吗？  /  Excuse me, is there a bank or a supermarket nearby?
    note: 附近 fùjìn: both syllables fall.
    note: 或者 huòzhě: a fall, then a dip.
- 听说这部电影非常有名，我想去看看。  /  I hear this film is very famous, and I want to go and see it.
    note: 听说 tīngshuō: both syllables are high and flat.
    note: 有名 yǒumíng: 有 only dips before the rising 名.
- 周末我们打算去参观一家工厂。  /  This weekend we plan to visit a factory.
    note: 参观 cānguān: both syllables are high and flat.
    note: 打算 dǎsuàn: a dip, then a fall.
- 他被老师表扬了，心里特别高兴。  /  He was praised by the teacher and is especially happy.
    note: 被 bèi: a short fall that marks who does the action.
    note: 心里 xīnlǐ: a high tone, then a dip.
- 我从来没有见过这么大的雪。  /  I have never seen such heavy snow.
    note: 从来 cónglái: both syllables rise.
    note: 见过 jiàn guo: after a verb, 过 is light (guo).
- 这件事很重要，也很复杂。  /  This matter is very important and also very complicated.
    note: 重要 zhòngyào: both syllables fall.
    note: 复杂 fùzá: a fall, then a rise.
- 请把声音关小一点，大家都在休息。  /  Please turn the sound down a little; everyone is resting.
    note: 声音 shēngyīn: both syllables are high and flat.
    note: 休息 xiūxi: 息 is light and short.
