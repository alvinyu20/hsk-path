Lessons:

### Lesson 1: 连…也 / 都… and 甚至: even
- 连 … 也 / 都 … means 'even'. Put the surprising thing after 连, then 也 or 都 before the verb: 他连饭都没吃 'He did not even eat'. The thing after 连 is the most extreme case, one you would expect to be easy or obvious.
- Most sentences are negative: 我连他的名字也不知道 'I do not even know his name'. A positive one is fine when the surprise is how easy or how small the thing is: 这个字连小孩子都能写 'Even small children can write this character'.
- 甚至 'even, to the point of' adds a stronger case after a weaker one: 他很累，甚至在车上睡着了 'He is tired, he even fell asleep in the car'. It often comes before 连: 他甚至连自己的名字都忘了 'He even forgot his own name'.
- (common mistake) Put 也 or 都 after the thing, before the verb. 他连饭没吃 is incomplete; say 他连饭都没吃.
- (common mistake) Use 也 or 都, not both: 他连饭也都没吃 is wrong.
- example: 他连饭都没吃就走了。 / He left without even eating.
- example: 我连他的名字也不知道。 / I do not even know his name.
- example: 这个字很简单，连小孩子都能写。 / This character is easy; even small children can write it.
- example: 他很忙，甚至没有时间吃饭。 / He is very busy; he does not even have time to eat.

### Lesson 2: 即使…也… and 尽管…: even if, although
- 即使 … 也 … 'even if' says that the result stays the same whatever happens: 即使下雨，我也去 'Even if it rains, I will go'. 即使 gives a situation that may or may not happen, and 也 (or 还是) comes in the second part.
- 尽管 … 还是 / 但是 … 'although, in spite of the fact that' is for something that is true: 尽管很累，他还是去上班了 'Although he was tired, he still went to work'. Compare 即使 (maybe it happens) with 尽管 (it really is so).
- In the second part use 还是 'still' or 但是 'but'. 还是 is the more natural partner of 尽管. You can drop the English 'but', and Chinese does too: 尽管很贵，我还是买了 'Although it was dear, I still bought it'.
- (common mistake) 即使 is mostly for something that may or may not happen. For something that is a fact, 尽管 is the usual choice: 尽管下雨了，我们还是去了.
- (common mistake) Do not leave out 也 / 还是 in the second part. 即使下雨，我去 sounds unfinished.
- example: 即使明天下雨，我也要去。 / Even if it rains tomorrow, I will go.
- example: 即使你不喜欢，也要吃一点。 / Even if you do not like it, you must eat a little.
- example: 尽管天气很冷，他还是出去跑步了。 / Although the weather was cold, he still went out running.
- example: 尽管她很累，但是她不想休息。 / Although she is tired, she does not want to rest.

### Lesson 3: 不管 / 无论…都…: no matter
- 不管 and 无论 both mean 'no matter'. They are followed by a question word or by 'A or B', and then 都 (or 也) gives the result that does not change: 不管你去哪里，我都跟你去 'No matter where you go, I will go with you'.
- A pair like 好不好 or a list with 还是 works too: 不管天气好不好，他都去跑步 'Whether the weather is good or not, he goes running'; 不管是大的还是小的，我都喜欢 'Whether it is big or small, I like it'.
- 无论 is a little more formal than 不管. The second part nearly always has 都: 无论多忙，她都给妈妈打电话 'No matter how busy she is, she calls her mother'.
- (common mistake) Do not forget 都 in the result. 不管谁来，我们欢迎 is not complete; use 都 (or 也).
- (common mistake) 不管 is for something that can change: who, where, how much. For a fact, use 尽管.
- example: 不管你去哪里，我都跟你去。 / No matter where you go, I will go with you.
- example: 不管天气好不好，他都去跑步。 / Whether the weather is good or not, he goes running.
- example: 无论多忙，她都给妈妈打电话。 / No matter how busy she is, she calls her mother.
- example: 不管谁来，我们都欢迎。 / No matter who comes, we welcome them.

### Lesson 4: 要是…就… and 既然…就…: if, and since
- 要是 … 就 … means 'if', like 如果 but more spoken: 要是明天下雨，我们就不去 'If it rains tomorrow, we will not go'. Like 如果, 要是 can come before or after the subject: 要是你累了，就休息吧 or 你要是累了，就休息吧.
- The result part usually has 就. In speech 的话 can be added at the end of the first part: 你要是不想去的话，就别去 'If you do not want to go, then do not go'.
- 既然 … 就 … means 'since, now that'. It is for something that is already true or known: 既然你累了，就休息吧 'Since you are tired, rest'. 要是 imagines something; 既然 accepts a fact.
- (common mistake) Do not use 要是 for facts. 要是你已经来了 is odd, because you are already here. Use 既然.
- (common mistake) Keep 就 in the second part when you can: 既然你累了，你就应该休息 (就 is common, but not always needed).
- example: 要是你有时间，就来我家玩。 / If you have time, come and play at my home.
- example: 你要是不舒服，就去看医生吧。 / If you are not feeling well, go and see a doctor.
- example: 既然你累了，就早点休息吧。 / Since you are tired, rest early.
- example: 既然已经来了，就多坐一会儿吧。 / Since you have already come, sit a bit longer.

### Lesson 5: 否则, 由于…因此… and 于是: otherwise, because … so, and so
- 否则 means 'otherwise, or else'. It starts the second part and tells what will happen if the first part is not done or not true: 快一点走，否则我们会迟到 'Hurry up, or we will be late'. What follows 否则 is nearly always something you do not want.
- 由于 … 因此 … 'because of …, so …' is the written form of 因为…所以…: 由于天气不好，因此我们没有出去玩 'Because the weather was bad, we did not go out to play'. 由于 gives the reason; 因此 gives the result. In speech people usually say 因为…所以….
- 于是 'so, and then' links two things that happen one after the other, where the second is a reaction to the first: 他很饿，于是去买了一个面包 'He was hungry, so he went and bought a bread roll'. Unlike 所以, 于是 tells a story in order.
- (common mistake) 否则 starts the second part and is followed by something unwanted. Do not use it for a good result.
- (common mistake) 因此 needs a reason before it to point back to: 由于…，因此….
- example: 快一点走，否则我们会迟到。 / Hurry up, or we will be late.
- example: 你要多喝水，否则会不舒服。 / You must drink more water, or you will feel unwell.
- example: 由于天气不好，因此我们没有出去玩。 / Because the weather was bad, we did not go out to play.
- example: 他很饿，于是去买了一个面包。 / He was hungry, so he went and bought a bread roll.

### Lesson 6: 然而, 不过, 并且 and 而: however, and also
- 然而 and 不过 both mean 'however, but'. They start the second part and turn it against the first: 这个菜很便宜，不过不太好吃 'This dish is cheap, but not very tasty'. 然而 is more formal and is used in writing; 不过 is spoken and softer than 可是 or 但是.
- 并且 'and also, moreover' adds a second thing that is just as true: 他会说汉语，并且说得很好 'He can speak Chinese, and he speaks it well'. It joins verbs or short clauses and is more formal than 而且.
- 而 joins two parts that are in contrast, two sides of one picture: 他很高，而他的弟弟不太高 'He is tall, while his younger brother is not very tall'. It is often written and often has no English word of its own.
- (common mistake) Do not put 不过 or 然而 in the first part. They always start the part that contrasts.
- (common mistake) 并且 adds; it does not contrast. For a contrast use 而 or 但是.
- example: 这个菜很便宜，不过不太好吃。 / This dish is cheap, but not very tasty.
- example: 我很想去，不过今天没有时间。 / I very much want to go, but I have no time today.
- example: 这个地方很有名，然而去的人不多。 / This place is famous, but not many people go.
- example: 他会说汉语，并且说得很好。 / He can speak Chinese, and he speaks it well.

### Lesson 7: 既…又… and 不是…而是…: both … and, not … but
- 既…又… 'both … and' joins two qualities or two actions of the same thing: 这个手机既便宜又好用 'This phone is both cheap and easy to use'. It is more formal than 又…又… (HSK 3), and the two parts are usually the same kind and length.
- 不是 … 而是 … 'not … but …' corrects an idea. Put the wrong idea after 不是 and the true one after 而是: 我不是不想去，而是没有时间 'It is not that I do not want to go; I have no time'.
- The two parts match in kind: 不是你，而是他 'Not you, but him'; 不是今天，而是明天 'Not today, but tomorrow'.
- (common mistake) 既 needs 又 to go with it: 既便宜，好用 is wrong.
- (common mistake) 不是…而是… needs both parts. 他不是老师，医生 is not complete.
- example: 这个手机既便宜又好用。 / This phone is both cheap and easy to use.
- example: 她既聪明又努力。 / She is both clever and hard-working.
- example: 我不是不想去，而是没有时间。 / It is not that I do not want to go; I have no time.
- example: 他不是老师，而是医生。 / He is not a teacher but a doctor.

### Lesson 8: 首先…其次…, 另外, 例如 and 同时: listing and adding
- 首先 'first of all' and 其次 'second, next' list reasons or steps in order, and 最后 'finally' ends the list: 首先要多听，其次要多说，最后要多写 'First listen a lot, second speak a lot, last write a lot'. They help you organise what you say.
- 另外 'besides, also' and 此外 'in addition' add one more thing. 另外 is spoken and 此外 is formal. They start the new part, followed by a comma: 这个房间很大；另外，它离学校也很近 'This room is big; besides, it is close to the school'.
- 例如 and 比如 'for example' introduce examples: 我喜欢运动，例如游泳、跑步 'I like sports, for example swimming and running'. 同时 'at the same time' joins two things that happen together, or two points that are both true: 他在工作，同时也在学习汉语 'He is working and at the same time studying Chinese'.
- (common mistake) 其次 needs 首先 before it. Do not start a list with 其次.
- (common mistake) 例如 is followed by the examples themselves, not by a whole new idea.
- example: 首先要多听，其次要多说。 / First you must listen a lot; second you must speak a lot.
- example: 这个房间很大；另外，它离学校也很近。 / This room is big; besides, it is close to the school.
- example: 我喜欢运动，例如游泳、跑步。 / I like sports, for example swimming and running.
- example: 他在工作，同时也在学习汉语。 / He is working and at the same time studying Chinese.

### Lesson 9: 不得不, 来不及 and 赶快: no choice, too late, quickly
- 不得不 'have no choice but to' says you do something you would rather not do, because you must: 太晚了，我不得不回家 'It is too late; I have to go home'. 只好 means almost the same, 'can only, the only thing left is to': 没有车，我们只好走路 'There is no bus, so we can only walk'. Both show that there is no other way.
- 来得及 'there is still time' and 来不及 'there is no time, too late' can stand alone or follow a clause: 现在走还来得及 'If we leave now, there is still time'; 来不及了 'Too late!'. They look forward at time that is left or gone.
- 赶快 and 赶紧 both mean 'quickly, without delay'. They go before the verb. 赶快 is often used in commands: 赶快去吧 'Go quickly'. 赶紧 is used for things that happened too: 他赶紧给医生打了电话 'He quickly called the doctor'.
- (common mistake) 不得不 has two negatives but means 'have to'. Say 我不得不回家, not 我不得不要回家.
- example: 太晚了，我不得不回家。 / It is too late; I have to go home.
- example: 没有公共汽车，我们只好走路。 / There is no bus, so we have to walk.
- example: 我们现在走还来得及。 / If we leave now, there is still time.
- example: 你赶快去吧，别迟到。 / Go quickly, do not be late.

### Lesson 10: 难道…吗, 到底 and 究竟: asking with force
- 难道 … 吗 is a question that already has an answer in the speaker's mind. 难道 means 'could it be that …?' and shows surprise or doubt: 难道你不知道吗 'Do you really not know?'. It is not a real request for information.
- 到底 and 究竟 both mean 'on earth, exactly' in a question. They add force to the question and show you want a clear answer: 你到底想吃什么 'What on earth do you want to eat?'. 究竟 is a little more formal.
- 到底 also means 'in the end, after all' in a statement: 他到底还是来了 'He came after all'. Put 到底 and 究竟 before the verb or before the question word.
- (common mistake) 难道 … 吗 is not a plain question. Use it when you are surprised. For a real question, use 吗 alone.
- (common mistake) Do not use 到底 in a yes/no question with 吗: 你到底去吗 is odd. Use a question word or A-not-A: 你到底去不去.
- example: 难道你不知道吗？ / Do you really not know?
- example: 难道他没有告诉你吗？ / Has he really not told you?
- example: 你到底想吃什么？ / What on earth do you want to eat?
- example: 你究竟去不去？ / Are you going or not, once and for all?

### Lesson 11: 竟然, 原来 and 本来: surprise and discovery
- 竟然 'to one's surprise, actually' shows you did not expect what happened: 他竟然忘了自己的生日 'He actually forgot his own birthday'. It goes before the verb and comes with something the speaker finds hard to believe.
- 原来 'so it turns out' marks a discovery: you have just found out the truth. 原来你在这里 'So you are here!'; 原来他是老师 'So he is a teacher'. It usually starts the sentence.
- 本来 'originally, in the first place' tells what was planned or true before something changed: 我本来想去，可是下雨了 'I originally wanted to go, but it rained'.
- (common mistake) 竟然 is for surprise, not for a plain fact. If the thing is not surprising, do not use it.
- (common mistake) 原来 is about finding out. Do not use it for something you already knew.
- example: 他竟然忘了自己的生日。 / He actually forgot his own birthday.
- example: 这么简单的题，他竟然做错了。 / He actually got such an easy question wrong.
- example: 原来你在这里，我找了你很久。 / So you are here! I looked for you for a long time.
- example: 我本来想去，可是下雨了。 / I originally wanted to go, but it rained.

### Lesson 12: 确实, 实在, 完全 and 十分: really and completely
- 确实 'really, indeed' confirms that something is true: 这个办法确实很好 'This way really is good'. 实在 'truly' stresses a feeling or a judgement and is often used with 太 or 不: 我实在太累了 'I am truly too tired'.
- 完全 'completely, entirely' goes before a verb or adjective: 我完全同意 'I completely agree'. With 不 it means 'not at all': 我完全不知道 'I have no idea at all'.
- 十分 'very, fully' is like 非常 or 很 but a little more formal. It goes before an adjective or a feeling: 他十分高兴 'He is very happy'.
- (common mistake) 完全 and 十分 go before the verb or adjective, not after it. 我同意完全 is wrong.
- (common mistake) 确实 confirms what is true. Do not use it to ask a question; for "is it really so?" use 真的吗.
- example: 这个办法确实很好。 / This way really is good.
- example: 我实在太累了，想休息一下。 / I am really too tired and want to rest a bit.
- example: 我完全同意你说的。 / I completely agree with what you say.
- example: 他对这个工作十分满意。 / He is very satisfied with this job.

### Lesson 13: 从来, 并 and 往往: never, not at all, tends to
- 从来 + 不 / 没 means 'never, at no time': 我从来不喝酒 'I never drink alcohol'; 他从来没去过那个地方 'He has never been to that place'. 从来不 is a habit or a rule; 从来没 + verb + 过 is experience up to now. 从来 is mostly used with a negative; a positive use such as 他从来都很准时 also exists.
- 并 + 不 / 没 'not at all, actually not' corrects a wrong idea: 这个菜并不贵 'This dish is not expensive at all'; 他并没有生气 'He did not actually get angry'. 并 only goes with a negative.
- 往往 'often, tends to' describes what usually happens in the same situation. It is a pattern, not one event: 周末他往往在家休息 'At the weekend he tends to rest at home'. Use 常常 for plain frequency and 往往 for a general rule.
- (common mistake) 从来 and 并 need a negative. 我从来去过 is wrong; say 我从来没去过.
- (common mistake) 往往 describes what usually happens. It is not used for the future or for a command: 明天他往往来 is wrong.
- example: 我从来不喝酒。 / I never drink alcohol.
- example: 他从来没去过那个地方。 / He has never been to that place.
- example: 这个菜并不贵，只是有点辣。 / This dish is not expensive at all; it is just a little spicy.
- example: 周末他往往在家休息。 / At the weekend he tends to rest at home.

### Lesson 14: 偶尔, 不断, 老是 and 差点: how often, and nearly
- 偶尔 'now and then, occasionally' is for something that happens rarely: 我偶尔去看电影 'I go to the cinema now and then'. 不断 'again and again, without a break' says something goes on and on: 他不断地练习 'He keeps practising'. 老是 'always, all the time' is spoken and often complains: 他老是迟到 'He is always late'.
- 不断 is often followed by 地 and a verb. 老是 is close to 总是 (HSK 3), but 老是 usually shows that the speaker is not happy about it.
- 差点 'almost, nearly' says that something nearly happened, but did not: 我昨天差点迟到 'I was almost late yesterday' (and I was not). It goes right before the verb: 他差点忘了 'He almost forgot'.
- (common mistake) 偶尔 means rarely, so do not use it with a fixed number of times: 我偶尔去三次 is wrong.
- (common mistake) 差点 describes something that did not happen. 我差点迟到 means you were not late.
- example: 我偶尔去看电影。 / I go to the cinema now and then.
- example: 他不断地练习，所以汉语越来越好。 / He keeps practising, so his Chinese is getting better and better.
- example: 他老是迟到，老师很不高兴。 / He is always late, and the teacher is very unhappy.
- example: 我昨天差点迟到。 / I was almost late yesterday.

### Lesson 15: 至少, 左右, 大约, 尤其 and 其中: amounts and focus
- 至少 'at least' goes before an amount: 我每天至少睡七个小时 'I sleep at least seven hours every day'. 左右 'about' goes after a number: 他三十岁左右 'He is about thirty'. 大约 'about, roughly' goes before it: 大约三十个人 'about thirty people'. Do not use both in one phrase.
- 尤其 'especially' picks out the most important case: 我喜欢水果，尤其是苹果 'I like fruit, especially apples'. 尤其 is often followed by 是 and can point at a thing, a time or a quality.
- 其中 'among them, of which' points back to a group you have just named: 这里有十个人，其中三个是老师 'There are ten people here, and three of them are teachers'.
- (common mistake) 左右 goes after the number, 大约 before it. 左右三十 is wrong. Do not use 大约 and 左右 together.
- (common mistake) 其中 points back to a group you have already named. Do not use it at the start of a text.
- example: 我每天至少睡七个小时。 / I sleep at least seven hours every day.
- example: 他三十岁左右，是一个医生。 / He is about thirty and is a doctor.
- example: 我喜欢水果，尤其是苹果。 / I like fruit, especially apples.
- example: 这里有十个人，其中三个是老师。 / There are ten people here, and three of them are teachers.

### Lesson 16: 也许, 恐怕, 估计, 肯定, 千万 and 无法: guessing and warning
- 也许 'maybe, perhaps' and 估计 'I guess' show that you are not sure: 他也许在家 'He may be at home'; 我估计明天会下雨 'I guess it will rain tomorrow'. 恐怕 'I am afraid that' is a polite way to give bad news or a doubt: 恐怕我不能去 'I am afraid I cannot go'. 肯定 'definitely, certainly' shows you are sure: 他肯定知道 'He definitely knows'.
- 千万 'be sure to, on no account' gives a strong warning, mostly in commands: 千万别忘了 'Be sure not to forget'; 路上千万小心 'Be very careful on the road'.
- 无法 'unable to, there is no way to' is the formal form of 不能: 我无法回答这个问题 'I cannot answer this question'. It is followed by a verb and shows that there really is no way.
- (common mistake) 恐怕 gives bad news or a doubt. Do not use it for good news: 恐怕你会赢 sounds odd.
- (common mistake) 千万 is for warnings and commands, not for the past: 昨天我千万去了 is wrong.
- example: 他也许在家，给他打个电话吧。 / Perhaps he is at home; give him a call.
- example: 我估计明天会下雨。 / I guess it will rain tomorrow.
- example: 恐怕我不能去，对不起。 / I am afraid I cannot go; sorry.
- example: 路上千万小心。 / Be very careful on the road.

### Lesson 17: 仍然, 重新, 继续 and 最终: still, again, go on, in the end
- 仍然 and 仍 'still, yet' say that a situation has not changed: 他仍然住在那里 'He still lives there'. They are more formal than 还 and 还是 and go before the verb or the adjective.
- 重新 'anew, from the start' means to do something again in a new way: 我想重新开始 'I want to start again'. Unlike 再 or 又, 重新 means starting over. 继续 'to continue' picks up what was going on: 请继续说 'Please go on talking'.
- 最终 'in the end' tells the result after a long process: 他努力学习，最终通过了考试 'He studied hard and in the end passed the exam'. It is more formal than 最后 and is about the result, not about the last item in a list.
- (common mistake) 仍然 is for a situation that has stayed the same. For something that happens once more use 又 or 再.
- (common mistake) 重新 means starting over. For simply "one more time" use 再: 再说一遍.
- example: 他仍然住在那个小城市里。 / He still lives in that small city.
- example: 我想重新开始学习汉语。 / I want to start learning Chinese again from the beginning.
- example: 请继续说，我在听。 / Please go on; I am listening.
- example: 他努力学习，最终通过了考试。 / He studied hard and in the end passed the exam.

### Lesson 18: 稍微, 有点, 多么, 却 and 倒: a little, how, but
- 稍微 'slightly, a little' is for a small amount or change: 请稍微等一下 'Please wait a moment'; 今天稍微热了一点 'Today is a little hotter'. It usually goes with 一点 or 一下. 有点 'a bit' is also small, but the speaker finds it too much: 这个有点贵 'This is a bit dear'.
- 多么 'how …!' is used in exclamations about a quality: 她唱得多么好啊 'How well she sings!'. It is followed by an adjective, and 啊 adds feeling.
- 却 and 倒 both turn the meaning against what you would expect, and both go before the verb. 却 'but, yet': 他很聪明，却不努力 'He is clever, but he does not work hard'. 倒 'actually, contrary to expectation': 这个菜看起来一般，吃起来倒很好吃 'This dish looks ordinary, but it tastes very good'.
- (common mistake) 有点 often means "a bit too much", so it goes with unwelcome words: 有点贵, 有点累. It is not used with 很.
- (common mistake) 却 goes before the verb, not at the start of the sentence: 却他不努力 is wrong.
- example: 请稍微等一下，我马上来。 / Please wait a moment; I will come right away.
- example: 这件衣服有点贵。 / This piece of clothing is a bit dear.
- example: 她唱得多么好啊！ / How well she sings!
- example: 他很聪明，却不努力。 / He is clever, but he does not work hard.

### Lesson 19: 更加, 不如, 相同 and 同样: more, less, the same
- 更加 'even more' is like 更 (HSK 3) but a little more formal. It goes before adjectives and some verbs: 天气更加冷了 'The weather is even colder'; 他更加努力了 'He works even harder'. It compares with an earlier state.
- A 不如 B means 'A is not as good as B': 这个手机不如那个手机好用 'This phone is not as easy to use as that one'. An adjective can follow B: 我不如他高 'I am not as tall as he is'. Compare 没有 (HSK 2): 这个没有那个好. 不如 can also suggest a better choice, 'might as well': 不如去睡觉吧 'we may as well go to sleep'.
- 相同 'the same, identical' and 同样 'in the same way, equally' say there is no difference: 我们的想法相同 'Our ideas are the same'; 他们同样很高兴 'They are equally happy'. 同样 can also come before a noun with 的: 同样的问题 'the same question'.
- (common mistake) 不如 compares two things, so you need both A and B. 这个不如好 is incomplete; say 这个不如那个好.
- (common mistake) 相同 does not take 很: 很相同 is wrong. Say 完全相同 'exactly the same' or 不太相同 'not quite the same'.
- example: 天气更加冷了，多穿一点衣服吧。 / The weather is even colder now; wear a bit more clothing.
- example: 这个手机不如那个手机好用。 / This phone is not as easy to use as that one.
- example: 我们的想法完全相同。 / Our ideas are exactly the same.
- example: 他们对这件事同样感兴趣。 / They are equally interested in this matter.

### Lesson 20: 对于, 对…来说, 通过 and 随着: regarding, for, through, along with
- 对于 'regarding, as for' and 对 … 来说 'for, as far as … is concerned' name the person or topic that a judgement is about: 对于这个问题，我没有意见 'On this question, I have no opinion'; 对我来说，汉字很难 'For me, characters are hard'. 对…来说 is used with people; 对于 with topics.
- 通过 'through, by means of' tells the way you do something: 通过看电影，他学会了很多词 'By watching films, he has learned many words'. It also means 'to pass': 他通过了考试 'He passed the exam'.
- 随着 'along with, as' says that one thing changes together with another: 随着天气变冷，人们穿得越来越多 'As the weather gets cold, people wear more and more'. 随着 starts the sentence and is followed by a change.
- (common mistake) 对…来说 is for people: 对我来说, 对学生来说. Do not use it for a thing: 对天气来说 is wrong.
- (common mistake) 随着 needs a change after it. 随着天气 is not complete; say 随着天气变冷.
- example: 对于这个问题，我没有意见。 / On this question, I have no opinion.
- example: 对我来说，汉字很难。 / For me, characters are hard.
- example: 通过看电影，他学会了很多词。 / By watching films, he has learned many words.
- example: 随着天气变冷，人们穿得越来越多。 / As the weather gets cold, people wear more and more.

### Lesson 21: 按照, 作为, 与…相比 and 使: according to, as, compared with, make
- 按照 'according to, in line with' is followed by a rule, a plan or an order, and then the action: 按照老师说的做 'Do it the way the teacher said'. 按照 is about following something. 根据 (HSK 3) is about the evidence a judgement rests on. 作为 'as, in the role of' says what role someone has: 作为学生，他应该努力学习 'As a student, he should study hard'.
- 与 … 相比 'compared with …' compares two things. 与 is the written form of 和, and 和 … 相比 is the spoken one: 与去年相比，今年的雨多了 'Compared with last year, there is more rain this year'.
- 使 'to make, to cause' is followed by a person and then a feeling or a state: 这个消息使我很高兴 'This news makes me very happy'. The subject is a thing or an event, not a person who acts on purpose.
- (common mistake) 使 is followed by a person and then the result. For a person who makes someone do a job, use 让.
- (common mistake) 与…相比 needs both parts: 与去年相比, then what is different.
- example: 请按照老师说的做。 / Please do it the way the teacher says.
- example: 作为学生，他应该努力学习。 / As a student, he should study hard.
- example: 与去年相比，今年的雨多了。 / Compared with last year, there is more rain this year.
- example: 这个消息使我很高兴。 / This news makes me very happy.

### Lesson 22: 一…就… and 任何 / 随便: as soon as, any, as you like
- 一 … 就 … links two actions that follow each other closely: 我一到家就给你打电话 'I will call you as soon as I get home'. It also means 'whenever': 他一累就想睡觉 'Whenever he is tired, he wants to sleep'. Put 一 before the first verb and 就 before the second.
- The two verbs can have the same subject or different ones: 一下课，他就去打球 'As soon as class is over, he goes to play ball'.
- 任何 'any, whatever' is followed by a noun and then 都 or 也: 任何人都可以来 'Anyone can come'; 我没有任何问题 'I have no questions at all'. 随便 'as you like, casual' means there is no limit: 你随便坐 'Sit wherever you like'.
- (common mistake) Do not leave out 就 in the second part. 我一到家给你打电话 is not natural.
- (common mistake) 任何 needs 都 or 也 after the noun: 任何人可以来 is not complete.
- example: 我一到家就给你打电话。 / I will call you as soon as I get home.
- example: 他一累就想睡觉。 / Whenever he is tired, he wants to sleep.
- example: 任何人都可以来。 / Anyone can come.
- example: 你随便坐，别客气。 / Sit wherever you like; do not stand on ceremony.

### Lesson 23: 所有, 各, 一切, 许多, 大量, 部分, 全部 and 整个: all and many
- 所有 'all, every' comes before a noun and usually goes with 的 and 都: 所有的学生都来了 'All the students have come'. 各 'each, every one of' is used with a few nouns: 各国 'every country', 各位 'everyone'. 一切 'everything, all' stands for the whole of something: 一切都很好 'Everything is fine'.
- 许多 'many, a lot of' and 大量 'a large amount of' say that there is a lot. 许多 goes before nouns: 许多人 'many people'. 大量 is more formal and goes with amounts: 大量的时间 'a lot of time'.
- 部分 'part', 全部 'all of it' and 整个 'the whole' say how much of a thing: 我只看了一部分 'I only watched part of it'; 他吃了全部的菜 'He ate all the dishes'; 整个下午 'the whole afternoon'. 整个 takes one thing as a whole; 全部 is for the whole set.
- (common mistake) 所有 and 一切 usually need 都 in the sentence: 所有的学生来了 is not natural.
- (common mistake) 整个 takes one thing as a whole. For a whole set of things use 全部.
- example: 所有的学生都来了。 / All the students have come.
- example: 一切都很好，请放心。 / Everything is fine; please rest assured.
- example: 他有许多朋友。 / He has many friends.
- example: 我睡了整个下午。 / I slept the whole afternoon.

### Lesson 24: 有的…有的… and 互相 / 共同: some … others, each other
- 有的 … 有的 … 'some … others …' splits a group into parts: 有的人喜欢夏天，有的人喜欢冬天 'Some people like summer, others like winter'. 有些 'some' works in the same pattern. You can leave out the noun after the second 有的.
- 互相 'each other, mutually' goes before the verb: 我们互相帮助 'We help each other'; 朋友们互相学习 'Friends learn from each other'. 互相 already means 'each other', so no object is needed.
- 共同 'common, together' works as an adjective or an adverb: 我们有共同的爱好 'We have a common hobby'; 大家共同努力 'Everyone works hard together'. 共同 is more formal than 一起 and stresses sharing the same goal or thing.
- (common mistake) 有的 … 有的 … needs two or more parts. One 有的 alone does not make the pattern; say 有的…，有的….
- (common mistake) 互相 goes before the verb, not after: 我们帮助互相 is wrong.
- example: 有的人喜欢夏天，有的人喜欢冬天。 / Some people like summer, others like winter.
- example: 这些书，有的好，有的不好。 / Of these books, some are good and some are not.
- example: 我们互相帮助，一起学习汉语。 / We help each other and study Chinese together.
- example: 我们有共同的爱好。 / We have a common hobby.

### Lesson 25: 之前, 之后, 之间, 当时, 从此 and 接着: before, after, between, then
- 之前 'before' and 之后 'after' go after a time or an action: 三点之前 'before three o'clock'; 吃饭之前，先洗手 'Before eating, wash your hands first'; 下课之后 'after class'. They work like 以前 and 以后 (HSK 3) but are a little more formal.
- 之间 'between, among' goes after two things or two limits: 房子和学校之间 'between the house and the school'; 三点到五点之间 'between three and five'. 当时 'at that time, then' points back to a time already named: 我十岁的时候搬到这里，当时这里很小 'I moved here when I was ten; at that time it was small'.
- 从此 'from then on' says that after one event a new situation began: 他来到这里，从此没有离开 'He came here, and from then on has not left'. 接着 'next, then' follows the action just before it: 他先吃饭，接着看电视 'He eats first, then watches TV'.
- (common mistake) 之前 and 之后 come after the time or action: 之前吃饭 is wrong. Say 吃饭之前.
- (common mistake) 从此 mostly marks a turning point in the past. For a plan, 以后 is the safer word, although 从此我要努力学习 is also possible.
- example: 吃饭之前，先洗手。 / Before eating, wash your hands first.
- example: 下课之后，我们去吃饭。 / After class, we go and eat.
- example: 我十岁的时候搬到这里，当时这里很小。 / I moved here when I was ten; at that time it was small.
- example: 他先吃饭，接着看电视。 / He eats first, then watches TV.

### Lesson 26: 说明, 表示 and 成为: shows, says, becomes
- 说明 has two uses: 'to explain' (请你说明一下 'Please explain') and 'to show that' something is true (这件事说明他很努力 'This shows that he works hard'). With a clause after it, it is a conclusion drawn from facts.
- 表示 'to express, to show' says what someone shows by words or acts: 他点头表示同意 'He nods to show agreement'; 他表示不同意 'He says he does not agree'. The subject is a person, or an act that shows a feeling.
- 成为 'to become' is followed by a noun, a job or a role: 他成为了一名医生 'He became a doctor'. For adjectives use 变得: 天气变得很冷 'The weather has become cold'.
- (common mistake) 成为 is followed by a noun or a role, not an adjective: 他成为很高 is wrong. Say 他变得很高.
- (common mistake) 说明 for "explain" needs a thing to explain; 说明 for "show that" needs a clause after it.
- example: 这件事说明他很努力。 / This shows that he works hard.
- example: 请你说明一下这个问题。 / Please explain this problem.
- example: 他点头表示同意。 / He nods to show agreement.
- example: 他后来成为了一名医生。 / Later he became a doctor.

### Lesson 27: Words you know, with a new meaning
- Some words you already know come back in HSK 4 with another meaning or another sound. The character is the same; the words around it tell you which one it is.
- 空 kòng means 'free time, a gap': 有空 'to be free', 没空 'to have no time'. 空 kōng is 'empty': 空的 'empty'. 重 chóng means 'again': 重新 'anew', 重来 'do it again'. 重 zhòng is 'heavy'. 干 gān means 'dry': 衣服干了 'The clothes are dry'; 干净 'clean' is gān too. 干 gàn is 'to do': 干什么.
- 等 děng at the end of a list means 'and so on, etc.': 苹果、西瓜等水果 'fruits such as apples and watermelons'. 生 also means 'raw' and 'unfamiliar': 生的 'raw', 生词 'a new word'. 种 zhòng means 'to plant': 种花 'plant flowers'. 种 zhǒng is 'kind': 这种水果.
- 着 zháo means 'to succeed in': 睡着 'fall asleep', 找着 'find'. 着 zhe shows a state: 门开着. 为 wéi means 'to be, to act as': 成为 'become', 作为 'as'. 为 wèi is 'for': 为你高兴. 两 liǎng is also a Chinese unit of weight, 50 grams: 二两 'two liang'. 啊 ā at the start of a sentence is a cry of surprise: 啊！是你！ 'Ah! It is you!'
- (common mistake) The sound is decided by the words around: 你有空吗 is kòng, 杯子是空的 is kōng. 他睡着了 is zháo, 门开着 is zhe.
- (common mistake) 为 wèi 'for' goes before a person: 为你高兴. 成为 and 作为 use wéi.
- example: 你明天有空吗？ / Are you free tomorrow?
- example: 衣服干了，你可以穿了。 / The clothes are dry now; you can wear them.
- example: 我喜欢苹果、西瓜等水果。 / I like fruits such as apples and watermelons.
- example: 他在花园里种了很多花。 / He planted many flowers in the garden.
