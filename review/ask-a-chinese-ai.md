你好！我是一个正在学习汉语的美国成年人，我在做一个 HSK 1 学习网站，想请你以中国母语者和汉语老师的身份检查下面 13 个句子。
学习者的词汇只有 HSK 1（约 300 个最常用词），所以请尽量保持简单。

对每一个问题，请按这个格式回答：
1. 判断：自然 / 可以说但不太自然 / 不自然
2. 简短原因（用简单的中文，再加一句英文解释）
3. 如果不太自然，请给出更自然的说法（尽量只用 HSK 1 的词）

Hello! I am an adult American learning Mandarin and I am building an HSK 1 study site. Please act as a native speaker and Chinese teacher and check the 13 items below. Learners only know the ~300 most common words (HSK 1), so keep suggestions simple. For each item answer with: (1) verdict: natural / possible but not very natural / not natural; (2) a short reason (simple Chinese plus one line of English); (3) if it is not natural, a more natural version using HSK 1 words if possible.

**1.** 句子 / sentence: 他不在房间里，在房间外。
   问题 / question: Is 房间外 natural, or would you say 房间外边 / 外面? (localizer 外 straight after a noun)

**2.** 句子 / sentence: 商店在学校前。
   问题 / question: Is 学校前 natural here, or should this be 学校前面?

**3.** 句子 / sentence: 医院在学校后。
   问题 / question: Is 学校后 natural here, or should this be 学校后面?

**4.** 句子 / sentence: 猫在桌子下。
   问题 / question: Is 桌子下 natural, or 桌子下面?

**5.** 句子 / sentence: 我弟弟喜欢玩电脑。
   问题 / question: Is 玩电脑 natural for "play on the computer" (games, browsing), or is there a better verb?

**6.** 句子 / sentence: 你的电话是多少？
   问题 / question: Is 你的电话是多少 natural for "what's your phone number" without 号码?

**7.** 句子 / sentence: 中国很好玩儿。
   问题 / question: Is 好玩儿 natural for a country, or is it mostly for things like games and activities?

**8.** 句子 / sentence: 我想买书。
   问题 / question: Fine, but would a native speaker add a measure word or say 几本书 here?

**9.** 句子 / sentence: 这里有很多人。
   问题 / question: Neutral tone: 这里 zhèli (as written) or zhèlǐ? Same for 那里 and 哪里.

**10.** 句子 / sentence: 我哥哥是大学生。
   问题 / question: Fine? (checking family-term style: 我哥哥 vs 我的哥哥)

**11.** 句子 / sentence: 买些水果吧。
   问题 / question: Is 买些水果吧 natural as a standalone suggestion?

**12.** 句子 / sentence: 今天是五月一日。
   问题 / question: Is 一日 the right spoken form here, or 一号? (written/formal vs spoken)

**13.** 句子 / sentence: 今天下雪了。
   问题 / question: I mean "it has started snowing / it is snowing today". Is 今天下雪了 natural for that, and does it risk reading as "it snowed earlier today"?


最后，请再告诉我：这 13 个句子里，有没有你觉得最需要改的前三个？ / Finally: which three of these most need changing?
