### Pairs and opposites
- 左 / 右: Add 边 to each to say a side: 左边, 右边.
- 前面 / 后面: Both end in 面 (side): 前面 is in front, 后面 is behind.
- 里面 / 外面: Same 面 pattern: 里面 is inside, 外面 is outside.
- 上面 / 下面: Same 面 pattern again: 上面 is above, 下面 is below.
- 远 / 近: Both go with 离: 学校离我家很近, 机场离这里很远.
- 快 / 慢: Both describe speed: 很快, 很慢. 快 also means soon, as in 快要.
- 进 / 出: Add 来 or 去 for direction: 进来, 进去, 出来, 出去.
- 回来 / 回去: 来 means toward the speaker and 去 means away: 回来 come back, 回去 go back.
- 上来 / 下来: Both end in 来: moving toward the speaker, upward or downward.
- 男孩儿 / 女孩儿: Both contain 孩 (child): boy and girl.
- 丈夫 / 妻子: A married couple: 丈夫 is the husband, 妻子 is the wife.
- 爷爷 / 奶奶: Your father’s father and your father’s mother.
- 晴 / 阴: Both describe the sky: 晴天 is sunny, 阴天 is overcast.
- 红茶 / 绿茶: Chinese calls it 红茶 (red tea); English calls it black tea. 绿茶 is green tea.
- 记得 / 忘: 记得 is to remember and 忘 is to forget; 忘 is often followed by 了: 忘了.
- 开始 / 完: 开始 is to begin and 完 is to finish.
