### g2-35  …的时候 (when …)  (lesson: 多 questions, …的时候 and doubled verbs)

A153. **choose**. English: When it rains, I read at home.
   - right: 下雨的时候，我在家看书。  (Xiàyǔ de shíhou, wǒ zài jiā kàn shū.)
   - wrong: 下雨时候的，我在家看书。
   - note shown to the learner: 的时候 comes after the first part.
A154. **fill**. 他累的___，就睡觉。  (Tā lèi de shíhou, jiù shuìjiào.)
   - English: When he is tired, he sleeps.
   - blank: 时候
   - note shown to the learner: … 的时候 means when ….
A155. **build**. 他生病的时候，不想吃饭。  (Tā shēngbìng de shíhou, bù xiǎng chī fàn.)
   - English: When he is ill, he does not want to eat.
   - note shown to the learner: … 的时候 comes first.
A156. **spot**. sentence with the extra word: 我很吃饭的时候，不说话。
   - extra word: 很
   - correct sentence: 我吃饭的时候，不说话。  (Wǒ chī fàn de shíhou, bù shuōhuà.)
   - English: When I eat, I do not talk.
   - note shown to the learner: Do not put 很 before a verb here.

## C. Listening clips

Each clip is read aloud; the learner types what they hear. Two notes under it say what to listen for.

C1. 你的生日是几月几号？  (Nǐ de shēngrì shì jǐ yuè jǐ hào?)
   - English: What month and day is your birthday?
   - note 1: 生日 shēngrì: high and flat, then falling.
   - note 2: 几月 jǐ yuè and 几号 jǐ hào: 几 only dips before a falling tone.
C2. 我弟弟比我小两岁。  (Wǒ dìdi bǐ wǒ xiǎo liǎng suì.)
   - English: My younger brother is two years younger than me.
   - note 1: 两岁 liǎng suì: with a measure word, "two" is 两 liǎng, not 二 èr.
   - note 2: 弟弟 dìdi: the second syllable is light and short.
C3. 这件衣服很便宜，我想买两件。  (Zhè jiàn yīfu hěn piányi, wǒ xiǎng mǎi liǎng jiàn.)
   - English: This piece of clothing is cheap; I want to buy two.
   - note 1: 便宜 piányi: the last syllable is light and short.
   - note 2: 两件 liǎng jiàn: 两 is a third tone before a fall, so it only dips.
C4. 你喜欢看书还是看电视？  (Nǐ xǐhuan kàn shū háishi kàn diànshì?)
   - English: Do you like reading books or watching TV?
   - note 1: 还是 háishi: 还 is said hái here (or), and 是 is light.
   - note 2: 看书 kàn shū: a fall, then high and flat.
C5. 请问，我去火车站怎么走？  (Qǐngwèn, wǒ qù huǒchēzhàn zěnme zǒu?)
   - English: Excuse me, how do I get to the train station?
   - note 1: 怎么 zěnme: 么 is light and short.
   - note 2: 火车站 huǒchēzhàn: a dip, a high tone, then a fall.
C6. 我昨天晚上睡得很晚。  (Wǒ zuótiān wǎnshang shuì de hěn wǎn.)
   - English: I went to bed very late last night.
   - note 1: 晚上 wǎnshang: 上 is light and short.
   - note 2: 睡得很晚 shuì de hěn wǎn: 得 is light; 很晚 are two third tones, so 很 rises: hén wǎn.
C7. 我有一个哥哥和两个姐姐。  (Wǒ yǒu yí ge gēge hé liǎng ge jiějie.)
   - English: I have one older brother and two older sisters.
   - note 1: 一个 yí ge: 一 is said yí before the fourth tone, and 个 is light.
   - note 2: 两个 liǎng ge: 两 dips, and 个 is light.
C8. 他在商店买了一本书。  (Tā zài shāngdiàn mǎi le yì běn shū.)
   - English: He bought a book at the shop.
   - note 1: 一本 yì běn: 一 is said yì before the third tone 本.
   - note 2: 买了 mǎi le: 买 dips, and 了 is light.
C9. 外面下雨了，我们别出去了。  (Wàimiàn xiàyǔ le, wǒmen bié chūqù le.)
   - English: It has started to rain outside, so let us not go out.
   - note 1: 外面 wàimiàn: both syllables fall.
   - note 2: 别出去了 bié chūqù le: 别 rises, 出去 is high and flat then falling, and 了 is light.
C10. 你们几点下课？我们四点下课。  (Nǐmen jǐ diǎn xiàkè? Wǒmen sì diǎn xiàkè.)
   - English: What time do you finish class? We finish class at four.
   - note 1: 下课 xiàkè: both syllables fall.
   - note 2: 几点 jǐ diǎn: two third tones, so 几 rises: jí diǎn.
C11. 我家在医院旁边，离学校不远。  (Wǒ jiā zài yīyuàn pángbiān, lí xuéxiào bù yuǎn.)
   - English: My home is next to the hospital, and not far from the school.
   - note 1: 旁边 pángbiān: a rise, then high and flat.
   - note 2: 不远 bù yuǎn: 不 stays bù before a third tone.
C12. 他唱歌唱得很好听。  (Tā chàng gē chàng de hěn hǎotīng.)
   - English: He sings very nicely.
   - note 1: 唱歌 chàng gē: a fall, then high and flat.
   - note 2: 好听 hǎotīng: 好 only dips before the high 听.
C13. 我想吃一个面包，再喝一杯牛奶。  (Wǒ xiǎng chī yí ge miànbāo, zài hē yì bēi niúnǎi.)
   - English: I want to eat a piece of bread and then drink a glass of milk.
   - note 1: 一个 yí ge: 一 is said yí before the fourth tone, and 个 is light.
   - note 2: 一杯 yì bēi: 一 is said yì before the high tone 杯.
C14. 你明天想做什么？  (Nǐ míngtiān xiǎng zuò shénme?)
   - English: What do you want to do tomorrow?
   - note 1: 明天 míngtiān: a rise, then high and flat.
   - note 2: 做什么 zuò shénme: a fall, a rise, then a light 么.
C15. 他跑得比我快，但是我游得比他快。  (Tā pǎo de bǐ wǒ kuài, dànshì wǒ yóu de bǐ tā kuài.)
   - English: He runs faster than me, but I swim faster than him.
   - note 1: 比他 bǐ tā: 比 only dips before the high 他.
   - note 2: 游得 yóu de: a rise, then a light de.
C16. 我已经给你打了三次电话。  (Wǒ yǐjīng gěi nǐ dǎ le sān cì diànhuà.)
   - English: I have already called you three times.
   - note 1: 给你 gěi nǐ: two third tones in a row, so 给 rises: géi nǐ.
   - note 2: 三次 sān cì: high and flat, then falling.
C17. 你要多喝水，少喝咖啡。  (Nǐ yào duō hē shuǐ, shǎo hē kāfēi.)
   - English: You need to drink more water and less coffee.
   - note 1: 少喝 shǎo hē: 少 only dips before the high 喝.
   - note 2: 咖啡 kāfēi: both syllables are high and flat.
C18. 我觉得今天的菜很好吃。  (Wǒ juéde jīntiān de cài hěn hǎochī.)
   - English: I think the food today is delicious.
   - note 1: 觉得 juéde: 觉 is said jué here (to feel), a rise, and 得 is light.
   - note 2: 好吃 hǎochī: 好 only dips before the high 吃.
C19. 妈妈在做饭，爸爸在看电视。  (Māma zài zuòfàn, bàba zài kàn diànshì.)
   - English: Mom is cooking and Dad is watching TV.
   - note 1: 在 zài before a verb shows the action is going on: 在做饭, 在看电视.
   - note 2: 电视 diànshì: both syllables fall.
C20. 我的手表慢了五分钟。  (Wǒ de shǒubiǎo màn le wǔ fēnzhōng.)
   - English: My watch has gone five minutes slow.
   - note 1: 手表 shǒubiǎo: two third tones, so 手 rises: shóubiǎo.
   - note 2: 五分钟 wǔ fēnzhōng: 五 only dips before the high 分.
C21. 我们八点在学校门口见面。  (Wǒmen bā diǎn zài xuéxiào ménkǒu jiàn miàn.)
   - English: We will meet at the school gate at eight.
   - note 1: 见面 jiàn miàn: both syllables fall.
   - note 2: 学校 xuéxiào: a rise, then a fall.
C22. 你知道那个人是谁？  (Nǐ zhīdào nàge rén shì shéi?)
   - English: Do you know who that person is?
   - note 1: 知道 zhīdào: high and flat, then falling.
   - note 2: 谁 shéi: a rise.
C23. 我的朋友在中国学习汉语。  (Wǒ de péngyou zài Zhōngguó xuéxí Hànyǔ.)
   - English: My friend studies Chinese in China.
   - note 1: 中国 Zhōngguó: a high tone, then a rise.
   - note 2: 汉语 hànyǔ: a fall, then a dip.
C24. 他的眼睛很大，个子也很高。  (Tā de yǎnjing hěn dà, gèzi yě hěn gāo.)
   - English: His eyes are very big, and he is tall too.
   - note 1: 眼睛 yǎnjing: the last syllable is light and short.
   - note 2: 很大 hěn dà: 很 only dips before the falling 大.
