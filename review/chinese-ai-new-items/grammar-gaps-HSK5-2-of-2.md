## L5-5. 跟…相比, 比 + amount and 高我一头: compared with, three years older  (new section in an existing lesson)
New grammar points: A + adjective + B + amount (他高我一头)

**Explanation (new part):**
L5-5.E1. In speech the 比 can be left out when an amount follows: A + adjective + B + amount. 他高我一头 'He is a head taller than me'; 她大我三岁 'She is three years older than me'. It works with short adjectives such as 高, 大 and 早.

**Examples (new):**
L5-5.X1. 他高我一头。  (Tā gāo wǒ yì tóu.)  — He is a head taller than me.

**"Watch out" notes (new):**
L5-5.M1. Without 比 you need the amount: 她大我三岁, not 她大我.

## L5-6. Spoken patterns (3): 用不着, 不管怎么说, 真有你的, 不看白不看  (new lesson)
New grammar points: 用不着 (no need to); X着也是X着 (闲着也是闲着); 不管怎么说 (anyway); 真有你的 (you're amazing); 不X白不X (a waste not to)

**Explanation (new part):**
L5-6.E1. 用不着 means 'there is no need to': 这么小的病，用不着去医院 'It's such a small illness; no need to go to hospital'.
L5-6.E2. X着也是X着 means 'since I'm … anyway, I might as well': 闲着也是闲着，我来帮你吧 'I've got nothing else to do anyway, let me help you'.
L5-6.E3. 不管怎么说 means 'anyway, whatever you say': 不管怎么说，健康最重要 'Anyway, health matters most'.
L5-6.E4. 真有你的 praises or teases someone for something clever or surprising: 真有你的，这么难的题都做对了 'You're amazing: you even got such a hard question right'.
L5-6.E5. 不X白不X means 'it would be a waste not to': 票是免费的，不看白不看 'The tickets are free; it'd be a waste not to go'.

**Examples (new):**
L5-6.X1. 这么小的病，用不着去医院。  (Zhème xiǎo de bìng, yòngbuzháo qù yīyuàn.)  — It's such a small illness; no need to go to hospital.
L5-6.X2. 闲着也是闲着，我来帮你吧。  (Xián zhe yě shì xián zhe, wǒ lái bāng nǐ ba.)  — Since I'm free anyway, let me help you.
L5-6.X3. 不管怎么说，健康最重要。  (Bùguǎn zěnme shuō, jiànkāng zuì zhòngyào.)  — Anyway, health matters most.
L5-6.X4. 真有你的，这么难的题都做对了。  (Zhēn yǒu nǐ de, zhème nán de tí dōu zuò duì le.)  — You're amazing: you even got such a hard question right.
L5-6.X5. 票是免费的，不看白不看。  (Piào shì miǎnfèi de, bú kàn bái bú kàn.)  — The tickets are free; it'd be a waste not to go.

**"Watch out" notes (new):**
L5-6.M1. 用不着 is casual; in writing say 不必 or 不需要.

## L5-7. Spoken patterns (4): 打也打不得, 好是它坏也是它, 想来想去, 说什么就是什么  (new lesson)
New grammar points: X也X不得，Y也Y不得 (打也打不得); X是它，Y也是它 (好是它，坏也是它); X来X去，都是 / 还是… (想来想去); verb + 什么就是什么 (说什么就是什么)

**Explanation (new part):**
L5-7.E1. X也X不得，Y也Y不得 describes being stuck, with nothing you are allowed to do: 对这个孩子他真没办法，打也打不得，说也说不得 'He really can't handle this child: he can't hit him and he can't scold him'.
L5-7.E2. X是它，Y也是它 means 'whether X or Y, this is the one; there is no other choice': 好是它，坏也是它 'Good or bad, this is it'.
L5-7.E3. X来X去，都是 / 还是… means 'after all the …ing, it is still …': 想来想去，还是这个办法最好 'After thinking it over and over, this is still the best way'; 听来听去，都是一些差不多的意见 'After hearing them all, they are much the same ideas'.
L5-7.E4. Verb + 什么就是什么 means 'whatever someone says goes': 父母说什么就是什么 'Whatever the parents say goes'.

**Examples (new):**
L5-7.X1. 对这个孩子他真没办法，打也打不得，说也说不得。  (Duì zhège háizi tā zhēn méi bànfǎ, dǎ yě dǎ bù de, shuō yě shuō bù de.)  — He really can't handle this child: he mustn't hit him and he mustn't scold him.
L5-7.X2. 好是它，坏也是它。  (Hǎo shì tā, huài yě shì tā.)  — Good or bad, this is it.
L5-7.X3. 想来想去，还是这个办法最好。  (Xiǎng lái xiǎng qù, háishi zhège bànfǎ zuìhǎo.)  — After thinking it over and over, this is still the best way.
L5-7.X4. 父母说什么就是什么。  (Fùmǔ shuō shénme jiùshì shénme.)  — Whatever the parents say goes.

**"Watch out" notes (new):**
L5-7.M1. In X来X去 the same verb comes twice, 来 first: 想来想去.
