## L5-1. 哪怕…也…, 不是…还… and 不…不…  (new lesson)
New grammar points: 哪怕…，也… (even if); 不是…，还 / 还要… (not enough on its own); 不…不… (不尝不知道, 不见不散)

**Explanation (new part):**
L5-1.E1. 哪怕…，也… means 'even if, even though', and sounds stronger than 即使: 哪怕只有一天，我也想去 'Even if it's only one day, I want to go'; 哪怕再忙，我也会给你打电话 'No matter how busy I am, I will call you'.
L5-1.E2. 不是…，还 / 还要… says the first thing is not enough on its own: 不是光有目标就可以，还要行动起来 'Just having a goal is not enough; you also have to act'.
L5-1.E3. 不…不… is a short 'unless … not …': 不尝不知道 'You won't know unless you try it'; 这件事不做不行 'This has to be done'. 不见不散 'don't leave until we meet' is said when making a date.

**Examples (new):**
L5-1.X1. 哪怕只有一天，我也想去。  (Nǎpà zhǐyǒu yì tiān, wǒ yě xiǎng qù.)  — Even if it's only one day, I want to go.
L5-1.X2. 不是光有目标就可以，还要行动起来。  (Bú shì guāng yǒu mùbiāo jiù kěyǐ, hái yào xíngdòng qǐlái.)  — You can't just have a goal; you also have to act.
L5-1.X3. 不尝不知道，这个菜真好吃。  (Bù cháng bù zhīdào, zhège cài zhēn hǎochī.)  — You won't know unless you taste it: this dish is really good.
L5-1.X4. 明天八点，不见不散。  (Míngtiān bā diǎn, bújiàn bú sàn.)  — Tomorrow at eight; I'll wait until you come.

**"Watch out" notes (new):**
L5-1.M1. 哪怕 needs 也 (or 都) in the second part.
L5-1.M2. 不见不散 is a fixed phrase for meeting up.

## L5-2. More 被 and 把: 被他给洗坏了, 把书包一扔 and 墙上挂有  (new lesson)
New grammar points: 被 / 叫 / 让 … 给 + verb (被他给洗坏了); 把 + thing + 一 + verb (把书包一扔); 把 + thing 1 + verb + thing 2 (把钱都买了衣服); verb + 有 (墙上挂有一张地图)

**Explanation (new part):**
L5-2.E1. In speech, 给 can go right before the verb in a 被 / 叫 / 让 sentence. It adds no new meaning but sounds more spoken: 我新买的衣服被他给洗坏了 'My new clothes got ruined in the wash by him'.
L5-2.E2. 把 + thing + 一 + verb describes a sudden, quick action, usually followed by what happened next: 孩子把书包一扔，就跑出去玩了 'The child tossed his schoolbag down and ran out to play'.
L5-2.E3. 把 + thing 1 + verb + thing 2 says what thing 1 was spent on or used for: 他把钱都买了衣服 'He spent all the money on clothes'.
L5-2.E4. Verb + 有 is a written way to say something is on a place: 墙上挂有一张地图 'A map hangs on the wall'; 桌子上放有一些书 'Some books lie on the table'. In speech, use verb + 着: 墙上挂着一张地图.

**Examples (new):**
L5-2.X1. 我新买的衣服被他给洗坏了。  (Wǒ xīn mǎi de yīfu bèi tā gěi xǐ huài le.)  — My new clothes got ruined in the wash by him.
L5-2.X2. 孩子把书包一扔，就跑出去玩了。  (Háizi bǎ shūbāo yì rēng, jiù pǎo chūqù wán le.)  — The child tossed his schoolbag down and ran out to play.
L5-2.X3. 他把钱都买了衣服。  (Tā bǎ qián dōu mǎi le yīfu.)  — He spent all the money on clothes.
L5-2.X4. 墙上挂有一张地图。  (Qiáng shàng guà yǒu yì zhāng dìtú.)  — A map hangs on the wall.

**"Watch out" notes (new):**
L5-2.M1. 给 goes right before the verb: 被他给洗坏了.
L5-2.M2. Verb + 有 is written style; in speech say verb + 着.

**Lesson exercises (new):**
L5-2.Q1. **build** (put the words in order). 我的手机被他给弄坏了。  (Wǒ de shǒujī bèi tā gěi nòng huài le.)
   - English: My phone got broken by him.

   - right: 孩子把书包一扔，就跑了出去。  (Háizi bǎ shūbāo yì rēng, jiù pǎo le chūqù.)
   - wrong: 孩子把一书包扔，就跑了出去。
   - note shown to the learner: 一 goes right before the verb.
L5-2.Q3. **fill**. 他把钱都___了衣服。  (Tā bǎ qián dōu mǎi le yīfu.)

   - blank: 买
   - note shown to the learner: 把 + money + 买了 + what he bought.
L5-2.Q4. **choose**. Which sentence says (in written style): Some books lie on the table?

## L5-3. V得 + clause, 得了 / 不了 and 开不得: so … that, can or cannot, must not  (new section in an existing lesson)

**Explanation (new part):**

## L5-4. 有A有B, 没A没B, A的A B的B, X也不是Y也不是 and 一来…二来…  (new section in an existing lesson)
New grammar points: 没A没B (没日没夜); A的A，B的B (大的大，小的小)

**Explanation (new part):**
L5-4.E1. 没A没B means 'with neither, without any': 没日没夜 'day and night (without stopping)'; 没大没小 'with no respect for age'. 他没日没夜地工作 'He works day and night'.
L5-4.E2. A的A，B的B describes a mix, like 有的…有的…: 这些汉字写得大的大，小的小 'These characters are written some big, some small'; 孩子们哭的哭，笑的笑 'Some of the children are crying and some are laughing'.

**Examples (new):**
L5-4.X1. 他没日没夜地工作。  (Tā méi rì méi yè de gōngzuò.)  — He works day and night.
L5-4.X2. 这些汉字写得大的大，小的小。  (Zhèxiē hànzì xiě de dà de dà, xiǎo de xiǎo.)  — These characters are written some big, some small.

**"Watch out" notes (new):**
L5-4.M1. 没A没B uses 没 twice: 没日没夜.

**Lesson exercises (new):**
L5-4.Q1. **fill**. 他没日没___地工作。  (Tā méi rì méi yè de gōngzuò.)
   - English: He works day and night.
   - blank: 夜
   - note shown to the learner: 没日没夜: day and night.
L5-4.Q2. **choose**. Which sentence says: These characters are written some big, some small?
   - right: 这些汉字写得大的大，小的小。  (Zhèxiē hànzì xiě de dà de dà, xiǎo de xiǎo.)
   - wrong: 这些汉字写得大小的，大小的。
   - note shown to the learner: A的A，B的B.
