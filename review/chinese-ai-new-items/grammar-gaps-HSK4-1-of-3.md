## L4-1. Numbers: 三分之一, 百分之二十, 两倍, 三十来个 and 一脸  (new lesson)
New grammar points: fractions, percentages, decimals (三分之一, 百分之二十, 零点五); multiples with 倍 (两倍, 多了一倍); number + 来 (about: 三十来个); borrowed measure words and 一大碗, 一小杯 (一脸高兴)

**Explanation (new part):**
L4-1.E1. Fractions say the bottom number first: 三分之一 'one third', 四分之三 'three quarters'. Percentages use 百分之: 百分之二十 '20%'. Decimals use 点: 零点五 '0.5', 三点五 '3.5'.
L4-1.E2. 倍 means 'times': 他的收入是我的两倍 'His income is twice mine'. 多了一倍 means 'doubled': 今年的游客比去年多了一倍 'There are twice as many visitors this year as last year'.
L4-1.E3. A round number + 来 + measure word means 'about': 三十来个学生 'about thirty students'; 四十来岁 'about forty years old'.
L4-1.E4. Some nouns can be used as measure words with 一 to mean 'all over, a whole …': 一脸高兴 'happy all over his face'; 一屋子人 'a roomful of people'. An adjective can go between 一 and the measure word: 一大碗米饭 'a big bowl of rice'; 一小杯咖啡 'a small cup of coffee'.

**Examples (new):**
L4-1.X1. 三分之一的学生是外国人。  (Sān fēn zhīyī de xuésheng shì wàiguórén.)  — One third of the students are foreigners.
L4-1.X2. 他的收入是我的两倍。  (Tā de shōurù shì wǒ de liǎng bèi.)  — His income is twice mine.
L4-1.X3. 教室里有三十来个学生。  (Jiàoshì lǐ yǒu sānshí lái ge xuésheng.)  — There are about thirty students in the classroom.
L4-1.X4. 她一脸高兴地跑了进来。  (Tā yì liǎn gāoxìng de pǎo le jìnlái.)  — She ran in, beaming.

**"Watch out" notes (new):**
L4-1.M1. The bottom number comes first: 三分之一 is 1/3, not 3/1.
L4-1.M2. 来 meaning 'about' goes after a round number: 三十来个, not 三来十个.

## L4-2. More pairs: 不仅…还…, 一方面…另一方面…, 不是…就是…  (new lesson)
New grammar points: 不仅 / 不光…，还 / 而且… (not only … but also); 一方面…，另一方面… (on the one hand … on the other); 不是…，就是… (either … or)

**Explanation (new part):**
L4-2.E1. 不仅…，还 / 而且… means 'not only … but also', a little more formal than 不但…而且…: 我不仅会说汉语，还会写汉字 'I can not only speak Chinese but also write characters'. 不光 means the same and sounds casual.
L4-2.E2. 一方面…，另一方面… gives two sides or two reasons: 学汉语一方面有意思，另一方面很有用 'Learning Chinese is interesting on the one hand and useful on the other'.
L4-2.E3. 不是…，就是… means 'either … or' (one of the two, nothing else): 周末他不是在家，就是在公园 'At weekends he is either at home or in the park'. Compare 不是…而是…, which corrects: 他不是老师，而是学生 'He is not a teacher but a student'.

**Examples (new):**
L4-2.X1. 我不仅会说汉语，还会写汉字。  (Wǒ bùjǐn huì shuō Hànyǔ, hái huì xiě hànzì.)  — I can not only speak Chinese but also write characters.
L4-2.X2. 学汉语一方面有意思，另一方面很有用。  (Xué Hànyǔ yì fāngmiàn yǒuyìsi, lìng yì fāngmiàn hěn yǒuyòng.)  — Learning Chinese is interesting on the one hand and useful on the other.
L4-2.X3. 周末他不是在家，就是在公园。  (Zhōumò tā bú shì zài jiā, jiùshì zài gōngyuán.)  — At weekends he is either at home or in the park.

**"Watch out" notes (new):**
L4-2.M1. 不是…就是… gives two choices; 不是…而是… corrects a mistake.
L4-2.M2. The second half is 另一方面, with 另.

**Lesson exercises (new):**
L4-2.Q1. **build** (put the words in order). 我不仅会说汉语，还会写汉字。  (Wǒ bùjǐn huì shuō Hànyǔ, hái huì xiě hànzì.)
   - English: I can not only speak Chinese but also write characters.
   - note shown to the learner: 不仅 … 还 ….
L4-2.Q2. **fill**. 他___是我的同事，也是我的好朋友。  (Tā bùguāng shì wǒ de tóngshì, yě shì wǒ de hǎo péngyou.)

## L4-3. 叫 and 让 for the passive, and more 把 sentences  (new lesson)
New grammar points: passive with 叫 / 让 (自行车让朋友借走了); more 把 sentences (把衣服洗一洗, 把密码忘了, 把他批评了一顿)

**Explanation (new part):**
L4-3.E1. In speech, 叫 and 让 can work like 被: 我的自行车让朋友借走了 'My bike was borrowed by a friend'; 蛋糕叫弟弟吃了 'The cake was eaten by my little brother'. Unlike 被, they need the doer after them.
L4-3.E2. More 把 sentences: a doubled verb (你把衣服洗一洗 'Give your clothes a wash'); verb + 了 for something lost or forgotten (我把银行卡密码忘了 'I forgot my bank card PIN'); verb + 一顿 (妈妈把他批评了一顿 'Mom gave him a scolding'); and a direction before the verb (别把垃圾往地上扔 'Don't throw rubbish on the ground').

**Examples (new):**
L4-3.X1. 我的自行车让朋友借走了。  (Wǒ de zìxíngchē ràng péngyou jiè zǒu le.)  — My bike was borrowed by a friend.
L4-3.X2. 蛋糕叫弟弟吃了。  (Dàngāo jiào dìdi chī le.)  — The cake was eaten by my little brother.
L4-3.X3. 我把银行卡密码忘了。  (Wǒ bǎ yínhángkǎ mìmǎ wàng le.)  — I forgot my bank card PIN.
L4-3.X4. 别把垃圾往地上扔。  (Bié bǎ lājī wǎng dìshang rēng.)  — Don't throw rubbish on the ground.

**"Watch out" notes (new):**
L4-3.M1. 叫 / 让 in the passive need the doer: 自行车让朋友借走了, not 自行车让借走了.
L4-3.M2. With 把 the verb cannot stand alone: 我把密码忘了, not 我把密码忘.

**Lesson exercises (new):**

## L4-4. Saying it strongly: 累死了, 谁不…？, 没有…不… and 够…的  (new lesson)
New grammar points: adjective + 死了 / 得厉害 (累死了); question words for emphasis (谁不认识他？); double negatives for emphasis (没有人不喜欢); 够…的 (really, quite)

**Explanation (new part):**
L4-4.E1. Adjective or feeling + 死了 means 'terribly' in casual speech: 我累死了 'I'm dead tired'; 考试的时候，我紧张死了 'I was terribly nervous in the exam'. 得厉害 means 'badly, a lot': 他头疼得厉害 'His head hurts badly'.
L4-4.E2. A question with a question word can say the opposite, for emphasis: 谁不认识他？ 'Who doesn't know him?' (everyone does); 我怎么知道？ 'How would I know?' (I don't).
L4-4.E3. Two negatives make a strong yes: 没有人不喜欢放假 'There is nobody who doesn't like holidays' (everyone does); 我不能不去 'I can't not go' (I must).
L4-4.E4. 够…的 means 'really, quite' and is casual: 这个房间真够大的 'This room is really big'. Keep the 的 at the end.

**Examples (new):**
L4-4.X1. 我累死了。  (Wǒ lèi sǐ le.)  — I'm dead tired.
L4-4.X2. 谁不认识他？  (Shéi bú rènshi tā?)  — Who doesn't know him?
L4-4.X3. 没有人不喜欢放假。  (Méiyǒu rén bù xǐhuan fàngjià.)  — There is nobody who doesn't like holidays.
L4-4.X4. 这个房间真够大的。  (Zhège fángjiān zhēn gòu dà de.)  — This room is really big.

**"Watch out" notes (new):**
L4-4.M1. 死了 is casual; in polite or written Chinese use 非常 or 十分.
L4-4.M2. 够…的 needs the 的: 真够大的.

**Lesson exercises (new):**

   - note shown to the learner: Adjective + 死了: terribly.

   - wrong: 谁认识他？
