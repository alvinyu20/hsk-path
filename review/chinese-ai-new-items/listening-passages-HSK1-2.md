   - Would you like coffee or milk tea?
2. 女：我晚上不喝咖啡，喝了睡不着。  (Wǒ wǎnshang bù hē kāfēi, hē le shuì bù zháo.)
   - I do not drink coffee in the evening: if I drink it, I cannot fall asleep.
3. 男：那我们要一杯奶茶和一杯咖啡吧。  (Nà wǒmen yào yì bēi nǎichá hé yì bēi kāfēi ba.)
   - Then let's get a milk tea and a coffee.
4. 女：好。你还想吃什么？  (Hǎo. Nǐ hái xiǎng chī shénme?)
   - OK. What else would you like to eat?
5. 男：我要一个面包，我早上没吃饭。  (Wǒ yào yí ge miànbāo, wǒ zǎoshang méi chī fàn.)
   - I want a bread roll. I did not eat this morning.
