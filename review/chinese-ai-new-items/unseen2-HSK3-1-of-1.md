## More word examples

1. 太阳从西边下山了。  /  The sun has gone down in the west.
2. 今天晚上的月亮很大。  /  The moon is very big tonight.
3. 这个班级有三十个学生。  /  This class has thirty students.
4. 夏天，我喜欢在茶里加冰。  /  In summer I like to put ice in my tea.
5. 小心，别把手机丢了。  /  Be careful not to lose your phone.
6. 我想多练练汉字。  /  I want to practise my characters more.
7. 他喜欢很多种运动，比如跑步、游泳。  /  He likes a lot of sports, for example running and swimming.

## Grammar lessons and extra questions

8. 关于这个问题，我不太清楚。  /  I am not too sure about this question.
9. 根据这张地图，银行就在前面。  /  According to this map, the bank is just ahead.
10. 他向我笑了笑。  /  He smiled at me.

## Write-your-own model answers

11. 为了见我的朋友，我去了火车站。  /  In order to see my friend, I went to the station.
12. 请向你的家人问好。  /  Please say hello to your family for me.

## Listening passages

13. 对，过了红绿灯，银行就在路边，银行旁边有一个超市。  /  Yes. Once you have passed the traffic lights, the bank is right by the road, with a supermarket next to it.
