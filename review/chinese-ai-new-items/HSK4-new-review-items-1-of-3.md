# HSK 4: new review items (247 review questions, 118 write-your-own prompts, 24 listening clips)


## A. Grammar review questions

Each item belongs to one grammar point. Types: **choose** (pick the sentence that says the English; option 1 is the right one), **fill** (the blank is a word), **build** (put the words in order; the sentence is the answer), **spot** (tap the extra word; the sentence with the extra word is wrong).


### g4-1  连…也 / 都…  (lesson: 连…也 / 都… and 甚至: even)

A1. **choose**. English: He left without even drinking water.
   - right: 他连水都没喝就走了。  (Tā lián shuǐ dōu méi hē jiù zǒu le.)
   - wrong: 他连都水没喝就走了。
   - note shown to the learner: 都 comes after the thing that follows 连.
A2. **fill**. 这个问题连老师___不会。  (Zhège wèntí lián lǎoshī yě bú huì.)
   - English: Even the teacher cannot do this problem.
   - blank: 也
   - note shown to the learner: 连 + thing + 也 + verb.
A3. **build**. 我连今天几号都忘了。  (Wǒ lián jīntiān jǐ hào dōu wàng le.)
   - English: I even forgot what date it is today.
   - note shown to the learner: 连 + thing + 都 + verb.
A4. **spot**. sentence with the extra word: 他连一个字都也不认识。
   - extra word: 也
   - correct sentence: 他连一个字都不认识。  (Tā lián yí ge zì dōu bú rènshi.)
   - English: He does not even know one character.
   - note shown to the learner: Use 都 or 也 after the thing, not both.
