### g5-66  把 + noun + 当作 / 当成 / 看作 / 变成  (lesson: 有着 and 把…当作…: have, treat as)

A261. **choose**. English: We regard him as our older brother.
   - right: 我们把他当成哥哥。  (Wǒmen bǎ tā dàngchéng gēge.)
   - wrong: 我们把哥哥当成他。
   - note shown to the learner: The thing that is treated comes right after 把.
A262. **fill**. 他把书___朋友。  (Tā bǎ shū dàngchéng péngyou.)
   - English: He treats books as friends.
   - blank: 当成
   - note shown to the learner: 把 + thing + 当成 + what it is taken as.
A263. **build**. 我们把这里变成了公园。  (Wǒmen bǎ zhèlǐ biànchéng le gōngyuán.)
   - English: We turned this place into a park.
   - note shown to the learner: 把 + thing + 变成 + result.
A264. **spot**. sentence with the extra word: 她把这只狗是当作家人。
   - extra word: 是
   - correct sentence: 她把这只狗当作家人。  (Tā bǎ zhè zhī gǒu dàngzuò jiārén.)
   - English: She treats this dog as family.
   - note shown to the learner: Do not put 是 before 当作.

## B. "Write your own sentence" prompts

The learner sees the English, writes a Chinese sentence, and is checked for the required words (a+b: all of them; a|b: any one). The model answer is shown afterwards.


### g5-1  一旦…就…

B1. English: Once you finish the work, you can go home.
   - model answer: 一旦做完工作，你就可以回家。
   - required: 一旦+就

   - model answer: 一旦考试结束，我就休息。
   - required: 一旦+就
