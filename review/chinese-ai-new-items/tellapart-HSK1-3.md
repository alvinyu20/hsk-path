## T1-dou-ye. HSK 1: 都 or 也?  (options: 都 / 也)
**What each option means (shown when it is picked by mistake):**
- 都: 都 means 'all' or 'both'. It points back to a group that comes before it: 我们都…, 这些菜我都….
- 也: 也 means 'also' or 'too'. One more person or thing does the same as before.

**Cards** (the right answer is in 【 】):
T1-dou-ye.1. 我们【都】是学生。  (Wǒmen dōu shì xuésheng.)  — We are all students.
   - why: 都 sums up the group 我们: all of us.
T1-dou-ye.2. 他是学生，我【也】是学生。  (Tā shì xuésheng, wǒ yě shì xuésheng.)  — He is a student, and I am a student too.
   - why: 也: one more person, me, is the same.
T1-dou-ye.3. 他喜欢喝茶，我【也】喜欢。  (Tā xǐhuan hē chá, wǒ yě xǐhuan.)  — He likes tea, and so do I.
   - why: 也: I like it too.
T1-dou-ye.4. 这些菜我【都】喜欢。  (Zhèxiē cài wǒ dōu xǐhuan.)  — I like all of these dishes.
   - why: 都 points back to 这些菜: all of them.
T1-dou-ye.5. 你们【都】去吗？  (Nǐmen dōu qù ma?)  — Are you all going?
   - why: 都 covers everyone in 你们.
T1-dou-ye.6. 我们两个人【都】很忙。  (Wǒmen liǎng ge rén dōu hěn máng.)  — We are both busy.
   - why: 都 covers the two people: both.
T1-dou-ye.7. 你不知道？我【也】不知道。  (Nǐ bù zhīdào? Wǒ yě bù zhīdào.)  — You don't know? I don't know either.
   - why: 也 + 不 is 'not either'. 我都不知道 would mean 'I had no idea at all'.
T1-dou-ye.8. 爸爸妈妈【都】在家。  (Bàba māma dōu zài jiā.)  — Mum and Dad are both at home.
   - why: 都 covers the two people before it.
T1-dou-ye.9. 他会写汉字，【也】会说汉语。  (Tā huì xiě hànzì, yě huì shuō Hànyǔ.)  — He can write characters, and he can also speak Chinese.
   - why: 也: one more thing he can do.

## T1-bu-mei. HSK 1: 不 or 没?  (options: 不 / 没)
**What each option means (shown when it is picked by mistake):**
- 不: 不 says no to the present, the future, a habit, a wish or a quality. It goes before 是, 会, 想 and describing words.
- 没: 没 says something did not happen, or has not happened yet. It is always 没 before 有.

**Cards** (the right answer is in 【 】):
T1-bu-mei.1. 我昨天【没】去学校。  (Wǒ zuótiān méi qù xuéxiào.)  — I didn't go to school yesterday.
   - why: Yesterday: it did not happen, so 没.
T1-bu-mei.2. 我明天【不】去学校。  (Wǒ míngtiān bú qù xuéxiào.)  — I'm not going to school tomorrow.
   - why: Tomorrow, and it is your choice: 不.
T1-bu-mei.3. 他【不】喜欢喝茶。  (Tā bù xǐhuan hē chá.)  — He doesn't like tea.
   - why: A liking, true in general: 不.
T1-bu-mei.4. 我【没】有钱。  (Wǒ méiyǒu qián.)  — I don't have any money.
   - why: 有 always takes 没: 没有.
T1-bu-mei.5. 今天【不】冷。  (Jīntiān bù lěng.)  — It isn't cold today.
   - why: A describing word (冷) takes 不.
T1-bu-mei.6. 我【不】是老师。  (Wǒ bú shì lǎoshī.)  — I'm not a teacher.
   - why: 是 always takes 不: 不是.
T1-bu-mei.7. 你吃饭了吗？我还【没】吃。  (Nǐ chī fàn le ma? Wǒ hái méi chī.)  — Have you eaten? Not yet.
   - why: 还没 = not yet: it has not happened so far.
T1-bu-mei.8. 我【不】会说汉语。  (Wǒ bú huì shuō Hànyǔ.)  — I can't speak Chinese.
   - why: 会 takes 不: 不会.
T1-bu-mei.9. 我【不】想去。  (Wǒ bù xiǎng qù.)  — I don't want to go.
   - why: 想 takes 不: a wish, not an event.
T1-bu-mei.10. 上个星期我【没】看见他。  (Shàng ge xīngqī wǒ méi kànjiàn tā.)  — I didn't see him last week.
   - why: Last week, it did not happen: 没.

## T1-ma-ne-ba. HSK 1: 吗, 呢 or 吧?  (options: 吗 / 呢 / 吧)
**What each option means (shown when it is picked by mistake):**
- 吗: 吗 turns a statement into a plain yes/no question. Never with a question word like 什么 or 哪儿.
- 呢: 呢 asks 'and you?' or 'where is…?', and it can sit at the end of a question that already has a question word.
- 吧: 吧 makes a suggestion (let's…) or checks a guess (…, right?).

**Cards** (the right answer is in 【 】):
T1-ma-ne-ba.1. 你是中国人【吗】？  (Nǐ shì Zhōngguórén ma?)  — Are you Chinese?
   - why: A plain yes/no question: 吗.
T1-ma-ne-ba.2. 我很好，你【呢】？  (Wǒ hěn hǎo, nǐ ne?)  — I'm fine. And you?
   - why: 'And you?' is 你呢.
T1-ma-ne-ba.3. 我们去吃饭【吧】。  (Wǒmen qù chī fàn ba.)  — Let's go and eat.
   - why: A suggestion: 吧.
T1-ma-ne-ba.4. 你是老师【吧】？  (Nǐ shì lǎoshī ba?)  — You're a teacher, right?
   - why: Checking a guess: 吧. 你是老师吗 would be a plain question with no guess.
T1-ma-ne-ba.5. 你在做什么【呢】？  (Nǐ zài zuò shénme ne?)  — What are you doing?
   - why: The question already has 什么, so 吗 cannot go here; 呢 can.
T1-ma-ne-ba.6. 我的书【呢】？  (Wǒ de shū ne?)  — Where's my book?
   - why: Noun + 呢 asks where it is.
T1-ma-ne-ba.7. 你喝茶【吗】？  (Nǐ hē chá ma?)  — Do you drink tea?
   - why: A plain yes/no question: 吗.
T1-ma-ne-ba.8. 太晚了，明天再说【吧】。  (Tài wǎn le, míngtiān zài shuō ba.)  — It's too late. Let's talk about it tomorrow.
   - why: A suggestion: 吧.
T1-ma-ne-ba.9. 他去哪儿了【呢】？  (Tā qù nǎr le ne?)  — Where has he gone?
   - why: 哪儿 is already the question word, so 吗 cannot go here; 呢 makes it softer.

## T2-le-guo-zhe. HSK 2: 了, 过 or 着?  (options: 了 / 过 / 着)
**What each option means (shown when it is picked by mistake):**
- 了: 了 after a verb says the action happened and is done: 买了一本书 'bought a book'.
- 过: 过 says you have done it at some time in your life, as an experience: 去过北京 'have been to Beijing'.
- 着: 着 says a state goes on, or that one thing is done while doing another: 站着说话 'talk standing up'.

**Cards** (the right answer is in 【 】):
T2-le-guo-zhe.1. 我去【过】中国，那里很漂亮。  (Wǒ qù guo Zhōngguó, nàlǐ hěn piàoliang.)  — I've been to China. It's beautiful there.
   - why: Something you have done at some point in your life: 过.
T2-le-guo-zhe.2. 他笑【着】说：没问题。  (Tā xiào zhe shuō: méi wèntí.)  — He said with a smile: no problem.
   - why: Doing one thing (smiling) while doing another (speaking): 着.
T2-le-guo-zhe.3. 我昨天买【了】一本书。  (Wǒ zuótiān mǎi le yì běn shū.)  — I bought a book yesterday.
   - why: One action that happened and is done: 了.
T2-le-guo-zhe.4. 你吃【过】中国菜吗？  (Nǐ chī guo Zhōngguó cài ma?)  — Have you ever had Chinese food?
   - why: 'Ever' asks about an experience: 过.
T2-le-guo-zhe.5. 外面下【着】雨呢。  (Wàimiàn xià zhe yǔ ne.)  — It's raining outside.
   - why: Something going on right now: 着…呢.
T2-le-guo-zhe.6. 他们站【着】说话。  (Tāmen zhàn zhe shuōhuà.)  — They are talking standing up.
   - why: The way they talk (standing): 着.
T2-le-guo-zhe.7. 这个菜我还没吃【过】。  (Zhège cài wǒ hái méi chī guo.)  — I've never tried this dish.
   - why: Never yet, as an experience: 没…过.
T2-le-guo-zhe.8. 他喝【了】两杯咖啡就走了。  (Tā hē le liǎng bēi kāfēi jiù zǒu le.)  — He drank two cups of coffee and then left.
   - why: One finished action, then the next: 了.
T2-le-guo-zhe.9. 我们见【过】面吗？  (Wǒmen jiàn guo miàn ma?)  — Have we met before?
   - why: Have we ever met: an experience, 过.

## T2-de-de-de. HSK 2: 的, 地 or 得?  (options: 的 / 地 / 得)
**What each option means (shown when it is picked by mistake):**
- 的: 的 joins a describing word or an owner to a noun: 我的书, 红色的衣服.
- 地: 地 comes after a describing word and before a verb: how someone does it, 高兴地说.
- 得: 得 comes after a verb and says how well, fast or much it is done: 跑得很快.

**Cards** (the right answer is in 【 】):
T2-de-de-de.1. 这是我【的】书。  (Zhè shì wǒ de shū.)  — This is my book.
   - why: An owner + noun: 的.
T2-de-de-de.2. 他跑【得】很快。  (Tā pǎo de hěn kuài.)  — He runs fast.
   - why: Verb + 得 + how well: 得.
T2-de-de-de.3. 她高兴【地】说：谢谢！  (Tā gāoxìng de shuō: xièxie!)  — She said happily: thank you!
   - why: How she said it, before the verb: 地.
T2-de-de-de.4. 你说【得】对。  (Nǐ shuō de duì.)  — You're right. (What you said is right.)
   - why: Verb + 得 + a judgement of it: 得.
T2-de-de-de.5. 我们慢慢【地】走吧。  (Wǒmen màn màn de zǒu ba.)  — Let's walk slowly.
   - why: How we walk, before the verb: 地.
T2-de-de-de.6. 红色【的】那件是我的。  (Hóngsè de nà jiàn shì wǒ de.)  — The red one is mine.
   - why: A describing word + the thing: 的.
T2-de-de-de.7. 他汉语说【得】很好。  (Tā Hànyǔ shuō de hěn hǎo.)  — He speaks Chinese very well.
   - why: Verb + 得 + how well: 得.
T2-de-de-de.8. 这是妈妈做【的】菜。  (Zhè shì māma zuò de cài.)  — This is food Mum made.
   - why: 妈妈做 describes the noun 菜: 的.
T2-de-de-de.9. 孩子们快乐【地】玩着。  (Háizimen kuàilè de wán zhe.)  — The children are playing happily.
   - why: How they play, before the verb: 地.

## T2-bi-meiyou-zui. HSK 2: 比, 没有 or 最?  (options: 比 / 没有 / 最)
**What each option means (shown when it is picked by mistake):**
- 比: A 比 B + describing word: A is more … than B.
- 没有: A 没有 B + describing word: A is not as … as B.
- 最: 最 + describing word: the most … of all.

**Cards** (the right answer is in 【 】):
T2-bi-meiyou-zui.1. 哥哥【比】我大三岁。  (Gēge bǐ wǒ dà sān suì.)  — My older brother is three years older than me.
   - why: A is more than B, by an amount: 比.
T2-bi-meiyou-zui.2. 这个【没有】那个好看。  (Zhège méiyǒu nàge hǎokàn.)  — This one isn't as nice as that one.
   - why: Not as … as: 没有.
T2-bi-meiyou-zui.3. 他是我们班【最】高的。  (Tā shì wǒmen bān zuì gāo de.)  — He's the tallest in our class.
   - why: The most of all: 最.
T2-bi-meiyou-zui.4. 今天【比】昨天热多了。  (Jīntiān bǐ zuótiān rè duō le.)  — Today is much hotter than yesterday.
   - why: 比 + describing word + 多了 'much more'. 没有 cannot take 多了.
T2-bi-meiyou-zui.5. 我【没有】他那么忙。  (Wǒ méiyǒu tā nàme máng.)  — I'm not as busy as he is.
   - why: 没有 + B + 那么 + describing word: not as … as.
T2-bi-meiyou-zui.6. 这是我吃过的【最】好吃的菜。  (Zhè shì wǒ chī guo de zuì hǎochī de cài.)  — This is the best food I've ever eaten.
   - why: The most of all: 最.
T2-bi-meiyou-zui.7. 坐飞机【比】坐火车快。  (Zuò fēijī bǐ zuò huǒchē kuài.)  — Flying is faster than taking the train.
   - why: A is more … than B: 比.
T2-bi-meiyou-zui.8. 今天【没有】昨天冷。  (Jīntiān méiyǒu zuótiān lěng.)  — Today isn't as cold as yesterday.
   - why: Not as … as: 没有.
T2-bi-meiyou-zui.9. 你【最】喜欢什么颜色？  (Nǐ zuì xǐhuan shénme yánsè?)  — What colour do you like best?
   - why: Best of all: 最.

## T2-cong-li-wang. HSK 2: 从, 离 or 往?  (options: 从 / 离 / 往)
**What each option means (shown when it is picked by mistake):**
- 从: 从 is 'from': where or when something starts, often with 到 'to'.
- 离: 离 is the distance between two places or times: A 离 B 很近 / 很远 / 还有….
- 往: 往 is 'towards': the direction you go.

**Cards** (the right answer is in 【 】):
T2-cong-li-wang.1. 我家【离】学校很近。  (Wǒ jiā lí xuéxiào hěn jìn.)  — My home is close to the school.
   - why: Distance between two places: 离.
T2-cong-li-wang.2. 我【从】中国来。  (Wǒ cóng Zhōngguó lái.)  — I come from China.
   - why: Where it starts: 从.
T2-cong-li-wang.3. 【往】前走，就到了。  (Wǎng qián zǒu, jiù dào le.)  — Go straight ahead and you're there.
   - why: The direction you go: 往.
T2-cong-li-wang.4. 现在【离】考试还有三天。  (Xiànzài lí kǎoshì hái yǒu sān tiān.)  — There are three days left until the exam.
   - why: Distance in time: 离…还有.
T2-cong-li-wang.5. 他每天【从】早上八点工作到下午五点。  (Tā měitiān cóng zǎoshang bā diǎn gōngzuò dào xiàwǔ wǔ diǎn.)  — Every day he works from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m.
   - why: From … to …: 从…到….
T2-cong-li-wang.6. 【往】左看，那就是医院。  (Wǎng zuǒ kàn, nà jiù shì yīyuàn.)  — Look to the left: that's the hospital.
   - why: A direction: 往.
T2-cong-li-wang.7. 【从】这儿到火车站要多长时间？  (Cóng zhèr dào huǒchēzhàn yào duō cháng shíjiān?)  — How long does it take from here to the train station?
   - why: From here to there: 从…到….
T2-cong-li-wang.8. 你家【离】这儿远吗？  (Nǐ jiā lí zhèr yuǎn ma?)  — Is your home far from here?
   - why: Distance between two places: 离.
T2-cong-li-wang.9. 这个车【往】机场开。  (Zhège chē wǎng jīchǎng kāi.)  — This bus goes towards the airport.
   - why: The direction it goes: 往.

## T3-jiu-cai. HSK 3: 就 or 才?  (options: 就 / 才)
**What each option means (shown when it is picked by mistake):**
- 就: 就 says it happened early, quickly or easily: sooner than expected.
- 才: 才 says it happened late, slowly or with difficulty: later than expected. Before a number it can also mean 'only'.

**Cards** (the right answer is in 【 】):
T3-jiu-cai.1. 他八点【就】来了。  (Tā bā diǎn jiù lái le.)  — He came early, at eight.
   - why: Earlier than expected: 就 (and 了 at the end).
T3-jiu-cai.2. 他十点【才】来。  (Tā shí diǎn cái lái.)  — He didn't come until ten.
   - why: Later than expected: 才 (no 了).
T3-jiu-cai.3. 我看了三遍【才】看懂。  (Wǒ kàn le sān biàn cái kàn dǒng.)  — I only understood it after reading it three times.
   - why: It took a lot: 才.
T3-jiu-cai.4. 我看了一遍【就】懂了。  (Wǒ kàn le yí biàn jiù dǒng le.)  — I understood it after reading it once.
   - why: It was easy and quick: 就.
T3-jiu-cai.5. 他今年【才】十岁，个子已经很高了。  (Tā jīnnián cái shí suì, gèzi yǐjīng hěn gāo le.)  — He's only ten this year, but he's already tall.
   - why: Before a number, 'only': 才.
T3-jiu-cai.6. 你等一下，我马上【就】来。  (Nǐ děng yíxià, wǒ mǎshàng jiù lái.)  — Wait a moment, I'm coming right away.
   - why: Very soon: 马上就.
T3-jiu-cai.7. 你怎么现在【才】来？  (Nǐ zěnme xiànzài cái lái?)  — Why are you only getting here now?
   - why: Later than it should be: 才.
T3-jiu-cai.8. 早上五点他【就】起床了。  (Zǎoshang wǔ diǎn tā jiù qǐchuáng le.)  — He was already up at five in the morning.
   - why: Earlier than expected: 就…了.
T3-jiu-cai.9. 我等了一个小时，车【才】来。  (Wǒ děng le yí ge xiǎoshí, chē cái lái.)  — I waited an hour before the bus finally came.
   - why: It took a long time: 才.

## T3-zai-you. HSK 3: 再 or 又?  (options: 再 / 又)
**What each option means (shown when it is picked by mistake):**
- 再: 再 is 'again' for something that has not happened yet: next time, later, once more. It also means 'then' in 先…再….
- 又: 又 is 'again' for something that already happened again, or that will surely come round again (明天又是星期一).

**Cards** (the right answer is in 【 】):
T3-zai-you.1. 你明天【再】来吧。  (Nǐ míngtiān zài lái ba.)  — Come again tomorrow.
   - why: Again, but it has not happened yet: 再.
T3-zai-you.2. 他昨天来了，今天【又】来了。  (Tā zuótiān lái le, jīntiān yòu lái le.)  — He came yesterday, and he came again today.
   - why: It already happened again: 又.
T3-zai-you.3. 请你【再】说一遍。  (Qǐng nǐ zài shuō yí biàn.)  — Please say it once more.
   - why: Once more, from now on: 再.
T3-zai-you.4. 你怎么【又】迟到了？  (Nǐ zěnme yòu chídào le?)  — Why are you late again?
   - why: It has already happened again: 又.
T3-zai-you.5. 明天【又】是星期一了。  (Míngtiān yòu shì xīngqīyī le.)  — Tomorrow is Monday again.
   - why: Something that surely comes round again: 又, even about tomorrow.
T3-zai-you.6. 我们先吃饭，【再】去看电影。  (Wǒmen xiān chī fàn, zài qù kàn diànyǐng.)  — Let's eat first, then go to the cinema.
   - why: 先…再…: first … then ….
T3-zai-you.7. 这个菜很好吃，我想【再】吃一碗。  (Zhège cài hěn hǎochī, wǒ xiǎng zài chī yì wǎn.)  — This is delicious. I'd like another bowl.
   - why: Once more, not yet eaten: 再.
T3-zai-you.8. 他【又】忘了带手机。  (Tā yòu wàng le dài shǒujī.)  — He forgot his phone again.
   - why: It has happened again: 又.
T3-zai-you.9. 下了课【再】走吧。  (Xià le kè zài zǒu ba.)  — Let's leave after class.
   - why: Later, after something else: 再.

## T3-ba-bei. HSK 3: 把 or 被?  (options: 把 / 被)
**What each option means (shown when it is picked by mistake):**
- 把: 把: the doer comes first and does something to the thing: 我把作业写完了 'I finished my homework'.
- 被: 被: the thing comes first and something is done to it, often something bad: 杯子被打破了 'the cup got broken'.

**Cards** (the right answer is in 【 】):
T3-ba-bei.1. 我【把】作业写完了。  (Wǒ bǎ zuòyè xiě wán le.)  — I've finished my homework.
   - why: The doer (我) comes first and does something to the homework: 把.
T3-ba-bei.2. 我的自行车【被】人骑走了。  (Wǒ de zìxíngchē bèi rén qí zǒu le.)  — My bike was ridden off by someone.
   - why: The thing comes first and something is done to it: 被.
T3-ba-bei.3. 请你【把】门关上。  (Qǐng nǐ bǎ mén guān shàng.)  — Please close the door.
   - why: You do something to the door: 把.
T3-ba-bei.4. 那个蛋糕【被】弟弟吃了。  (Nàge dàngāo bèi dìdi chī le.)  — That cake was eaten by my little brother.
   - why: The cake comes first; it got eaten: 被.
T3-ba-bei.5. 妈妈【把】房间打扫得很干净。  (Māma bǎ fángjiān dǎsǎo de hěn gānjìng.)  — Mum cleaned the room until it was spotless.
   - why: The doer first, then what she did to the room: 把.
T3-ba-bei.6. 他的手机【被】拿走了。  (Tā de shǒujī bèi ná zǒu le.)  — His phone was taken.
   - why: The phone comes first and something bad happened to it: 被. The doer can be left out.
T3-ba-bei.7. 你【把】手机放在哪儿了？  (Nǐ bǎ shǒujī fàng zài nǎr le?)  — Where did you put your phone?
   - why: You did something to the phone (put it somewhere): 把.
T3-ba-bei.8. 那本书【被】他借走了。  (Nà běn shū bèi tā jiè zǒu le.)  — That book has been borrowed by him.
   - why: The book comes first; something was done to it: 被.
T3-ba-bei.9. 他【把】我的杯子拿走了。  (Tā bǎ wǒ de bēizi ná zǒu le.)  — He took my cup.
   - why: The doer first: 把.

## T3-huozhe-haishi. HSK 3: 或者 or 还是?  (options: 或者 / 还是)
**What each option means (shown when it is picked by mistake):**
- 或者: 或者 is 'or' in a statement: either one is fine.
- 还是: 还是 is 'or' in a question, including a question inside a sentence (我不知道…还是…).

**Cards** (the right answer is in 【 】):
T3-huozhe-haishi.1. 你喝茶【还是】咖啡？  (Nǐ hē chá háishi kāfēi?)  — Would you like tea or coffee?
   - why: A question asking you to choose: 还是.
T3-huozhe-haishi.2. 你可以明天【或者】后天来。  (Nǐ kěyǐ míngtiān huòzhě hòutiān lái.)  — You can come tomorrow or the day after.
   - why: A statement, either is fine: 或者.
T3-huozhe-haishi.3. 你想坐飞机【还是】坐火车？  (Nǐ xiǎng zuò fēijī háishi zuò huǒchē?)  — Do you want to fly or take the train?
   - why: A question: 还是.
T3-huozhe-haishi.4. 周末我常常看书【或者】看电影。  (Zhōumò wǒ chángcháng kàn shū huòzhě kàn diànyǐng.)  — At weekends I often read or watch a film.
   - why: A statement: 或者.
T3-huozhe-haishi.5. 我不知道他是老师【还是】学生。  (Wǒ bù zhīdào tā shì lǎoshī háishi xuésheng.)  — I don't know whether he's a teacher or a student.
   - why: A question inside a sentence (I don't know which): 还是.
T3-huozhe-haishi.6. 你可以给我打电话【或者】发电子邮件。  (Nǐ kěyǐ gěi wǒ dǎ diànhuà huòzhě fā diànzǐ yóujiàn.)  — You can phone me or send an email.
   - why: A statement, either way works: 或者.
T3-huozhe-haishi.7. 你是今天走【还是】明天走？  (Nǐ shì jīntiān zǒu háishi míngtiān zǒu?)  — Are you leaving today or tomorrow?
   - why: A question: 还是.
T3-huozhe-haishi.8. 星期六【或者】星期天我去看你。  (Xīngqīliù huòzhě xīngqītiān wǒ qù kàn nǐ.)  — I'll come and see you on Saturday or Sunday.
   - why: A statement: 或者.
T3-huozhe-haishi.9. 去不去，【还是】你自己决定吧。  (Qù bú qù, háishi nǐ zìjǐ juédìng ba.)  — Whether to go or not, you'd better decide yourself.
   - why: 还是 can also mean 'had better'; 或者 never does.

## T3-gang-gangcai. HSK 3: 刚 or 刚才?  (options: 刚 / 刚才)
**What each option means (shown when it is picked by mistake):**
- 刚: 刚 is an adverb, 'just': it sits right before the verb and can take a length of time after the verb (刚来两天).
- 刚才: 刚才 is a time word, 'a moment ago': it can come before the person, after 比, or before 不 / 没.

**Cards** (the right answer is in 【 】):
T3-gang-gangcai.1. 【刚才】你去哪儿了？  (Gāngcái nǐ qù nǎr le?)  — Where did you go just now?
   - why: Before the person (你), only the time word 刚才 can go.
T3-gang-gangcai.2. 我【刚】来两天，还不太习惯。  (Wǒ gāng lái liǎng tiān, hái bú tài xíguàn.)  — I only arrived two days ago, so I'm not used to it yet.
   - why: 刚 + verb + length of time: 刚来两天.
T3-gang-gangcai.3. 【刚才】我没听清楚，请再说一遍。  (Gāngcái wǒ méi tīng qīngchu, qǐng zài shuō yí biàn.)  — I didn't hear clearly just now. Please say it again.
   - why: Before 没, and at the start: 刚才.
T3-gang-gangcai.4. 他【刚】到家就下雨了。  (Tā gāng dào jiā jiù xiàyǔ le.)  — It started raining just as he got home.
   - why: 刚…就…: as soon as it had just happened.
T3-gang-gangcai.5. 现在比【刚才】好多了。  (Xiànzài bǐ gāngcái hǎoduō le.)  — It's much better now than a moment ago.
   - why: After 比 you need a noun-like time word: 刚才.
T3-gang-gangcai.6. 这件衣服是我【刚】买的。  (Zhè jiàn yīfu shì wǒ gāng mǎi de.)  — I just bought this jacket.  also accepted: 刚才
   - why: Right before the verb, 'just': 刚. 刚才 also works here ('a moment ago').
T3-gang-gangcai.7. 【刚才】的电话是谁打的？  (Gāngcái de diànhuà shì shéi dǎ de?)  — Who was that on the phone just now?
   - why: 刚才的 describes a noun; 刚 cannot.
T3-gang-gangcai.8. 我们【刚】认识一个月。  (Wǒmen gāng rènshi yí ge yuè.)  — We've only known each other for a month.
   - why: 刚 + verb + length of time.

## T3-zhiyao-zhiyou. HSK 3: 只要 or 只有?  (options: 只要 / 只有)
**What each option means (shown when it is picked by mistake):**
- 只要: 只要…就…: as long as this happens, that will follow. One easy condition is enough.
- 只有: 只有…才…: only if this happens can that follow. Nothing else will do. 只有 + person also means 'only'.

**Cards** (the right answer is in 【 】):
T3-zhiyao-zhiyou.1. 【只要】你努力，就一定能学好。  (Zhǐyào nǐ nǔlì, jiù yídìng néng xué hǎo.)  — As long as you work hard, you'll learn it well.
   - why: One condition is enough, with 就: 只要.
T3-zhiyao-zhiyou.2. 【只有】多练习，才能说得好。  (Zhǐyǒu duō liànxí, cái néng shuō de hǎo.)  — Only with lots of practice can you speak well.
   - why: The only way, with 才: 只有.
T3-zhiyao-zhiyou.3. 【只要】有时间，我就去看他。  (Zhǐyào yǒu shíjiān, wǒ jiù qù kàn tā.)  — Whenever I have time, I go to see him.
   - why: As long as: 只要…就….
T3-zhiyao-zhiyou.4. 这件事【只有】他知道。  (Zhè jiàn shì zhǐyǒu tā zhīdào.)  — Only he knows about this.
   - why: 只有 + person: only him.
T3-zhiyao-zhiyou.5. 【只要】不下雨，我们就去爬山。  (Zhǐyào bú xiàyǔ, wǒmen jiù qù pá shān.)  — As long as it doesn't rain, we'll go hiking.
   - why: As long as: 只要…就….
T3-zhiyao-zhiyou.6. 【只有】到了晚上，这里才安静。  (Zhǐyǒu dào le wǎnshang, zhèlǐ cái ānjìng.)  — Only at night is it quiet here.
   - why: Only then, with 才: 只有.
T3-zhiyao-zhiyou.7. 【只要】你喜欢，就拿去吧。  (Zhǐyào nǐ xǐhuan, jiù ná qù ba.)  — If you like it, take it.
   - why: As long as you like it: 只要…就….
T3-zhiyao-zhiyou.8. 【只有】你自己去，才能看清楚。  (Zhǐyǒu nǐ zìjǐ qù, cái néng kàn qīngchu.)  — Only if you go yourself will you see clearly.
   - why: The only way, with 才: 只有.
