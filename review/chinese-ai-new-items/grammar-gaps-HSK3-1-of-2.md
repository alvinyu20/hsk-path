## L3-1. How long: 学了两年, 打球打了一个下午 and 三年没见了  (new section in an existing lesson)
New grammar points: verb + object + verb again + time (打篮球打了一个下午); how long since (开学已经两个星期了)

**Explanation (new part):**
L3-1.E1. With an object, you can say the verb twice: verb + object + the verb again + 了 + time. 我们打篮球打了一个下午 'We played basketball all afternoon'; 他看书看了两个小时 'He read for two hours'. The other way you know also works: 他看了两个小时书.
L3-1.E2. To say how long it has been since something happened, put the time after the whole event and end with 了: 开学已经两个星期了 'It has been two weeks since school started'; 他来中国一年了 'He has been in China for a year'; 他们结婚十年了 'They have been married for ten years'.

**Examples (new):**
L3-1.X1. 我们打篮球打了一个下午。  (Wǒmen dǎ lánqiú dǎ le yí ge xiàwǔ.)  — We played basketball all afternoon.
L3-1.X2. 开学已经两个星期了。  (Kāixué yǐjīng liǎng ge xīngqī le.)  — It has been two weeks since school started.

**"Watch out" notes (new):**
L3-1.M1. The time cannot follow the object without the verb again: 他看书看了两个小时 or 他看了两个小时书, not 他看书了两个小时.

**Lesson exercises (new):**

## L3-2. 先…再…, 一边…一边…, 又…又… and 一会儿…一会儿…  (new section in an existing lesson)
New grammar points: 一会儿…，一会儿… (one moment … the next)

**Explanation (new part):**
L3-2.E1. 一会儿…，一会儿… means 'one moment … , the next …': two things that keep taking turns. 他一会儿进来，一会儿出去 'He keeps coming in and going out'; 天气一会儿冷，一会儿热 'The weather is cold one moment and hot the next'. Compare 一边…一边…, where both things happen at the same time.

**Examples (new):**
L3-2.X1. 天气一会儿冷，一会儿热。  (Tiānqì yíhuìr lěng, yíhuìr rè.)  — The weather is cold one moment and hot the next.

**"Watch out" notes (new):**
L3-2.M1. 一会儿…一会儿… takes turns; 一边…一边… happens at the same time. 一边吃饭一边看电视 'watch TV while eating'.

**Lesson exercises (new):**
L3-2.Q1. **build** (put the words in order). 他一会儿进来，一会儿出去。  (Tā yíhuìr jìnlái, yíhuìr chūqù.)
   - English: He keeps coming in and going out.
   - note shown to the learner: 一会儿 before each of the two actions that take turns.
L3-2.Q2. **choose**. Which sentence says: The child is laughing one moment and crying the next?
   - right: 孩子一会儿笑，一会儿哭。  (Háizi yíhuìr xiào, yíhuìr kū.)
   - wrong: 孩子一边笑，一边哭。
   - note shown to the learner: 一会儿…一会儿… takes turns; 一边…一边… would be both at once.

## L3-3. 一样, 不同, 不比 and more ways to compare  (new section in an existing lesson)
New grammar points: A 不比 B + adjective; 比 + 早 / 晚 / 多 / 少 + verb + amount

**Explanation (new part):**
L3-3.E1. A 不比 B + adjective means 'A is no more … than B' (they are about the same): 这张照片不比那张好看 'This photo is no better-looking than that one'. It answers someone who thinks A is better; it does not say A is worse. For 'A is not as … as B' use 没有: 我没有他高.
L3-3.E2. To say someone did something earlier, later, more or less, and by how much, put 早, 晚, 多 or 少 before the verb and the amount after it: 我比他早到十分钟 'I arrived ten minutes earlier than him'; 他比我多吃了一碗饭 'He ate one more bowl of rice than me'.

**Examples (new):**
L3-3.X1. 这张照片不比那张好看。  (Zhè zhāng zhàopiàn bù bǐ nà zhāng hǎokàn.)  — This photo is no better-looking than that one.
L3-3.X2. 我比他早到十分钟。  (Wǒ bǐ tā zǎo dào shí fēnzhōng.)  — I arrived ten minutes earlier than him.

**"Watch out" notes (new):**
L3-3.M1. 早 / 晚 / 多 / 少 go before the verb and the amount after it: 我比他早到十分钟, not 我比他到早十分钟.
L3-3.M2. 不比 is not 没有: 他不比我高 means he is not taller (we may be the same); 他没有我高 means he is shorter.

**Lesson exercises (new):**
L3-3.Q1. **choose**. Which sentence says: I arrived ten minutes earlier than him?
   - right: 我比他早到十分钟。  (Wǒ bǐ tā zǎo dào shí fēnzhōng.)

   - note shown to the learner: 早 goes before the verb, the amount after it.

   - English: This photo is no better-looking than that one.

## L3-4. Question words for "any" and "some": 谁都, 想吃什么就吃什么, 在哪儿见过  (new section in an existing lesson)
New grammar points: question words for "some-" (在哪儿见过她)

**Explanation (new part):**
L3-4.E1. A question word can also mean 'some-' when it is not the thing you ask about: 我好像在哪儿见过她 'I think I have seen her somewhere'; 我想给他买一点儿什么 'I want to buy him something'. In a question that ends with 吗, the question word means 'something, anywhere': 你想吃一点儿什么吗？ 'Would you like something to eat?'.

**Examples (new):**
L3-4.X1. 我好像在哪儿见过她。  (Wǒ hǎoxiàng zài nǎr jiàn guo tā.)  — I think I have seen her somewhere.
L3-4.X2. 你想吃一点儿什么吗？  (Nǐ xiǎng chī yìdiǎnr shénme ma?)  — Would you like something to eat?

**"Watch out" notes (new):**
L3-4.M1. With 吗 at the end, 什么 means 'something': 你想喝一点儿什么吗？ 'Would you like something to drink?'; without 吗 it is a real question: 你想喝什么？ 'What would you like to drink?'.

**Lesson exercises (new):**
L3-4.Q1. **build** (put the words in order). 我好像在哪儿见过她。  (Wǒ hǎoxiàng zài nǎr jiàn guo tā.)
   - English: I think I have seen her somewhere.
   - note shown to the learner: 哪儿 here means "somewhere"; it is not a question.
L3-4.Q2. **choose**. Which sentence asks: Would you like something to drink?
   - right: 你想喝一点儿什么吗？  (Nǐ xiǎng hē yìdiǎnr shénme ma?)
   - wrong: 你想喝什么？
   - note shown to the learner: With 吗, 什么 means "something". Without it, you ask "what?".

## L3-5. 起来, 出来, 下去, 向 and things that appear or leave  (new section in an existing lesson)
New grammar points: things appearing and leaving (来了一个新同学)

**Explanation (new part):**
L3-5.E1. To say that someone or something appears or leaves, put the place first, then the verb (with 了 or a direction), then the person or thing, usually with a number: 前边开过来一辆出租车 'A taxi came driving up ahead'; 树上飞走了三只鸟 'Three birds flew off the tree'; 我们班来了一个新同学 'A new classmate has joined our class'. What appears is new to the listener, so it comes last.

**Examples (new):**
L3-5.X1. 前边开过来一辆出租车。  (Qiánbian kāi guòlái yí liàng chūzūchē.)  — A taxi came driving up ahead.
L3-5.X2. 我们班来了一个新同学。  (Wǒmen bān lái le yí ge xīn tóngxué.)  — A new classmate has joined our class.

**"Watch out" notes (new):**

## L3-6. Showing what you think: 不是…吗？, 是…的 and …什么啊  (new lesson)
New grammar points: 不是…吗？ (isn't it …?); 是…的 for a firm view; …什么(啊) (难过什么啊)

**Explanation (new part):**
L3-6.E1. 不是…吗？ is not a real question. It reminds someone of something you both know, often with surprise: 你不是写完作业了吗？ 'Didn't you say you had finished your homework?'; 你不是会说汉语吗？ 'You can speak Chinese, can't you?'. The answer it expects is 'yes'.
L3-6.E2. 是…的 around a description makes it a firm view, like 'really is': 这个问题其实是很简单的 'This problem really is simple'; 他是会来的 'He will come, I am sure'.
L3-6.E3. A verb or adjective + 什么(啊) pushes back: 'what is there to …?'. 难过什么啊 'What is there to be sad about?'; 你着急什么啊？ 'What are you in such a hurry for?'. It is friendly between people who know each other, but can sound rude to strangers or older people.

**Examples (new):**
L3-6.X1. 你不是写完作业了吗？  (Nǐ bú shì xiě wán zuòyè le ma?)  — Didn't you say you had finished your homework?
L3-6.X2. 这个问题其实是很简单的。  (Zhège wèntí qíshí shì hěn jiǎndān de.)  — This problem really is simple.
L3-6.X3. 难过什么啊？你的成绩不是挺好的吗？  (Nánguò shénme a? Nǐ de chéngjì bú shì tǐng hǎo de ma?)  — What is there to be sad about? Your grades are pretty good, aren't they?
L3-6.X4. 你着急什么啊？  (Nǐ zháojí shénme a?)  — What are you in such a hurry for?

**"Watch out" notes (new):**
L3-6.M1. 不是…吗？ expects 'yes': 你不是去过中国吗？ means 'You have been to China, haven't you?'.
L3-6.M2. Use …什么啊 with friends; with strangers it can sound rude.

**Lesson exercises (new):**

## L3-7. Doubling: 个个, 一天一天 and 不大不小  (new lesson)
New grammar points: doubled measure words (个个都, 张张都); 一个一个, 一天一天 (one by one); 不A不B (不大不小)

**Explanation (new part):**
L3-7.E1. A measure word said twice means 'every single one': 这些苹果个个都很大 'Every one of these apples is big'; 他的照片张张都很好看 'Every one of his photos is good-looking'. It comes after the noun and usually goes with 都.
L3-7.E2. 一 + measure word + 一 + measure word means 'one by one, step by step', with 地 before the verb: 请一个一个地进来 'Please come in one at a time'; 我的汉语一天一天地好起来了 'My Chinese is getting better day by day'; 一点一点地 'little by little'.
L3-7.E3. 不A不B with two opposite adjectives means 'just right, neither too … nor too …': 这件衣服不大不小 'This top is neither too big nor too small'; 今天不冷不热 'Today is neither cold nor hot'.

**Examples (new):**
L3-7.X1. 这些苹果个个都很大。  (Zhèxiē píngguǒ gègè dōu hěn dà.)  — Every one of these apples is big.
L3-7.X2. 请一个一个地进来。  (Qǐng yí ge yí ge de jìnlái.)  — Please come in one at a time.
L3-7.X3. 我的汉语一天一天地好起来了。  (Wǒ de Hànyǔ yì tiān yì tiān de hǎo qǐlái le.)  — My Chinese is getting better day by day.
L3-7.X4. 这件衣服不大不小，特别合适。  (Zhè jiàn yīfu bú dà bù xiǎo, tèbié héshì.)  — This top is neither too big nor too small; it fits really well.

**"Watch out" notes (new):**
L3-7.M1. Keep 都 after a doubled measure word: 这些苹果个个都很大.
L3-7.M2. 不A不B needs two opposites: 不大不小, 不冷不热, 不早不晚.

**Lesson exercises (new):**
