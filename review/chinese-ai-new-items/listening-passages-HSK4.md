## P1. HSK 4 · The new neighbour (p4-neighbour)
A woman who has just moved in meets her neighbour.

1. 女：你好，我是新搬来的邻居，就住在你对面。  (Nǐ hǎo, wǒ shì xīn bān lái de línjū, jiù zhù zài nǐ duìmiàn.)
   - Hello, I am the new neighbour who has just moved in. I live right across from you.
2. 男：欢迎欢迎！我在这儿住了三年了，有什么需要可以找我。  (Huānyíng huānyíng! Wǒ zài zhèr zhù le sān nián le, yǒu shénme xūyào kěyǐ zhǎo wǒ.)
   - Welcome! I have lived here for three years. If you need anything, you can come to me.
3. 女：谢谢！请问附近哪儿有超市？  (Xièxie! Qǐngwèn fùjìn nǎr yǒu chāoshì?)
   - Thank you! Excuse me, where is there a supermarket nearby?
4. 男：出了小区往左走，大概五百米就有一个大超市，晚上十一点才关门。  (Chū le xiǎoqū wǎng zuǒ zǒu, dàgài wǔ bǎi mǐ jiù yǒu yí ge dà chāoshì, wǎnshang shíyī diǎn cái guān mén.)
   - Leave the estate and turn left; about 500 metres on there is a big supermarket. It does not close until eleven at night.
5. 女：太好了。还有，垃圾我应该放在哪儿？  (Tài hǎo le. Hái yǒu, lājī wǒ yīnggāi fàng zài nǎr?)
   - Great. Also, where should I put the rubbish?
6. 男：楼下有四个垃圾桶，要按颜色放。  (Lóuxià yǒu sì ge lājītǒng, yào àn yánsè fàng.)
   - There are four bins downstairs, and you have to sort it by colour.
7. 女：好的，我会注意的。以后有问题我再找你。  (Hǎo de, wǒ huì zhùyì de. Yǐhòu yǒu wèntí wǒ zài zhǎo nǐ.)
   - OK, I will be careful about it. If I have a problem later, I will come to you again.

   - options: ✓ 三年 / 两年 / 五年 / 半年
   - answer in line 2
