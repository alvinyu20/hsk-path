Hello! These are word groups from my HSK study site (HSK 3.0, levels 1 to 5). In each group the words used to have exactly the same English meaning, so learners could not tell them apart. Each word now has a distinguishing label (in brackets) and a usage note saying when to use which, with short examples.
Please act as a native speaker and Chinese teacher. **Report only the problems**, no praise. For each problem give: group number / the word / what is wrong (simple Chinese plus one line of English) / a more accurate version.
Please watch for: 1) a wrong distinction (a word called written, northern or spoken when it is not); 2) unnatural or wrong examples; 3) English that does not match the Chinese; 4) a more important difference that was missed; 5) words that are in fact interchangeable where the note says they are not, or the reverse. Chinese examples in a note may only use words at or below that word's level, so a lower-level word sometimes does not name its higher-level partner: that is on purpose.
If there are no problems, answer "No problems found".

## 41. but; however

- **可是** (kěshì, HSK 3): but; however (spoken)
  - Example: 我想去，可是没有时间。 / I want to go, but I do not have time.
- **不过** (búguò, HSK 4): but; however (softer)
  - Note: 不过 is a softer "but", often adding a small point: 很好吃，不过有点儿辣. 可是 and 但是 are stronger. 不过 can also mean "only": 不过十块钱.
  - Example: 这个菜很好吃，不过有点儿辣。 / This dish is tasty, but a little spicy.

## 42. city; town

- **城市** (chéngshì, HSK 3): city; town (the full word)
  - Example: 这个城市很大，也很漂亮。 / This city is big and also beautiful.
- **城** (chéng, HSK 4): city; town (in words: 城里, 长城)
  - Note: 城 is mostly used inside longer words: 城市, 城里, 长城.
  - Example: 他住在城里，每天坐地铁上班。 / He lives in town and takes the metro to work every day.

## 43. convenient

- **方便** (fāngbiàn, HSK 3): convenient (also: a good time for you)
  - Note: 方便 is also "a good time for you": 你现在方便吗? (Is now a good time?)
  - Example: 这里坐地铁很方便。 / It is convenient to take the subway here.
- **便利** (biànlì, HSK 5): convenient (formal)
  - Note: 便利 is a little more formal than 方便.
  - Example: 这里买东西很便利。 / Shopping here is very convenient.

## 44. extremely

- **极** (jí, HSK 3): extremely (after the adjective: 好极了)
  - Note: 极 comes after the adjective with 了: 好极了, 累极了.
  - Example: 这个办法好极了。 / This idea is extremely good.
- **极其** (jíqí, HSK 5): extremely (formal, before the adjective)
  - Note: 极其 is a more formal "extremely", used before an adjective: 极其重要, 极其困难. 非常 is the everyday word.
  - Example: 这件事极其重要。 / This matter is extremely important.

## 45. famous

- **有名** (yǒumíng, HSK 3): famous (everyday: 很有名)
  - Example: 这个饭馆很有名。 / This restaurant is very famous.
- **著名** (zhùmíng, HSK 4): famous (formal, before a noun)
  - Note: 著名 is formal and usually comes before a noun: 著名作家 (a famous writer). 有名 is the everyday word and often follows 很: 这个饭馆很有名.
  - Example: 这是一位著名的作家。 / This is a famous writer.

## 46. for example

- **比如** (bǐrú, HSK 3): for example (spoken)
  - Example: 我喜欢运动，比如游泳、打篮球。 / I like sports, for example swimming and playing basketball.
- **例如** (lìrú, HSK 4): for example (more written)
  - Note: 例如 and 比如 mean the same. 例如 is a little more formal and common in writing; 比如 is more common in speech.
  - Example: 我喜欢水果，例如苹果和香蕉。 / I like fruit, for example apples and bananas.

## 47. if

- **如果** (rúguǒ, HSK 3): if (standard)
  - Example: 如果明天下雨，我们就不去了。 / If it rains tomorrow, we won't go.
- **要是** (yàoshi, HSK 4): if (spoken)
  - Note: 要是 is read yàoshi, with a light second syllable. It is spoken Chinese for "if" and is usually followed by 就.
  - Example: 要是下雨，我们就不去了。 / If it rains, we will no longer go.

## 48. kilogram

- **公斤** (gōngjīn, HSK 3): kilogram (everyday)
  - Example: 这个西瓜有三公斤。 / This watermelon weighs three kilograms.
- **千克** (qiānkè, HSK 4): kilogram (on labels, in science)
  - Note: 千克 is the official name of the unit, used on labels and in science. In daily life people say 公斤.
  - Example: 这个包重五千克。 / This bag weighs 5 kilograms.

## 49. kind; type

- **种** (zhǒng, HSK 3): kind; type (measure word: 这种)
  - Note: 种 is a measure word for kinds: 这种水果 (this kind of fruit), 两种 (two kinds).
  - Example: 这种水果很好吃。 / This kind of fruit is delicious.
- **类** (lèi, HSK 5): kind; type (a category)
  - Note: 类 is a kind or category: 这类 (this kind), 分类 (to sort into categories). As a measure word it counts kinds: 两类人.
  - Example: 这两类书都很有意思。 / These two kinds of books are both very interesting.
- **种类** (zhǒnglèi, HSK 5): kind; type (noun: 种类很多)
  - Note: 种类 is the kind or type: 种类多 (many kinds), 种类不同 (different types), 水果的种类 (kinds of fruit).
  - Example: 这家店的蛋糕种类很多。 / This shop has many kinds of cakes.

## 50. mouth

- **嘴** (zuǐ, HSK 3): mouth (everyday: 嘴里)
  - Example: 他的嘴里有一块糖。 / He has a piece of candy in his mouth.
- **嘴巴** (zuǐba, HSK 5): mouth (spoken, casual)
  - Note: 嘴巴 is a casual, spoken word for the mouth: 张大嘴巴 (to open the mouth wide). 嘴 is the usual word and is used in more phrases: 嘴里 (in the mouth).
  - Example: 孩子的嘴巴很小。 / The child's mouth is very small.

## 51. not only

- **不但** (búdàn, HSK 3): not only (with 而且)
  - Note: 不但 usually pairs with 而且 or 也 in the second half: 他不但会说，而且会写.
  - Example: 他不但会说汉语，而且会写汉字。 / He can not only speak Chinese but also write characters.
- **不光** (bùguāng, HSK 4): not only (spoken)
  - Note: 不光 is a spoken "not only", often followed by 还: 他不光会唱歌，还会跳舞. 不但 is the standard form and 不仅 the formal one.
  - Example: 他不光会唱歌，还会跳舞。 / He can not only sing, but also dance.
- **不仅** (bùjǐn, HSK 4): not only (formal, written)
  - Note: 不仅 is a formal "not only", common in writing: 她不仅聪明，而且很努力. In speech, 不但 or 不光 are more common.
  - Example: 她不仅聪明，而且很努力。 / She is not only smart, but also very hardworking.

## 52. season

- **季节** (jìjié, HSK 3): season (the full word)
  - Example: 你最喜欢哪个季节？ / Which season do you like best?
- **季** (jì, HSK 3): season (in words: 四季)
  - Note: 季 is mostly used inside words: 四季, 季节.
  - Example: 一年有四季。 / There are four seasons in a year.

## 53. should; ought to

- **应该** (yīnggāi, HSK 3): should; ought to (everyday)
  - Example: 你应该早一点儿睡觉。 / You should go to bed a bit earlier.
- **应当** (yīngdāng, HSK 5): should; ought to (formal)
  - Note: 应当 is "ought to, should", a little more formal than 应该: 应当努力 (should work hard), 应当帮助别人 (should help others).
  - Example: 学生应当努力学习。 / Students should study hard.

## 54. street

- **街** (jiē, HSK 3): street (everyday: 这条街)
  - Example: 这条街上有很多商店。 / There are many shops on this street.
- **街道** (jiēdào, HSK 4): street (formal; streets in general)
  - Note: 街道 is more formal and often means streets in general: 街道很干净 (the streets are clean). In speech, 街 is more common: 这条街 (this street).
  - Example: 街道上有很多人。 / There are many people on the street.

## 55. the same

- **一样** (yíyàng, HSK 3): the same (everyday: 跟…一样)
  - Note: 一样 is the everyday word, also for comparisons: 我跟你一样高 (I'm as tall as you).
  - Example: 我和你的手机一样。 / My phone is the same as yours.
- **相同** (xiāngtóng, HSK 4): the same (formal, written)
  - Note: 相同 is more formal and written: 相同的问题, 看法相同. In speech, and for comparisons, use 一样: 我跟你一样高.
  - Example: 我们的看法相同。 / Our views are the same.

## 56. to answer; to reply

- **回答** (huídá, HSK 3): to answer; to reply (everyday)
  - Example: 请回答我的问题。 / Please answer my question.
- **答** (dá, HSK 4): to answer; to reply (short; in words)
  - Note: 答 is rarely used alone. Common words: 回答 (answer), 答应 (promise), 答案 (answer key).
  - Example: 我问了三遍，他都没答。 / I asked three times and he did not answer once.

## 57. to chat

- **聊** (liáo, HSK 3): to chat (+ a topic: 聊工作)
  - Note: 聊 can take a topic after it: 聊工作 (to talk about work), 聊一聊 (to have a chat). 聊天儿 is "to chat" with no topic: 我们在聊天儿.
  - Example: 我和朋友聊了一个小时。 / I chatted with my friend for an hour.
- **聊天儿** (liáotiānr, HSK 3): to chat (no topic needed)
  - Note: 聊天儿 is a verb plus object, so you can say 聊了一会儿天儿 (chatted for a while).
  - Example: 我喜欢和朋友聊天儿。 / I like chatting with friends.

## 58. to choose; to pick

- **选** (xuǎn, HSK 3): to choose; to pick (from options)
  - Example: 菜单上的菜很多，你来选吧。 / There are many dishes on the menu. You choose.
- **挑** (tiāo, HSK 5): to choose; to pick (carefully, the best one)
  - Note: 挑 is to pick out the best: 挑东西 (to pick things), 挑一个 (to pick one). 选 is "to choose"; 挑 is carefully picking among many.
  - Example: 他在水果店里挑苹果。 / He is choosing apples in the fruit shop.

## 59. to raise; to improve

- **提高** (tígāo, HSK 3): to raise; to improve (a level, quality)
  - Note: 提高 is the everyday word for raising a level or quality: 提高汉语水平 (to improve your Chinese).
  - Example: 多练习可以提高你的汉语水平。 / More practice can raise your level of Chinese.
- **提升** (tíshēng, HSK 5): to raise; to improve (formal; also promote)
  - Note: 提升 is to raise something to a higher level: 提升水平 (to raise the level), 提升能力 (to improve ability). The opposite is 降低.
  - Example: 他的汉语水平提升了很多。 / His level of Chinese has improved a lot.

## 60. to use

- **用** (yòng, HSK 3): to use (everyday)
  - Example: 我可以用一下你的手机吗？ / May I use your phone for a moment?
- **使用** (shǐyòng, HSK 4): to use (formal: signs, instructions)
  - Note: 使用 is formal, common on signs and in instructions: 请不要使用手机. In speech people say 用: 我用一下你的手机.
  - Example: 请不要使用手机。 / Please do not use your phone.

## 61. address

- **地址** (dìzhǐ, HSK 4): address (any address)
  - Example: 请把你的地址写在这里。 / Please write your address here.
- **住址** (zhùzhǐ, HSK 5): address (where you live)
  - Note: 住址 is the place where one lives: 家庭住址 (a home address), 新住址 (a new address), 写住址 (to write an address).
  - Example: 请写下你的住址。 / Please write down your address.

## 62. beautiful

- **美** (měi, HSK 4): beautiful (short; also in words)
  - Note: 美 is also part of 美国 (the USA), 美丽, 美好 and 美食.
  - Example: 这里的花真美。 / The flowers here are really beautiful.
- **美丽** (měilì, HSK 4): beautiful (written, before a noun)
  - Note: 美丽 is a fuller, more written word, often before a noun: 美丽的城市. In speech people say 很美 or 很漂亮.
  - Example: 这座城市很美丽。 / This city is very beautiful.

## 63. besides; in addition

- **此外** (cǐwài, HSK 4): besides; in addition (formal, written)
  - Note: 此外 is formal and starts a sentence or clause: 此外，他还喜欢跳舞. In speech, 另外 is more common.
  - Example: 他喜欢唱歌，此外还喜欢跳舞。 / He likes singing; besides, he also likes dancing.
- **另外** (lìngwài, HSK 4): besides; in addition (everyday; also "another")
  - Note: 另外 is the everyday "besides": 另外还买了香蕉. It also means "another, other": 另外一个人 (another person).
  - Example: 我买了苹果，另外还买了香蕉。 / I bought apples, and in addition bought bananas.

## 64. difference

- **区别** (qūbié, HSK 4): difference (what tells things apart)
  - Note: 区别 is also a verb ("to distinguish"): 区别好坏.
  - Example: 这两个词有什么区别？ / What is the difference between these two words?
- **差别** (chābié, HSK 5): difference (a gap: how big)
  - Note: 差别 is a difference that can be big or small (差别很大). Here 差 is read chā, not chà.
  - Example: 这两个词的意思有一点儿差别。 / There is a small difference in meaning between these two words.

## 65. each other; mutually

- **互相** (hùxiāng, HSK 4): each other; mutually (everyday, before a verb)
  - Example: 我们应该互相帮助。 / We should help each other.
- **相互** (xiānghù, HSK 4): each other; mutually (formal; also before a noun)
  - Note: 相互 is more formal and can also come before a noun: 相互关系 (a mutual relationship). 互相 is more common in speech and goes before a verb: 互相帮助.
  - Example: 朋友之间要相互帮助。 / Friends need to help each other.

## 66. feature; characteristic

- **特点** (tèdiǎn, HSK 4): feature; characteristic (typical)
  - Note: 特点 is what is typical or special about something: 这个城市的特点.
  - Example: 这个城市有什么特点？ / What features does this city have?
- **特征** (tèzhēng, HSK 5): feature; characteristic (that identifies)
  - Note: 特征 is a distinguishing feature: 主要特征 (the main feature), 特征明显 (an obvious feature).
  - Example: 这个人的特征是个子很高。 / This person's main feature is being very tall.

## 67. friendship

- **友情** (yǒuqíng, HSK 4): friendship (between people)
  - Note: 友情 is the feeling between friends: 我们的友情很深.
  - Example: 我们的友情很深。 / Our friendship is deep.
- **友谊** (yǒuyì, HSK 4): friendship (also between countries)
  - Note: 友谊 is a little more formal and is also used between groups and countries: 两国的友谊 (friendship between two countries). For close friends, 友情 is also common.
  - Example: 友谊是最重要的。 / Friendship is the most important.

## 68. future

- **将来** (jiānglái, HSK 4): future (someone's later life; spoken)
  - Note: 将来 is common in speech, often about someone's own plans: 你将来想做什么?
  - Example: 你将来想做什么？ / What do you want to do in the future?
- **未来** (wèilái, HSK 5): future (the time ahead; formal)
  - Note: 未来 is the future, a little more formal than 将来: 未来几年 (the next few years), 美好的未来 (a bright future).
  - Example: 你未来想做什么工作？ / What job do you want to do in the future?

## 69. group; team

- **小组** (xiǎozǔ, HSK 4): group; team (a small team)
  - Note: 小组 is a small group for study or work: 学习小组.
  - Example: 我们是一个学习小组。 / We are a study group.
- **组** (zǔ, HSK 4): group; team (also a measure word)
  - Note: 组 is a group, and also a measure word for sets: 一组 (one group, one set).
  - Example: 这个组有五个人。 / This group has five people.

## 70. indeed; really

- **确实** (quèshí, HSK 4): indeed; really (also: true)
  - Note: 确实 confirms something: 这个电影确实很好看. It can also be an adjective, "true": 这个消息确实.
  - Example: 这个电影确实很好看。 / This movie really is good.
- **的确** (díquè, HSK 5): indeed; really (more formal)
  - Note: 的确 confirms that something is true. 的 is read dí in this word, not de.
  - Example: 他的确是个好老师。 / He really is a good teacher.

## 71. member

- **会员** (huìyuán, HSK 4): member (of a club, shop or website)
  - Note: 会员 is a member of a club, shop or website, often with a card: 会员卡 (a membership card).
  - Example: 我是这家书店的会员。 / I am a member of this bookshop.
- **成员** (chéngyuán, HSK 5): member (of a group, team or family)
  - Note: 成员 is a person who belongs to a group, such as a family, team or company.
  - Example: 他是我们学习小组的成员。 / He is a member of our study group.

## 72. nature

- **大自然** (dàzìrán, HSK 4): nature (the natural world)
  - Example: 我喜欢到大自然里走一走。 / I like to go for a walk in nature.
- **自然** (zìrán, HSK 4): nature (also: natural, naturally)
  - Note: 自然 is also an adjective ("natural") and an adverb ("naturally").
  - Example: 我喜欢大自然。 / I like nature.

## 73. no matter (what, how)

- **不管** (bùguǎn, HSK 4): no matter (what, how; spoken)
  - Note: 不管 is like 不论: the second half usually has 都 or 也.
  - Example: 不管多晚，我都等你。 / No matter how late, I will wait for you.
- **无论** (wúlùn, HSK 4): no matter (what, how; more formal)
  - Note: 无论 is often followed by a question word and then 都 or 也.
  - Example: 无论天气怎么样，我都去。 / No matter what the weather is like, I will go.

## 74. only; merely

- **仅** (jǐn, HSK 4): only; merely (formal)
  - Note: 仅 is formal, like 只.
  - Example: 他仅用了一天就学会了。 / He learned it in just one day.
- **仅仅** (jǐnjǐn, HSK 4): only; merely (stresses how little)
  - Note: 仅仅 stresses how little or how few: 仅仅三个月 (only three months). 只 is the everyday word.
  - Example: 他仅仅学了三个月，就会说汉语了。 / He has studied for just three months and can already speak Chinese.

## 75. otherwise; or else

- **否则** (fǒuzé, HSK 4): otherwise; or else (formal)
  - Note: 否则 is a little formal: 快走吧，否则你会迟到. In speech, 不然 or 要不然 is more common.
  - Example: 快走吧，否则你会迟到。 / Hurry up, otherwise you will be late.
- **不然** (bùrán, HSK 5): otherwise; or else (spoken)
  - Note: 不然 starts a new clause and means "if not". The longer form 要不然 is also common in speech.
  - Example: 快走吧，不然你要迟到了。 / Hurry up, otherwise you will be late.

## 76. perhaps; maybe

- **也许** (yěxǔ, HSK 4): perhaps; maybe (everyday)
  - Example: 他也许不来了。 / Perhaps he is not coming any more.
- **或许** (huòxǔ, HSK 5): perhaps; maybe (written)
  - Note: 或许 is a written "perhaps". It comes before the verb or the adjective, and is the same as 也许.
  - Example: 他或许已经回家了。 / Perhaps he has already gone home.
- **说不定** (shuōbudìng, HSK 5): perhaps; maybe (spoken: could well be)
  - Note: 说不定 means "maybe, it could be": 说不定会下雨 (it may rain). It shows you are not sure. 也许 is similar.
  - Example: 他说不定已经回家了。 / Perhaps he has already gone home.

## 77. physical strength

- **力气** (lìqi, HSK 4): physical strength (power to lift or push)
  - Note: 力气 is the power to lift, push or carry: 力气很大 (very strong), 没力气 (no energy).
  - Example: 他的力气很大。 / He is very strong.
- **体力** (tǐlì, HSK 5): physical strength (stamina)
  - Note: 体力 is physical strength or stamina: 体力好 (strong stamina), 体力不好 (weak stamina).
  - Example: 爬山需要很多体力。 / Climbing mountains needs a lot of physical strength.

## 78. province

- **省** (shěng, HSK 4): province (with a name; also: to save)
  - Note: 省 is also a verb meaning "to save", as in 省钱.
  - Example: 中国有很多省。 / China has many provinces.
- **省份** (shěngfèn, HSK 5): province (in general: 哪个省份)
  - Note: 省份 is a province: 哪个省份 (which province), 一个省份 (a province). The capital of a province is 省会.
  - Example: 中国有三十多个省份。 / China has more than thirty provinces.

## 79. rent

- **房租** (fángzū, HSK 4): rent (for a home)
  - Example: 这个月的房租已经交了。 / This month's rent has already been paid.
- **租金** (zūjīn, HSK 5): rent (money paid to rent anything)
  - Note: 租金 is the money paid to rent something: 付租金 (to pay rent), 租金很贵 (the rent is expensive), 每月租金 (monthly rent).
  - Example: 这个房子的租金很高。 / The rent of this house is very high.

## 80. scenery; view

- **风景** (fēngjǐng, HSK 4): scenery; view (everyday; also a scenic place)
  - Note: 风景 is the everyday word: 这里风景很好, 看风景 (to enjoy the view).
  - Example: 这里的风景真美。 / The scenery here is really beautiful.
- **景色** (jǐngsè, HSK 4): scenery; view (how a scene looks; written)
  - Note: 景色 describes how a scene looks, often in writing: 秋天的景色 (autumn scenery). 风景 is more common in speech: 看风景.
  - Example: 山上的景色很美。 / The scenery on the mountain is beautiful.
