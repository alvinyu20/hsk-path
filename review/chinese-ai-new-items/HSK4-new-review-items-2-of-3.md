### g4-33  尤其 and 其中  (lesson: 至少, 左右, 大约, 尤其 and 其中: amounts and focus)

A132. **choose**. English: I like animals, especially dogs.
   - right: 我喜欢动物，尤其是狗。  (Wǒ xǐhuan dòngwù, yóuqí shì gǒu.)
   - wrong: 我喜欢动物，是尤其狗。
   - note shown to the learner: The order is 尤其是.
A133. **fill**. 班里有二十个学生，___十个是男的。  (Bān lǐ yǒu èrshí ge xuésheng, qízhōng shí ge shì nán de.)
   - English: There are 20 students in the class, and 10 of them are male.
   - blank: 其中
   - note shown to the learner: 其中 points back to the group just named.
A134. **build**. 他喜欢水果，尤其是苹果。  (Tā xǐhuan shuǐguǒ, yóuqí shì píngguǒ.)
   - English: He likes fruit, especially apples.
   - note shown to the learner: 尤其是 picks out the main case.
A135. **spot**. sentence with the extra word: 我喜欢旅游，尤其是特别夏天。
   - extra word: 特别
   - correct sentence: 我喜欢旅游，尤其是夏天。  (Wǒ xǐhuan lǚyóu, yóuqí shì xiàtiān.)
   - English: I like travelling, especially in summer.
   - note shown to the learner: 尤其 already means especially; do not add 特别.
