Hello! I am building an HSK 5 study site (learners know HSK 1 to 4 and are now learning HSK 5). Below are the NEW practice items I just added: grammar review questions, "write your own sentence" prompts with a model answer, and listening clips. Please act as a native speaker and Chinese teacher and check them.
**Report only the problems**, no praise and no repeating good items. For each problem give: number / the item / what is wrong (simple Chinese plus one line of English) / a more natural version (using only HSK 1 to 5 words).
Please watch for: 1) unnatural or non-idiomatic wording; 2) grammar mistakes; 3) English that does not match the Chinese; 4) written style taught as spoken (or the reverse); 5) words too hard for HSK 5; 6) wrong pinyin (words with more than one reading, neutral tones, spaces between words); 7) in multiple-choice items, the "wrong" option must really be wrong (not another way that also works); 8) in "spot the extra word" items, the sentence without the word must be correct and the sentence with it must really be wrong; 9) the two "what to listen for" notes under each clip (tones, tone changes, readings, light syllables) must be accurate.
If a part has no problems, answer "No problems found".

C1. 他每天都坚持锻炼身体，所以很少生病。  (Tā měitiān dōu jiānchí duànliàn shēntǐ, suǒyǐ hěn shǎo shēngbìng.)
   - English: He keeps exercising every day, so he rarely gets sick.
   - note 1: 坚持 jiānchí: a high flat tone, then a rise.
   - note 2: 锻炼 duànliàn: both syllables fall.
C2. 我打算明年去国外留学，现在正在准备材料。  (Wǒ dǎsuàn míngnián qù guówài liúxué, xiànzài zhèngzài zhǔnbèi cáiliào.)
   - English: I plan to go abroad to study next year, and I am preparing the documents now.
   - note 1: 打算 dǎsuàn: 打 only dips before the falling 算.
   - note 2: 留学 liúxué: both syllables rise.
C3. 这家餐厅的服务很好，菜也很好吃。  (Zhè jiā cāntīng de fúwù hěn hǎo, cài yě hěn hǎochī.)
   - English: The service in this restaurant is very good, and the food is tasty too.
   - note 1: 餐厅 cāntīng: both syllables are high and flat.
   - note 2: 服务 fúwù: a rise, then a fall.
C4. 你的汉语说得越来越好了。  (Nǐ de Hànyǔ shuō de yuè lái yuè hǎo le.)
   - English: Your Chinese is getting better and better.
   - note 1: 越来越 yuè lái yuè: a fall, a rise, then a fall.
   - note 2: 汉语 Hànyǔ: a fall, then a dip.
C5. 我们应该保护环境，不要乱扔垃圾。  (Wǒmen yīnggāi bǎohù huánjìng, búyào luàn rēng lājī.)
   - English: We should protect the environment and not throw rubbish about.
   - note 1: 保护 bǎohù: 保 only dips before the falling 护.
   - note 2: 环境 huánjìng: a rise, then a fall.
C6. 请把声音调小一点，孩子已经睡着了。  (Qǐng bǎ shēngyīn diào xiǎo yì diǎn, háizi yǐjīng shuìzháo le.)
   - English: Please turn the volume down a little; the child has already fallen asleep.
   - note 1: 调小 tiáoxiǎo: 调 is said tiáo (to adjust), a rise.
   - note 2: 睡着 shuìzháo: 着 is said zháo here (the action worked), a rise.
C7. 他的汉语水平提高得很快，老师经常表扬他。  (Tā de Hànyǔ shuǐpíng tígāo de hěn kuài, lǎoshī jīngcháng biǎoyáng tā.)
   - English: His Chinese level is improving fast, and the teacher often praises him.
   - note 1: 提高 tígāo: a rise, then a high flat tone.
   - note 2: 表扬 biǎoyáng: 表 only dips before the rising 扬.
C8. 我对这个电影不太感兴趣，你呢？  (Wǒ duì zhège diànyǐng bú tài gǎnxìngqù, nǐ ne?)
   - English: I am not very interested in this film; what about you?
   - note 1: 感兴趣 gǎn xìngqù: 兴 is said xìng (a fall) here, not xīng.
   - note 2: 不太 bú tài: 不 is said bú before the falling 太.
C9. 为了身体健康，他每天早上都去公园走一走。  (Wèile shēntǐ jiànkāng, tā měitiān zǎoshang dōu qù gōngyuán zǒu yì zǒu.)
   - English: For the sake of his health, he goes for a walk in the park every morning.
   - note 1: 为了 wèile: 为 is said wèi (for), a fall; 了 is light.
   - note 2: 走一走 zǒu yi zǒu: 一 is light and short between the two verbs.
C10. 昨天我们看了一场很精彩的足球比赛。  (Zuótiān wǒmen kàn le yì chǎng hěn jīngcǎi de zúqiú bǐsài.)
   - English: Yesterday we watched a very exciting football match.
   - note 1: 精彩 jīngcǎi: a high flat tone, then a dip.
   - note 2: 一场 yì chǎng: 一 is said yì (a fall) before the dipping 场.
C11. 这些问题都比较复杂，需要慢慢解决。  (Zhèxiē wèntí dōu bǐjiào fùzá, xūyào màn màn jiějué.)

   - note 2: 复杂 fùzá: a fall, then a rise.
C12. 他已经把房间收拾干净了，妈妈很满意。  (Tā yǐjīng bǎ fángjiān shōushi gānjìng le, māma hěn mǎnyì.)

   - note 1: 收拾 shōushi: the second syllable is light and short.
   - note 2: 满意 mǎnyì: 满 only dips before the falling 意.
C13. 我们先坐地铁，然后换公共汽车。  (Wǒmen xiān zuò dìtiě, ránhòu huàn gōnggòng qìchē.)
   - English: First we take the metro, and then change to a bus.

   - note 2: 然后 ránhòu: a rise, then a fall.
C14. 这个周末你有空吗？我想请你吃饭。  (Zhège zhōumò nǐ yǒukòng ma? Wǒ xiǎng qǐng nǐ chī fàn.)
   - English: Are you free this weekend? I would like to invite you to dinner.
   - note 1: 请你 qǐng nǐ: two third tones in a row, so 请 rises (say qíngnǐ).
   - note 2: 周末 zhōumò: a high flat tone, then a fall.
C15. 他从小就喜欢画画，现在是一名画家。  (Tā cóngxiǎo jiù xǐhuan huà huà, xiànzài shì yì míng huàjiā.)

   - note 2: 一名 yì míng: 一 is said yì (a fall) before the rising 名.
