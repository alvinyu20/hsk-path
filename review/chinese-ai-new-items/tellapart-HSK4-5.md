## T4-buguan-ruguo-jishi. HSK 4: 不管, 如果 or 即使?  (options: 不管 / 如果 / 即使)
**What each option means (shown when it is picked by mistake):**
- 不管: 不管…都…: no matter which, who or how; the result is the same. It needs a question form or a choice after it.
- 如果: 如果…就…: if this happens, then that.
- 即使: 即使…也…: even if this happens, that still holds.

**Cards** (the right answer is in 【 】):
T4-buguan-ruguo-jishi.1. 【不管】你去不去，我都去。  (Bùguǎn nǐ qù bú qù, wǒ dōu qù.)  — Whether you go or not, I'm going.
   - why: A choice (去不去) + 都: 不管.
T4-buguan-ruguo-jishi.2. 【如果】明天下雨，我们就不去了。  (Rúguǒ míngtiān xiàyǔ, wǒmen jiù bú qù le.)  — If it rains tomorrow, we won't go.
   - why: A plain condition + 就: 如果.
T4-buguan-ruguo-jishi.3. 【即使】明天下雨，我们也要去。  (Jíshǐ míngtiān xiàyǔ, wǒmen yě yào qù.)  — Even if it rains tomorrow, we'll still go.
   - why: Even if + 也: 即使.
T4-buguan-ruguo-jishi.4. 【不管】多忙，他每天都运动。  (Bùguǎn duō máng, tā měitiān dōu yùndòng.)  — No matter how busy he is, he exercises every day.
   - why: 多忙 'how busy' + 都: 不管.
T4-buguan-ruguo-jishi.5. 【如果】你有时间，就来我家玩吧。  (Rúguǒ nǐ yǒu shíjiān, jiù lái wǒ jiā wán ba.)  — If you have time, come over to my place.
   - why: If + 就: 如果.
T4-buguan-ruguo-jishi.6. 【即使】你不说，我也知道。  (Jíshǐ nǐ bù shuō, wǒ yě zhīdào.)  — Even if you don't tell me, I know.
   - why: Even if + 也: 即使.
T4-buguan-ruguo-jishi.7. 【不管】谁来，都要先打电话。  (Bùguǎn shéi lái, dōu yào xiān dǎ diànhuà.)  — Whoever comes has to phone first.
   - why: 谁 'who' + 都: 不管.
T4-buguan-ruguo-jishi.8. 【如果】我是你，我就不买。  (Rúguǒ wǒ shì nǐ, wǒ jiù bù mǎi.)  — If I were you, I wouldn't buy it.
   - why: If + 就: 如果.
T4-buguan-ruguo-jishi.9. 【即使】再累，他也要把工作做完。  (Jíshǐ zài lèi, tā yě yào bǎ gōngzuò zuò wán.)  — However tired he is, he'll finish the work.
   - why: Even if + 也: 即使.

## T4-ranhou-erqie-raner. HSK 4: 然后, 而且 or 然而?  (options: 然后 / 而且 / 然而)
**What each option means (shown when it is picked by mistake):**
- 然后: 然后 is 'then, after that': the next step in time.
- 而且: 而且 is 'and also, what's more': it adds a second point in the same direction.
- 然而: 然而 is 'however': what follows goes against what came before. It sounds written.

**Cards** (the right answer is in 【 】):
T4-ranhou-erqie-raner.1. 我们先吃饭，【然后】去看电影。  (Wǒmen xiān chī fàn, ránhòu qù kàn diànyǐng.)  — Let's eat first, then go to the cinema.
   - why: The next step in time: 然后.
T4-ranhou-erqie-raner.2. 这家店的菜很好吃，【而且】很便宜。  (Zhè jiā diàn de cài hěn hǎochī, érqiě hěn piányi.)  — The food here is tasty, and it's cheap too.
   - why: A second good point: 而且.
T4-ranhou-erqie-raner.3. 他很努力，【然而】还是没有成功。  (Tā hěn nǔlì, rán'ér háishi méiyǒu chénggōng.)  — He worked hard; however, he still didn't succeed.
   - why: The opposite of what you'd expect: 然而.
T4-ranhou-erqie-raner.4. 他会说汉语，【而且】说得很好。  (Tā huì shuō Hànyǔ, érqiě shuō de hěn hǎo.)  — He speaks Chinese, and speaks it very well.
   - why: Adding a stronger point: 而且.
T4-ranhou-erqie-raner.5. 我先洗了个澡，【然后】就睡了。  (Wǒ xiān xǐ le ge zǎo, ránhòu jiù shuì le.)  — I had a shower first and then went to sleep.
   - why: The next step: 然后.
T4-ranhou-erqie-raner.6. 大家都以为他会来，【然而】他没来。  (Dàjiā dōu yǐwéi tā huì lái, rán'ér tā méi lái.)  — Everyone thought he would come. However, he didn't.
   - why: Against what was expected: 然而.
T4-ranhou-erqie-raner.7. 这个房间很大，【而且】很安静。  (Zhège fángjiān hěn dà, érqiě hěn ānjìng.)  — This room is big, and it's quiet as well.
   - why: One more good point: 而且.
T4-ranhou-erqie-raner.8. 你下了车往右走，【然后】就能看到银行了。  (Nǐ xià le chē wǎng yòu zǒu, ránhòu jiù néng kàn dào yínháng le.)  — Get off the bus and turn right, then you'll see the bank.
   - why: The next step: 然后.
T4-ranhou-erqie-raner.9. 这个办法听起来不错，【然而】做起来很难。  (Zhège bànfǎ tīng qǐlái búcuò, rán'ér zuò qǐlái hěn nán.)  — The idea sounds good. In practice, however, it's hard.
   - why: Turning against what came before: 然而.

## T4-jiran-yaoshi. HSK 4: 既然 or 要是?  (options: 既然 / 要是)
**What each option means (shown when it is picked by mistake):**
- 既然: 既然 is 'since, now that': the first part is already a fact.
- 要是: 要是 is 'if': the first part may or may not happen.

**Cards** (the right answer is in 【 】):
T4-jiran-yaoshi.1. 【既然】你已经决定了，我就不说什么了。  (Jìrán nǐ yǐjīng juédìng le, wǒ jiù bù shuō shénme le.)  — Since you've already decided, I won't say anything.
   - why: It is already a fact (已经): 既然.
T4-jiran-yaoshi.2. 【要是】明天下雨，我们就不去了。  (Yàoshi míngtiān xiàyǔ, wǒmen jiù bú qù le.)  — If it rains tomorrow, we won't go.
   - why: It may or may not happen: 要是.
T4-jiran-yaoshi.3. 【既然】来了，就多坐一会儿吧。  (Jìrán lái le, jiù duō zuò yíhuìr ba.)  — Since you're here, stay a bit longer.

T4-jiran-yaoshi.4. 【要是】明天你不舒服，就别来了。  (Yàoshi míngtiān nǐ bù shūfu, jiù bié lái le.)  — If you don't feel well tomorrow, don't come.

T4-jiran-yaoshi.5. 【既然】你不喜欢，为什么还要买？  (Jìrán nǐ bù xǐhuan, wèishénme hái yào mǎi?)  — Since you don't like it, why buy it?

T4-jiran-yaoshi.7. 【既然】大家都同意，那就这么办吧。  (Jìrán dàjiā dōu tóngyì, nà jiù zhème bàn ba.)  — Since everyone agrees, let's do it this way.
   - why: Already a fact: 既然.
T4-jiran-yaoshi.8. 【要是】有问题，就给我打电话。  (Yàoshi yǒu wèntí, jiù gěi wǒ dǎ diànhuà.)  — If there's a problem, give me a call.

## T5-genben-jianzhi-juedui. HSK 5: 根本, 简直 or 绝对?  (options: 根本 / 简直 / 绝对)

- 根本: 根本 is 'at all, simply not': it is mostly used with 不 or 没 to deny something completely.
