# HSK 2: new review items (160 review questions, 76 write-your-own prompts, 24 listening clips)


## A. Grammar review questions

Each item belongs to one grammar point. Types: **choose** (pick the sentence that says the English; option 1 is the right one), **fill** (the blank is a word), **build** (put the words in order; the sentence is the answer), **spot** (tap the extra word; the sentence with the extra word is wrong).


### g2-1  A 比 B + adjective  (lesson: Comparing: 比, 没有, 最, 这么 and 那么)

A1. **choose**. English: This one is more expensive than that one.
   - right: 这个比那个贵。  (Zhège bǐ nàge guì.)
   - wrong: 这个比那个很贵。
   - note shown to the learner: Do not put 很 in a 比 sentence.
A2. **fill**. 我的手机___你的新。  (Wǒ de shǒujī bǐ nǐ de xīn.)
   - English: My phone is newer than yours.
   - blank: 比
   - note shown to the learner: 比 compares A with B.
A3. **build**. 他比我忙。  (Tā bǐ wǒ máng.)
   - English: He is busier than me.
   - note shown to the learner: A 比 B + adjective.
A4. **spot**. sentence with the extra word: 这个房间比那个很大。
   - extra word: 很
   - correct sentence: 这个房间比那个大。  (Zhège fángjiān bǐ nàge dà.)
   - English: This room is bigger than that one.
   - note shown to the learner: Do not put 很 in a 比 sentence.
