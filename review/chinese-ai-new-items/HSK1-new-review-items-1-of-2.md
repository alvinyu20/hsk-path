# HSK 1: new review items (181 review questions, 85 write-your-own prompts, 24 listening clips)


## A. Grammar review questions

Each item belongs to one grammar point. Types: **choose** (pick the sentence that says the English; option 1 is the right one), **fill** (the blank is a word), **build** (put the words in order; the sentence is the answer), **spot** (tap the extra word; the sentence with the extra word is wrong).


### g1-svo  Subject + verb + object  (lesson: Word order: who does what)

A1. **choose**. English: I eat rice.
   - right: 我吃米饭。  (Wǒ chī mǐfàn.)
   - wrong: 我米饭吃。
   - note shown to the learner: Subject, verb, then object.
A2. **fill**. 爸爸___水。  (Bàba hē shuǐ.)
   - English: Dad drinks water.
   - blank: 喝
   - note shown to the learner: Subject + verb + object.
A3. **build**. 哥哥看电影。  (Gēge kàn diànyǐng.)
   - English: My older brother watches a film.
   - note shown to the learner: Subject + verb + object.
A4. **spot**. sentence with the extra word: 我很学汉语。
   - extra word: 很
   - correct sentence: 我学汉语。  (Wǒ xué Hànyǔ.)
   - English: I study Chinese.
   - note shown to the learner: Do not put 很 before a plain verb.
