# HSK 5: new review items (314 review questions, 157 write-your-own prompts, 24 listening clips)


## A. Grammar review questions

Each item belongs to one grammar point. Types: **choose** (pick the sentence that says the English; option 1 is the right one), **fill** (the blank is a word), **build** (put the words in order; the sentence is the answer), **spot** (tap the extra word; the sentence with the extra word is wrong).


### g5-1  一旦…就…  (lesson: 一旦…就…, 因而 and 从而: once, so, and so)

A1. **choose**. English: Once you have a problem, call me.
   - right: 一旦你有问题，就给我打电话。  (Yídàn nǐ yǒu wèntí, jiù gěi wǒ dǎ diànhuà.)
   - wrong: 一旦你有问题，给我就打电话。
   - note shown to the learner: 就 comes right before the result.
A2. **fill**. 一旦下雪，学校___会放假。  (Yídàn xià xuě, xuéxiào jiù huì fàngjià.)
   - English: Once it snows, the school will have a holiday.
   - blank: 就
   - note shown to the learner: 一旦 + condition, 就 + result.
A3. **build**. 一旦她生气，就不说话。  (Yídàn tā shēngqì, jiù bù shuōhuà.)
   - English: Once she gets angry, she does not talk.
   - note shown to the learner: 一旦 + condition, 就 + result.
A4. **spot**. sentence with the extra word: 一旦要是你累了，就休息一下。
   - extra word: 要是
   - correct sentence: 一旦你累了，就休息一下。  (Yídàn nǐ lèi le, jiù xiūxi yíxià.)
   - English: Once you get tired, take a rest.
   - note shown to the learner: 一旦 already means once or if; do not add 要是.

### g5-2  因而 and 从而  (lesson: 一旦…就…, 因而 and 从而: once, so, and so)

A5. **choose**. English: He exercises every day and so stays healthy.
   - right: 他每天锻炼，从而保持健康。  (Tā měitiān duànliàn, cóng'ér bǎochí jiànkāng.)
   - wrong: 他每天锻炼，保持从而健康。
   - note shown to the learner: 从而 comes right after the first action and before the result.
A6. **fill**. 天气很冷，___他穿了很多衣服。  (Tiānqì hěn lěng, yīn'ér tā chuān le hěn duō yīfu.)
   - English: It was very cold, so he put on a lot of clothes.
   - blank: 因而
   - note shown to the learner: 因而 gives the result of a reason.
A7. **build**. 她多听多说，从而提高了水平。  (Tā duō tīng duō shuō, cóng'ér tígāo le shuǐpíng.)
   - English: She listens and speaks more, and so has raised her level.
   - note shown to the learner: 从而 adds a second step that follows from the first.
A8. **spot**. sentence with the extra word: 他生病了，因而所以没去。
   - extra word: 所以
   - correct sentence: 他生病了，因而没去。  (Tā shēngbìng le, yīn'ér méi qù.)
   - English: He got sick, so he did not go.
   - note shown to the learner: 因而 already means so; do not add 所以.

### g5-3  或是…或是…  (lesson: 或是…或是…, …也好…也好 and 加上: either, whether, plus)

A9. **fill**. 你可以喝茶___咖啡。  (Nǐ kěyǐ hē chá huòshì kāfēi.)
   - English: You can have tea or coffee.
   - blank: 或是
   - note shown to the learner: 或是 joins choices in a statement.
A10. **choose**. English: At the weekend he either plays football or plays basketball.
   - right: 周末他或是踢足球，或是打篮球。  (Zhōumò tā huòshì tī zúqiú, huòshì dǎ lánqiú.)
   - wrong: 周末他还是踢足球，还是打篮球。
   - note shown to the learner: In a statement use 或是, not 还是.
A11. **spot**. sentence with the extra word: 明天我或是游泳，或是还是跑步。
   - extra word: 还是
   - correct sentence: 明天我或是游泳，或是跑步。  (Míngtiān wǒ huòshì yóuyǒng, huòshì pǎobù.)
   - English: Tomorrow I will either swim or run.
   - note shown to the learner: Do not put 还是 next to 或是.
A12. **build**. 晚上他或是看电视，或是玩游戏。  (Wǎnshang tā huòshì kàn diànshì, huòshì wán yóuxì.)
   - English: In the evening he either watches TV or plays games.
   - note shown to the learner: 或是 + choice, 或是 + choice.

### g5-4  …也好，…也好  (lesson: 或是…或是…, …也好…也好 and 加上: either, whether, plus)
