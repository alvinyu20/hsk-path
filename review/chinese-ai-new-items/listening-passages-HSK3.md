## P1. HSK 3 · Moving house (p3-move)
A woman asks a friend about his new flat.

1. 女：我听说你下个月要搬家？  (Wǒ tīngshuō nǐ xià ge yuè yào bānjiā?)
   - I heard you will move house next month?
2. 男：对，新房子离公司很近，坐地铁二十分钟就到。  (Duì, xīn fángzi lí gōngsī hěn jìn, zuò dìtiě èrshí fēnzhōng jiù dào.)
   - Yes. The new place is very close to my company: it only takes twenty minutes by metro.
3. 女：那太方便了。新房子大吗？  (Nà tài fāngbiàn le. Xīn fángzi dà ma?)
   - That is really convenient. Is the new place big?
4. 男：不太大，但是很安静，附近还有一个公园。  (Bú tài dà, dànshì hěn ānjìng, fùjìn hái yǒu yí ge gōngyuán.)
   - Not very big, but it is quiet, and there is a park nearby too.
5. 女：星期六我去帮你搬东西吧？  (Xīngqīliù wǒ qù bāng nǐ bān dōngxi ba?)
   - Shall I come and help you move things on Saturday?
6. 男：太好了！星期六上午九点，你能来吗？  (Tài hǎo le! Xīngqīliù shàngwǔ jiǔ diǎn, nǐ néng lái ma?)
   - Great! Nine on Saturday morning: can you come?
7. 女：没问题。  (Méi wèntí.)
   - No problem.

Q1. 男的什么时候搬家？  (Nán de shénme shíhou bānjiā?)
   - English: When is the man moving?
   - options: ✓ 下个月 / 下个星期 / 明天 / 今年冬天
   - answer in line 1
Q2. 新房子离公司怎么样？  (Xīn fángzi lí gōngsī zěnmeyàng?)
   - English: How far is the new place from his company?
   - options: ✓ 很近 / 很远 / 有点儿远 / 就在公司旁边
   - answer in line 2
Q3. 新房子附近有什么？  (Xīn fángzi fùjìn yǒu shénme?)
   - English: What is near the new place?
   - options: ✓ 一个公园 / 一个银行 / 一个学校 / 一个医院
   - answer in line 4
Q4. 女的什么时候去帮男的？  (Nǚ de shénme shíhou qù bāng nán de?)
   - English: When will the woman go to help the man?
   - options: ✓ 星期六上午 / 星期天上午 / 星期六下午 / 星期五晚上
   - answer in line 6
