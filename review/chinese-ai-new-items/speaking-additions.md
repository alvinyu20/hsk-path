你好！我在做一个 HSK 学习网站。下面是“说一说”练习里新写的对话，你还没有看过。学习者说“你”的话，明明说另一方的话。
有的对话里，学习者可以从两句话里选一句（A / B），明明的下一句会跟着变。有的是“倒霉的一天”版本：事情不顺利（没有茶了、火车晚点了），学习者要处理问题。
请你作为母语者和中文老师检查。**只列出有问题的地方**，不用表扬。每个问题请写：编号 / 原文 / 问题是什么（简单的中文，再加一句英文）/ 更好的说法（只用这个级别和以下的词）。
需要特别注意：1) 不自然或不地道的说法，尤其是服务员、司机、经理会不会这样说；2) 语法错误；3) 英文和中文意思不一致；4) A / B 两句和明明的回答对不对得上；5) 太难的词（请按新版 HSK 3.0（2021）词表判断）。
如果全部没问题，请回答“没有发现问题”。

Hello! These are new dialogues for the speaking practice on my study site; you have not seen them. The learner says the 你 lines; Mingming says the other side.
In some, the learner chooses one of two lines (A / B) and Mingming's next line follows that choice. Some are "bad day" versions where things go wrong (no tea left, the train is late) and the learner has to deal with it.
Please act as a native speaker and Chinese teacher. **Report only problems**, no praise. For each problem give: number / the item / what is wrong (simple Chinese plus one line of English) / a better version (words of this level or below only).
Please watch for: 1) unnatural wording, especially whether a waiter, driver or manager would really say it; 2) grammar mistakes; 3) English that does not match the Chinese; 4) whether the A / B lines and Mingming's answers fit together; 5) words too hard for the level (judge by the new HSK 3.0 (2021) lists).
If there are no problems, answer "No problems found".

183. 明明：你好，请问你要办理什么业务？  /  Hello, what business would you like to do?
184. 你：你好，我想开一个新的银行账户。  /  Hello, I would like to open a new bank account.
185. 明明：好的。请把你的身份证给我。  /  Okay. Please give me your ID card.
186. 你：哎呀，我把身份证忘在家里了。用护照可以吗？  /  Oh dear, I left my ID card at home. Can I use my passport?
187. 明明：不好意思，办这个业务必须用身份证。  /  Sorry, for this you must use your ID card.
188. 你：那我只能明天再来。你们几点下班？  /  Then I can only come back tomorrow. What time do you close?
189. 明明：我们下午五点下班，周末也开门。  /  We close at five in the afternoon, and we are open at weekends too.
190. 你：好的，那我明天上午带身份证再来。  /  Okay, then I will come back tomorrow morning with my ID card.
191. 明明：好的。下次来之前，可以先打电话问清楚要带什么。  /  Okay. Before you come next time, you can phone first and ask exactly what you need to bring.

## HSK 5 · Report a problem in your flat: a bad day · 倒霉的一天 / bad day of "repair"
192. 明明：你好，这里是物业，请问有什么事？  /  Hello, this is the property office. What is the matter?
193. 你：你好，我家的空调又坏了，这已经是这个月第二次了。  /  Hello, my air conditioner has broken down again; this is already the second time this month.
194. 明明：真不好意思。我们的师傅今天都很忙，最早明天下午才能去。  /  I am very sorry. Our repairmen are all busy today; the earliest they can come is tomorrow afternoon.
195. 你：明天下午？现在家里热得我受不了，能不能今天就安排？  /  Tomorrow afternoon? It is so hot at home now that I cannot bear it. Could you arrange it today?
196. 明明：我理解您的心情。我再问问，看看今天晚上能不能派人去。  /  I understand how you feel. I will ask again and see whether someone can be sent this evening.
197. 你：好的，谢谢你。另外，这个空调是不是应该换新的了？  /  Okay, thank you. Also, should this air conditioner be replaced with a new one?
198. 明明：这个问题我需要问一下经理，明天给您回电话。  /  I need to ask the manager about that; I will call you back tomorrow.
199. 你：好，希望这次能彻底解决。  /  Okay, hope it can be fixed for good this time.
200. 明明：好的，我们一定尽快处理。  /  Okay, we will certainly deal with it as soon as possible.
