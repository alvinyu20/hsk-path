## Word web topic sentences

1. 他是我的朋友，她是我的同学，我们都很好。  /  He is my friend, she is my classmate, and we are all well.
2. 这个很好，那个太贵了。  /  This one is good; that one is too expensive.
3. 我家有三口人，他八岁，她十岁。  /  There are three people in my family; he is eight and she is ten.
4. 我买了三本书、两件衣服和一个苹果。  /  I bought three books, two items of clothing and an apple.
5. 我叫小明，认识你很高兴。  /  My name is Xiaoming; nice to meet you.
6. 这个人是我的朋友，那个小朋友是他的孩子。  /  This person is my friend, and that child is his child.
7. 我家有五口人：爸爸、妈妈、哥哥、姐姐和我。  /  There are five people in my family: dad, mom, older brother, older sister and me.
8. 我女儿八岁，她是小学生。  /  My daughter is eight; she is a primary-school student.
9. 我在公司工作，今天很忙。  /  I work at a company, and I am busy today.
10. 今天是四月五日，明天是星期天。  /  Today is April 5th, and tomorrow is Sunday.
11. 现在几点？现在是七点半。  /  What time is it? It is half past seven.
12. 你在做什么？我正在看电视。  /  What are you doing? I am watching TV.
13. 我早饭吃面包，午饭吃米饭，晚饭吃饺子。  /  I eat bread for breakfast, rice for lunch and dumplings for dinner.
14. 我喝茶，他喝牛奶，你喝水吗？  /  I drink tea, he drinks milk; do you drink water?
15. 这件衣服多少钱？太贵了，我想买便宜的。  /  How much is this piece of clothing? It is too expensive; I want to buy a cheaper one.
16. 这件衣服很好看，你想穿吗？  /  This piece of clothing is pretty; do you want to wear it?
17. 我去商店买东西，也去书店看书。  /  I go to the shop to buy things, and I also go to the bookstore to read.
18. 你的书在桌子上，我的手机在这里。  /  Your book is on the table, and my phone is here.
19. 我今天去学校，明天回家。  /  I go to school today and go home tomorrow.
20. 我坐出租车去，你坐火车去。  /  I go by taxi, and you go by train.
21. 我想去中国玩，你呢？  /  I want to go and have fun in China; what about you?
22. 我家有三个房间，桌子和椅子在大房间里。  /  My home has three rooms; the table and chairs are in the big room.
23. 我是大学生，我哥哥是中学生。  /  I am a university student, and my older brother is a middle-school student.
24. 我学习汉语，我有很多问题。  /  I study Chinese, and I have many questions.
25. 我学汉语，会写一些汉字。  /  I study Chinese and can write some characters.
26. 我的手机在桌子上，请打电话给我。  /  My phone is on the table; please call me.
27. 我早上起床，晚上睡觉，中午吃饭。  /  I get up in the morning, go to bed in the evening and eat at noon.
28. 今天天气很冷，在下雨，明天会下雪。  /  The weather is cold today and it is raining; it will snow tomorrow.
29. 我家有一只狗和一只猫。  /  My family has a dog and a cat.
30. 你喜欢玩什么？  /  What do you like to play?
31. 他生病了，我们去医院看医生。  /  He is ill, so we go to the hospital to see a doctor.
32. 我很高兴，你也很高兴吗？  /  I am very happy; are you happy too?
33. 我知道，我觉得这个很好，我想买。  /  I know; I think this one is good, and I want to buy it.
34. 我在做饭，你可以开电视吗？  /  I am cooking; can you turn on the TV?
35. 我看见他了，他说话，我听见了。  /  I saw him; he spoke, and I heard it.
36. 这个大，那个小，这里的人很多。  /  This one is big, that one is small, and there are many people here.
37. 这个菜很好吃，这本书很新。  /  This dish is very tasty, and this book is new.
38. 这些东西是我的，这件事我知道。  /  These things are mine, and I know about this matter.
39. 我是学生，我有一个哥哥，他在家。  /  I am a student, I have an older brother, and he is at home.
40. 我不想去，我没有时间，你不要问了。  /  I do not want to go, and I have no time; you should not ask again.
41. 今天太热了，我非常想喝水。  /  It is too hot today; I really want a drink of water.
42. 我和你都是学生，他也是。  /  You and I are both students, and he is too.
43. 我给你一个苹果，你对我很好。  /  I give you an apple; you are very good to me.
44. 你好吗？我很好，你呢？我们去吧。  /  How are you? I am fine; and you? Let us go.
