Hello! I am building an HSK 3 study site (learners know HSK 1 to 2 and are now learning HSK 3). Below are the NEW practice items I just added: grammar review questions, "write your own sentence" prompts with a model answer, and listening clips. Please act as a native speaker and Chinese teacher and check them.
**Report only the problems**, no praise and no repeating good items. For each problem give: number / the item / what is wrong (simple Chinese plus one line of English) / a more natural version (using only HSK 1 to 3 words).
Please watch for: 1) unnatural or non-idiomatic wording; 2) grammar mistakes; 3) English that does not match the Chinese; 4) written style taught as spoken (or the reverse); 5) words too hard for HSK 3; 6) wrong pinyin (words with more than one reading, neutral tones, spaces between words); 7) in multiple-choice items, the "wrong" option must really be wrong (not another way that also works); 8) in "spot the extra word" items, the sentence without the word must be correct and the sentence with it must really be wrong; 9) the two "what to listen for" notes under each clip (tones, tone changes, readings, light syllables) must be accurate.
If a part has no problems, answer "No problems found".

C1. 我的朋友在银行工作。  (Wǒ de péngyou zài yínháng gōngzuò.)
   - English: My friend works in a bank.
   - note 1: 银行 yínháng: 行 is said háng here (a bank), not xíng.
   - note 2: 工作 gōngzuò: a high tone, then a fall.
C2. 我已经把书还给他了。  (Wǒ yǐjīng bǎ shū huángěi tā le.)
   - English: I have already given the book back to him.
   - note 1: 还 huán: said huán here (give back), a rise, not hái.
   - note 2: 已经 yǐjīng: 已 only dips before the high 经.
C3. 你的房间很干净，东西也不多。  (Nǐ de fángjiān hěn gānjìng, dōngxi yě bù duō.)
   - English: Your room is very clean, and there are not many things either.
   - note 1: 干净 gānjìng: a high tone, then a fall.
   - note 2: 东西 dōngxi: the second syllable is light and short.
C4. 我弟弟长得很快，已经比我高了。  (Wǒ dìdi zhǎng de hěn kuài, yǐjīng bǐ wǒ gāo le.)
   - English: My younger brother is growing fast and is already taller than me.
   - note 1: 长 zhǎng: said zhǎng (to grow), a dip.
   - note 2: 比我 bǐ wǒ: two third tones in a row, so 比 rises (say bí wǒ).
C5. 我一般早上七点起床。  (Wǒ yìbān zǎoshang qī diǎn qǐchuáng.)
   - English: I usually get up at 7 in the morning.
   - note 1: 一般 yìbān: 一 is said yì before the high 般.
   - note 2: 起床 qǐchuáng: 起 only dips before the rising 床.
C6. 这件事不难，我们一起做吧。  (Zhè jiàn shì bù nán, wǒmen yìqǐ zuò ba.)
   - English: This matter is not hard; let us do it together.
   - note 1: 不难 bù nán: 不 stays bù before a rising tone.
   - note 2: 一起 yìqǐ: 一 is said yì before the dipping 起.
C7. 他每天都骑自行车去上班。  (Tā měitiān dōu qí zìxíngchē qù shàngbān.)
   - English: He rides a bike to work every day.
   - note 1: 自行车 zìxíngchē: a fall, a rise, then high and flat. 行 is said xíng here, not háng.
   - note 2: 骑 qí: a rise. 骑自行车 means ride a bike.
C8. 请等一下，我马上就来。  (Qǐng děng yíxià, wǒ mǎshàng jiù lái.)
   - English: Please wait a moment; I will come right away.
   - note 1: 等一下 děng yíxià: 一 is said yí before the falling 下.
   - note 2: 马上 mǎshàng: 马 only dips before the falling 上.
C9. 我得回家了，明天见。  (Wǒ děi huí jiā le, míngtiān jiàn.)
   - English: I have to go home now; see you tomorrow.
   - note 1: 得 děi: said děi here (have to), a dip, not de or dé.
   - note 2: 明天 míngtiān: a rise, then a high tone.
C10. 这个电影很好看，你应该去看看。  (Zhège diànyǐng hěn hǎokàn, nǐ yīnggāi qù kànkan.)
   - English: This film is very good to watch; you should go and see it.
   - note 1: 好看 hǎokàn: 好 only dips before the falling 看.
   - note 2: 看看 kànkan: the second 看 is light and short.
C11. 这个包太大了，你能帮我吗？  (Zhège bāo tài dà le, nǐ néng bāng wǒ ma?)
   - English: This bag is too big; can you help me?
   - note 1: 你能 nǐ néng: 你 only dips before the rising 能.
   - note 2: 帮我 bāng wǒ: a high tone, then a dip.
C12. 这家饭店的菜很好吃，也不贵。  (Zhè jiā fàndiàn de cài hěn hǎochī, yě bú guì.)
   - English: The food at this restaurant is delicious, and it is not expensive either.
   - note 1: 好吃 hǎochī: 好 only dips before the high 吃.
   - note 2: 不贵 bú guì: 不 is said bú before the falling 贵.
C13. 他的声音很大，我听得很清楚。  (Tā de shēngyīn hěn dà, wǒ tīng de hěn qīngchu.)
   - English: His voice is loud, and I hear it very clearly.
   - note 1: 声音 shēngyīn: both syllables are high and flat.
   - note 2: 清楚 qīngchu: the second syllable is light and short.
C14. 我们坐出租车去机场吧。  (Wǒmen zuò chūzūchē qù jīchǎng ba.)
   - English: Let us take a taxi to the airport.
   - note 1: 出租车 chūzūchē: all three syllables are high and flat.
   - note 2: 机场 jīchǎng: a high tone, then a dip.
C15. 你想喝茶还是喝咖啡？  (Nǐ xiǎng hē chá háishi hē kāfēi?)
   - English: Do you want to drink tea or coffee?
   - note 1: 还是 háishi: 还 is said hái here (or), and 是 is light.
   - note 2: 想喝 xiǎng hē: 想 only dips before the high 喝.
C16. 我给你买了一把雨伞。  (Wǒ gěi nǐ mǎi le yì bǎ yǔsǎn.)
   - English: I bought you an umbrella.
   - note 1: 一把 yì bǎ: 一 is said yì before the dipping 把.
   - note 2: 雨伞 yǔsǎn: two third tones in a row, so 雨 rises (say yú sǎn).
C17. 他一直在看书，一点也不累。  (Tā yìzhí zài kàn shū, yì diǎn yě bú lèi.)
   - English: He has been reading all along and is not tired at all.
   - note 1: 一直 yìzhí: 一 is said yì before the rising 直.
   - note 2: 一点 yì diǎn: 一 is said yì before the dipping 点.
C18. 你今年多大？我今年二十岁。  (Nǐ jīnnián duō dà? Wǒ jīnnián èrshí suì.)
   - English: How old are you this year? I am twenty this year.
   - note 1: 多大 duō dà: high and flat, then a fall.
   - note 2: 二十岁 èrshí suì: a fall, a rise, then a fall.
C19. 昨天晚上我看了一个很有意思的电影。  (Zuótiān wǎnshang wǒ kàn le yí ge hěn yǒuyìsi de diànyǐng.)
   - English: Last night I watched a very interesting film.
   - note 1: 有意思 yǒuyìsi: the last syllable is light and short.
   - note 2: 很有 hěn yǒu: two third tones in a row, so 很 rises (say hén yǒu).
C20. 你为什么没去上课？我生病了。  (Nǐ wèishénme méi qù shàngkè? Wǒ shēngbìng le.)
   - English: Why did you not go to class? I fell ill.
   - note 1: 为什么 wèishénme: a fall, a rise, then a light 么.
   - note 2: 生病 shēngbìng: a high tone, then a fall.
C21. 这个周末我们去爬山，你想去吗？  (Zhège zhōumò wǒmen qù pá shān, nǐ xiǎng qù ma?)
   - English: This weekend we are going to climb a mountain; do you want to go?
   - note 1: 周末 zhōumò: a high tone, then a fall.
   - note 2: 爬山 pá shān: a rise, then a high tone.
C22. 今天比昨天冷多了，多穿一点吧。  (Jīntiān bǐ zuótiān lěng duō le, duō chuān yì diǎn ba.)
   - English: Today is much colder than yesterday; wear a bit more.
   - note 1: 冷多了 lěng duō le: 多了 after an adjective means much more.
   - note 2: 一点 yì diǎn: 一 is said yì before the dipping 点.
C23. 请再说一遍，我没听懂。  (Qǐng zài shuō yí biàn, wǒ méi tīng dǒng.)
   - English: Please say it once more; I did not understand.
   - note 1: 一遍 yí biàn: 一 is said yí before the falling 遍.
   - note 2: 没听懂 méi tīng dǒng: a rise, a high tone, then a dip.
C24. 前面有一家很大的书店。  (Qiánmiàn yǒu yì jiā hěn dà de shūdiàn.)
   - English: There is a very big bookstore ahead.
   - note 1: 一家 yì jiā: 一 is said yì before the high tone 家.
   - note 2: 前面 qiánmiàn: a rise, then a fall.
