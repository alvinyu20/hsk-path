Hello! These are word groups from my HSK study site (HSK 3.0, levels 1 to 5). In each group the words used to have exactly the same English meaning, so learners could not tell them apart. Each word now has a distinguishing label (in brackets) and a usage note saying when to use which, with short examples.
Please act as a native speaker and Chinese teacher. **Report only the problems**, no praise. For each problem give: group number / the word / what is wrong (simple Chinese plus one line of English) / a more accurate version.
Please watch for: 1) a wrong distinction (a word called written, northern or spoken when it is not); 2) unnatural or wrong examples; 3) English that does not match the Chinese; 4) a more important difference that was missed; 5) words that are in fact interchangeable where the note says they are not, or the reverse. ## 81. sweat

- **汗** (hàn, HSK 4): sweat (everyday: 出汗)
  - Note: 汗 is the everyday word: 出汗 (to sweat), 一身汗 (covered in sweat).
  - Example: 运动以后，我出了很多汗。 / After exercising, I sweated a lot.
- **汗水** (hànshuǐ, HSK 5): sweat (descriptive, written)
  - Note: 汗水 is sweat. It is also used for hard work: 用汗水换来成功 (to earn success through sweat).
  - Example: 他的脸上都是汗水。 / His face is covered in sweat.

## 82. taste; smell

- **味** (wèi, HSK 4): taste; smell (in words: 香味, 辣味)
  - Note: 味 is mostly used in words such as 味道, 香味 and 辣味.
  - Example: 这个菜有一点辣味。 / This dish has a little spicy taste.
- **味道** (wèidào, HSK 4): taste; smell (everyday word)
  - Example: 这个菜的味道很好。 / This dish tastes very good.

## 83. theatre

- **剧院** (jùyuàn, HSK 4): theatre (the building; often in names)
  - Note: 剧院 is a theatre, and the word often appears in names: 大剧院 (grand theatre). 剧场 is the hall where a show is put on. A theatre company (the group of actors) has a different word.
  - Example: 这个剧院很大。 / This theatre is very big.
- **剧场** (jùchǎng, HSK 5): theatre (the hall where shows are on)
  - Note: 剧场 is a theatre building where plays and shows are put on. A cinema is 电影院.
  - Example: 我们去剧场看表演。 / We go to the theatre to watch a performance.

## 84. to congratulate

- **祝贺** (zhùhè, HSK 4): to congratulate (formal)
  - Note: 祝贺 is a little formal and also used in writing: 祝贺你! 祝贺你考上了大学!
  - Example: 祝贺你考上了大学！ / Congratulations to you on getting into university!
- **恭喜** (gōngxǐ, HSK 5): to congratulate (spoken)
  - Note: 恭喜 is said to congratulate someone: 恭喜你, 恭喜你们. Add the reason after it: 恭喜你毕业. 恭喜 is more spoken and warmer than 祝贺, which is a little formal.
  - Example: 恭喜你，你考上大学了！ / Congratulations, you have got into university!

## 85. to cough

- **咳** (ké, HSK 4): to cough (short; in 咳嗽)
  - Note: 咳 alone is short, for a cough or two: 咳了几声 (coughed a few times). For a cough that goes on, say 咳嗽: 我咳嗽了两天了.
  - Example: 他咳了几声。 / He coughed a few times.
- **咳嗽** (késou, HSK 4): to cough (everyday word)
  - Note: 咳嗽 is read késou, with a light second syllable.
  - Example: 我感冒了，一直咳嗽。 / I have a cold and keep coughing.

## 86. to exceed; to overtake

- **超过** (chāoguò, HSK 4): to exceed; to overtake (everyday)
  - Example: 这辆车超过了前面的出租车。 / This car passed the taxi in front.
- **超** (chāo, HSK 5): to exceed; to overtake (short, spoken)
  - Note: 超 means to go beyond or pass. It is also a prefix, as in 超市 (supermarket).
  - Example: 这辆车超了前面的那辆车。 / This car overtook the car in front.

## 87. to lack

- **缺少** (quēshǎo, HSK 4): to lack (be short of)
  - Note: 缺少 is to be short of something needed: 缺少水, 缺少一个人.
  - Example: 这个城市缺少水。 / This city lacks water.
- **缺乏** (quēfá, HSK 5): to lack (formal; experience, skills)
  - Note: 缺乏 is to lack: 缺乏经验 (to lack experience), 缺乏运动 (to lack exercise). It is more formal than 缺少.
  - Example: 他缺乏经验。 / He lacks experience.

## 88. to lose

- **失去** (shīqù, HSK 4): to lose (something you had)
  - Note: 失去 is to lose something you had, often something important: 失去工作 (to lose a job). To lose a game is 输.
  - Example: 他失去了工作。 / He lost his job.
- **输** (shū, HSK 4): to lose (a game or a bet)
  - Note: 输 is to lose a game or a match: 我们队输了. To lose something you had is 失去.
  - Example: 我们队输了。 / Our team lost.

## 89. to make; to cause

- **使** (shǐ, HSK 4): to make; to cause (formal)
  - Note: 使 is formal: 这个消息使大家很高兴. In speech, 让 is used: 让大家很高兴.
  - Example: 这个消息使大家很高兴。 / This news makes everyone happy.
- **令** (lìng, HSK 5): to make; to cause (in 令人 + a feeling)
  - Note: 令 is "to make, to cause", almost always as 令人 + a feeling: 令人高兴 (pleasing), 令人吃惊 (surprising).
  - Example: 这个消息令人高兴。 / This news makes people happy.
- **使得** (shǐde, HSK 5): to make; to cause (written, + a result)
  - Note: 使得 means "to make, to cause" and is followed by a result: 使得大家很高兴 (to make everyone happy). It is formal; 让 is the everyday word.
  - Example: 大雨使得路很难走。 / Heavy rain makes the road hard to walk on.

## 90. to mention

- **提到** (tídào, HSK 4): to mention (say in passing)
  - Note: 提到 is to mention something while talking or writing: 他没有提到这件事.
  - Example: 他没有提到这件事。 / He did not mention this matter.
- **提起** (tíqǐ, HSK 5): to mention (bring up a topic)
  - Note: 提起 is to bring up a topic: 提起这件事 (to mention this matter), 不要提起 (do not bring it up).
  - Example: 提起这件事，他就很生气。 / Whenever this matter is mentioned, he gets angry.

## 91. to pay

- **付** (fù, HSK 4): to pay (everyday: 付钱)
  - Example: 请在这里付钱。 / Please pay here.
- **支付** (zhīfù, HSK 4): to pay (formal: ways of paying)
  - Note: 支付 is formal, about how you pay: 手机支付 (paying by phone). In speech, 付 is common: 付钱 (to pay).
  - Example: 你可以用手机支付。 / You can pay with your phone.

## 92. to print

- **打印** (dǎyìn, HSK 4): to print (with a printer)
  - Example: 请把这份文件打印出来。 / Please print out this document.
- **印刷** (yìnshuā, HSK 5): to print (books, with a press)
  - Note: 印刷 is to print books and papers with a press: 印刷书 (to print books). 打印 is to print from a computer.
  - Example: 这本书印刷得很好。 / This book is very well printed.

## 93. to save; to economise

- **节约** (jiéyuē, HSK 4): to save; to economise (not waste)
  - Note: 节约 is about not wasting things, often as a good habit: 节约用水 (to save water). For saving money in speech, people say 省钱.
  - Example: 我们要节约用水。 / We need to save water.
- **节省** (jiéshěng, HSK 5): to save; to economise (use less)
  - Note: 节省 is to use less money, time or things: 节省时间, 节省用水 (to save water). 省 is the shorter everyday word.
  - Example: 坐地铁可以节省时间。 / Taking the underground can save time.

## 94. tomato

- **西红柿** (xīhóngshì, HSK 4): tomato (common in the north)
  - Note: 西红柿 is read xīhóngshì.
  - Example: 我喜欢吃西红柿鸡蛋面。 / I like eating tomato and egg noodles.
- **番茄** (fānqié, HSK 5): tomato (common in the south)
  - Note: 番茄 is the tomato. People also say 西红柿 in speech.
  - Example: 我喜欢吃番茄炒鸡蛋。 / I like eating stir-fried tomatoes with eggs.

## 95. unexpectedly; actually

- **竟然** (jìngrán, HSK 4): unexpectedly; actually (surprise; a bit more in writing)
  - Note: 竟然 shows surprise that something happened: 他竟然忘了. 竟然 and 居然 are very close and both are common in speech and writing; 竟然 is a little more common in writing.
  - Example: 他竟然忘了自己的生日。 / He actually forgot his own birthday.
- **居然** (jūrán, HSK 5): unexpectedly; actually (surprise; very close to 竟然)
  - Note: 居然 shows surprise that something happened or did not: 居然不知道 (to be surprised that someone does not know). 居然 and 竟然 are very close and both are common in speech and writing.
  - Example: 他居然忘了自己的生日。 / He actually forgot his own birthday.

## 96. window

- **窗** (chuāng, HSK 4): window (in words: 窗外, 车窗)
  - Note: 窗 is mostly used inside longer words: 窗户, 窗外, 车窗.
  - Example: 他看着窗外的雨。 / He is looking at the rain outside the window.
- **窗户** (chuānghu, HSK 4): window (on its own)
  - Note: 窗户 is the window on its own: 打开窗户 (to open the window). 窗 is mostly used inside words: 窗外 (outside the window).
  - Example: 外面太热了，请把窗户关上。 / It is too hot outside; please close the window.

## 97. ancient; old

- **古** (gǔ, HSK 5): ancient; old (short, in words)
  - Note: 古 means ancient. It is mostly used inside words: 古代, 古老, 古书, 古人.
  - Example: 这个城市有很多古树。 / This city has many ancient trees.
- **古老** (gǔlǎo, HSK 5): ancient; old (with a long history)
  - Note: 古老 describes something with a very long history: 古老的文化 (an ancient culture).
  - Example: 这是一座古老的城市。 / This is an ancient city.

## 98. children (sons and daughters)

- **儿女** (érnǚ, HSK 5): children (sons and daughters; neutral)
  - Note: 儿女 means sons and daughters, usually when talking about someone's grown-up children. 孩子 is any child.
  - Example: 他的儿女都在国外工作。 / His children all work abroad.
- **子女** (zǐnǚ, HSK 5): children (sons and daughters; formal)
  - Note: 子女 are sons and daughters, in a formal way: 他的子女 (his children), 教育子女 (to educate one's children), 两个子女 (two children).
  - Example: 他的子女都在国外。 / His children are all abroad.

## 99. deep; profound

- **深厚** (shēnhòu, HSK 5): deep; profound (feelings, friendship)
  - Note: 深厚 describes deep, strong feelings: 深厚的感情 (deep feelings), 深厚的友谊 (deep friendship).
  - Example: 他们之间有很深厚的感情。 / There is a deep bond between them.
- **深刻** (shēnkè, HSK 5): deep; profound (impressions, thinking)
  - Note: 深刻 means deep and strong, about impressions and thinking: 深刻的印象 (a deep impression), 理解深刻 (a deep understanding).
  - Example: 这次旅行给我留下了深刻的印象。 / This trip left a deep impression on me.

## 100. gradually

- **渐渐** (jiànjiàn, HSK 5): gradually (in descriptions and stories)
  - Note: 渐渐 means slowly and step by step, and is typical of descriptions and stories rather than chat. It is often followed by 了 to show a change: 渐渐好了, 渐渐明白了. In everyday speech people often say 慢慢: 天气慢慢冷了.
  - Example: 天气渐渐冷了。 / The weather is gradually getting cold.
- **逐渐** (zhújiàn, HSK 5): gradually (step by step, more formal)
  - Note: 逐渐 is "gradually", for a change that happens by itself: 逐渐变好 (to get better gradually), 逐渐明白 (to understand gradually), 逐渐增多 (to increase gradually).
  - Example: 天气逐渐变冷了。 / The weather has gradually become cold.

## 101. maternal grandfather

- **姥爷** (lǎoye, HSK 5): maternal grandfather (in the north)
  - Note: 姥爷 is your mother's father, used in the north of China. In the south people say 外公. 爷爷 is your father's father.
  - Example: 我姥爷每天早上去公园锻炼。 / My maternal grandfather goes to the park to exercise every morning.
- **外公** (wàigōng, HSK 5): maternal grandfather (in the south)
  - Note: 外公 is your mother's father: 外公外婆 (maternal grandparents). Your father's father is 爷爷.
  - Example: 我外公住在上海，我常常去看他。 / My maternal grandfather lives in Shanghai, and I often go to see him.

## 102. maternal grandmother

- **姥姥** (lǎolao, HSK 5): maternal grandmother (in the north)
  - Note: 姥姥 is your mother's mother, used in the north of China. In the south people say 外婆. 奶奶 is your father's mother.
  - Example: 我姥姥住在北方。 / My maternal grandmother lives in the north.
- **外婆** (wàipó, HSK 5): maternal grandmother (in the south)
  - Note: 外婆 is your mother's mother: 外婆家 (your maternal grandmother's home). Your father's mother is 奶奶.
  - Example: 外婆做的菜很好吃。 / The food my maternal grandmother makes is very tasty.

## 103. obvious; clear

- **明显** (míngxiǎn, HSK 5): obvious; clear (easy to see)
  - Note: 明显 means obvious, easy to notice: 明显的变化 (an obvious change), 很明显 (it is obvious), 不明显 (not obvious).
  - Example: 他的进步很明显。 / His progress is very obvious.
- **显然** (xiǎnrán, HSK 5): obvious; clear (as a conclusion: obviously)
  - Note: 显然 is "obviously": 很显然 (very obvious), 显然不对 (obviously wrong).
  - Example: 他显然不想去。 / He obviously does not want to go.

## 104. platform

- **平台** (píngtái, HSK 5): platform (online, or a raised floor)
  - Note: 平台 is a platform: 网络平台 (an online platform), 学习平台 (a learning platform). It is also a raised flat place.
  - Example: 这个网上平台有很多课程。 / This online platform has many courses.
- **站台** (zhàntái, HSK 5): platform (at a train station)
  - Note: 站台 is the platform at a station: 在站台上 (on the platform), 火车站台 (a train platform), 第一站台 (platform one).
  - Example: 请在站台上等火车。 / Please wait for the train on the platform.

## 105. since

- **以来** (yǐlái, HSK 5): since (after the time: …以来)
  - Note: 以来 is "since", placed after the time: 来中国以来 (since coming to China), 今年以来 (since the start of this year).
  - Example: 来中国以来，我学了很多汉语。 / Since coming to China, I have learned a lot of Chinese.
- **自从** (zìcóng, HSK 5): since (before the time: 自从…)
  - Note: 自从 is "since" a point in the past: 自从…以后 (ever since …), 自从去年 (since last year), 自从那天 (since that day).
  - Example: 自从来到这里，他每天都很忙。 / Since coming here, he has been busy every day.

## 106. tears

- **泪水** (lèishuǐ, HSK 5): tears (literary)
  - Note: 泪水 is tears as a mass, a little more literary than 眼泪: 泪水流下来 (tears fall).
  - Example: 她的脸上都是泪水。 / Her face is covered in tears.
- **眼泪** (yǎnlèi, HSK 5): tears (everyday)
  - Note: 眼泪 is tears: 流眼泪 (to shed tears), 擦眼泪 (to wipe away tears).
  - Example: 她的眼泪流下来了。 / Tears ran down her face.

## 107. to be good at

- **擅长** (shàncháng, HSK 5): to be good at (+ a skill or subject)
  - Note: 擅长 means to be good at a skill: 擅长数学 (good at maths), 擅长唱歌 (good at singing). It is more formal than 会.
  - Example: 她擅长画画。 / She is good at drawing.
- **善于** (shànyú, HSK 5): to be good at (+ a verb; written)
  - Note: 善于 means to be good at doing something, and is followed by a verb: 善于学习 (good at learning), 善于表达 (good at expressing). 擅长 can also be followed by a noun.
  - Example: 他善于讲故事。 / He is good at telling stories.

## 108. to build; to construct

- **建造** (jiànzào, HSK 5): to build; to construct (houses, ships)
  - Note: 建造 is to build a large thing such as a bridge, a house or a ship: 建造房子, 建造大桥.
  - Example: 他们用了两年建造这座桥。 / They took two years to build this bridge.
- **修建** (xiūjiàn, HSK 5): to build; to construct (roads, bridges)
  - Note: 修建 is to build big things such as roads and bridges: 修建公路 (to build a road), 修建桥 (to build a bridge).
  - Example: 政府要在这里修建一座桥。 / The government will build a bridge here.

## 109. to carry out; to implement

- **实施** (shíshī, HSK 5): to carry out; to implement (a plan, a law)
  - Note: 实施 is to carry out a plan or rule: 实施计划 (to carry out a plan), 开始实施 (to start being carried out). 实行 is to practise a system; 实现 is to make a goal come true.
  - Example: 公司实施了新的规定。 / The company has carried out new rules.
- **执行** (zhíxíng, HSK 5): to carry out; to implement (orders, rules)
  - Note: 执行 is to carry out an order, a plan or a rule: 执行规定 (to carry out a rule), 执行计划 (to carry out a plan), 严格执行 (to carry out strictly).
  - Example: 大家都要执行这个规定。 / Everyone must carry out this rule.

## 110. to change; to shift

- **变动** (biàndòng, HSK 5): to change; to shift (plans, times, prices)
  - Note: 变动 is also a noun, "a change".
  - Example: 飞机的时间有变动，请注意。 / The flight time has changed; please pay attention.
- **转变** (zhuǎnbiàn, HSK 5): to change; to shift (ideas, attitudes)
  - Note: 转变 is a change in thinking or in a situation: 转变想法 (to change one's ideas), 转变态度 (to change one's attitude), 一个转变 (a change).
  - Example: 他的想法转变了。 / His ideas have changed.

## 111. to contain; to include

- **包含** (bāohán, HSK 5): to contain; to include (is part of it)
  - Note: 包含 is for what is part of something. 包括 lists the items.
  - Example: 这个价格包含早饭。 / This price includes breakfast.
- **含有** (hányǒu, HSK 5): to contain; to include (has inside)
  - Note: 含有 is used for a thing that has something inside it: 含有糖 (contains sugar), 含有很多信息 (contains a lot of information). The negative is 不含有.
  - Example: 这本书含有很多有用的信息。 / This book contains a lot of useful information.

## 112. to describe

- **描述** (miáoshù, HSK 5): to describe (give an account)
  - Note: 描述 is to describe in words: 描述得很清楚 (described very clearly), 描述一下 (to describe a little).
  - Example: 请描述一下你的家。 / Please describe your home.
- **形容** (xíngróng, HSK 5): to describe (how it looks or feels)
  - Note: 形容 is to describe how something looks or feels: 形容一下 (to describe a little), 无法形容 (impossible to describe).
  - Example: 你能形容一下那个人吗？ / Can you describe that person?

## 113. to form; to make up

- **构成** (gòuchéng, HSK 5): to form; to make up (what it consists of)
  - Note: 构成 is to form or make up: 由……构成 means "to be made up of".
  - Example: 这个句子由五个词构成。 / This sentence is made up of five words.
- **组成** (zǔchéng, HSK 5): to form; to make up (parts come together)
  - Note: 组成 is to make up a whole from parts: 由…组成 (to be made up of …), 组成一个队 (to form a team), 组成小组 (to form a group).
  - Example: 这个队由十个人组成。 / This team is made up of ten people.

## 114. to have; to possess

- **具备** (jùbèi, HSK 5): to have; to possess (the needed conditions)
  - Note: 具备 is to have the needed qualities or conditions: 具备条件 (to meet the conditions), 具备能力 (to have the ability).
  - Example: 他具备这个工作需要的条件。 / He has the conditions this job needs.
- **具有** (jùyǒu, HSK 5): to have; to possess (qualities)
  - Note: 具有 is a formal "to have", used for qualities and features: 具有特点 (to have features), 具有价值 (to have value).
  - Example: 这个办法具有很多好处。 / This method has many benefits.

## 115. to prevent

- **防止** (fángzhǐ, HSK 5): to prevent (stop something bad)
  - Note: 防止 is to stop something bad before it happens: 防止事故 (prevent accidents), 防止浪费 (prevent waste).
  - Example: 我们要防止孩子玩火。 / We must prevent children from playing with fire.
- **预防** (yùfáng, HSK 5): to prevent (in advance: illness)
  - Note: 预防 is to prevent in advance: 预防感冒 (to prevent colds), 预防生病 (to prevent illness).
  - Example: 多洗手可以预防感冒。 / Washing hands often can prevent colds.

## 116. to regard as

- **看作** (kànzuò, HSK 5): to regard as (spoken)
  - Note: 看作 is to think of something as something else: 把 A 看作 B (to regard A as B). It is close to 当作.
  - Example: 他把我看作自己的朋友。 / He regards me as his own friend.
- **视为** (shìwéi, HSK 5): to regard as (written)
  - Note: 视为 means "to regard as", in writing: 视为朋友 (to regard as a friend), 把……视为 (to treat … as). It is formal.
  - Example: 大家把他视为朋友。 / Everyone regards him as a friend.

## 117. to rise; to go up

- **上升** (shàngshēng, HSK 5): to rise; to go up (numbers, temperature)
  - Note: 上升 is to go up, for numbers, levels and temperature: 价格上升 (prices rise), 温度上升 (the temperature rises). The opposite is 下降.
  - Example: 温度在慢慢上升。 / The temperature is rising slowly.
- **升** (shēng, HSK 5): to rise; to go up (the sun, a flag)
  - Note: 升 is to rise: 升起来 (to rise up), 升高 (to rise in height), 升级 (to upgrade). It is also a litre: 一升水 (a litre of water).
  - Example: 太阳从东边升起来了。 / The sun has risen from the east.

## 118. to search

- **搜** (sōu, HSK 5): to search (short, spoken)
  - Note: 搜 is to search, mostly used on a phone or computer: 搜一下 (to search it), 搜一搜 (to have a search). 找 is "to look for"; 搜 needs a search tool.
  - Example: 他在手机上搜了一下。 / He did a search on his phone.
- **搜索** (sōusuǒ, HSK 5): to search (the fuller word)
  - Note: 搜索 is to search for information: 搜索信息 (to search for information), 网上搜索 (to search online). It is the longer, more formal form of 搜.
  - Example: 请在网上搜索这个问题。 / Please search for this question online.

## 119. to sign

- **签** (qiān, HSK 5): to sign (short; also 签名)
  - Note: 签 is to sign: 签名 (to sign one's name), 签字, 签合同 (to sign a contract). It is also a stick used for drawing lots.
  - Example: 请在这里签个字。 / Please sign here.
- **签字** (qiānzì, HSK 5): to sign (a document)
  - Note: 签字 is to sign a document: 请签字 (please sign), 领导签字 (the leader's signature). 签名 stresses your name; 签字 stresses the act of signing.
  - Example: 他在合同上签字。 / He signs the contract.

## 120. to train; training

- **培训** (péixùn, HSK 5): to train; training (for a job)
  - Note: 培训 is to train people for a job: 参加培训 (to attend training), 培训班 (a training class), 员工培训 (staff training). It is a verb and a noun.
  - Example: 公司给新人做培训。 / The company gives training to new people.
- **训练** (xùnliàn, HSK 5): to train; training (practice, sports)
  - Note: 训练 is to train, or training: 训练运动员 (to train athletes), 每天的训练 (daily training).
  - Example: 运动员每天都要训练。 / The athletes must train every day.
