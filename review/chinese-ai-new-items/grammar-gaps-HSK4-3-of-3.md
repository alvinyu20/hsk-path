## L4-11. Can and never: 吃得了, 再也不, 怎么也…不  (new lesson)
New grammar points: verb + 得了 / 不了 (can manage or not); 再也不 / 再也没 (never again); 怎么都 / 也 + 不 / 没 (no matter how)

**Explanation (new part):**
L4-11.E1. Verb + 得了 / 不了 says whether you can manage it: 这么多菜，我们吃得了吗？ 'Can we finish this much food?'; 明天我去不了 'I can't go tomorrow'. Here 了 is read liǎo.
L4-11.E2. 再也不 / 再也没 means 'never again': 我保证再也不迟到了 'I promise never to be late again'; 我再也没见过他 'I never saw him again'.
L4-11.E3. 怎么都 / 怎么也 + 不 / 没 means 'however hard, still not': 我怎么也想不起来他的名字 'I just can't remember his name'; 他们怎么都找不到 'However hard they looked, they couldn't find it'.

**Examples (new):**
L4-11.X1. 这么多菜，我们吃得了吗？  (Zhème duō cài, wǒmen chī de liǎo ma?)  — Can we finish this much food?
L4-11.X2. 明天我去不了。  (Míngtiān wǒ qù bù liǎo.)  — I can't go tomorrow.
L4-11.X3. 我保证再也不迟到了。  (Wǒ bǎozhèng zài yě bù chídào le.)  — I promise never to be late again.
L4-11.X4. 我怎么也想不起来他的名字。  (Wǒ zěnme yě xiǎng bù qǐlái tā de míngzi.)  — I just can't think of his name.

**"Watch out" notes (new):**
L4-11.M1. 再也 goes before 不 / 没: 再也不, not 不再也.
L4-11.M2. In 得了 / 不了 the 了 is liǎo, not le.

## L4-12. Spoken patterns (1): 有什么好怕的, 他是他，我是我, 说就是了  (new lesson)
New grammar points: 有什么好 … 的 (there is nothing to …); X是X，Y是Y (他是他，我是我); X也得X，不X也得X (like it or not); X就是了 (just do it); 还 + noun + 呢 (call that a …?); verb + 一X是一X (过一天是一天)

**Explanation (new part):**
L4-12.E1. 有什么(好) + verb or adjective + 的 means 'there is nothing to …': 有什么好害怕的？ 'What is there to be afraid of?'.
L4-12.E2. X是X，Y是Y keeps two things apart: 他是他，我是我 'He is he and I am I'; 工作是工作，生活是生活 'Work is work and life is life'.
L4-12.E3. X也得X，不X也得X means 'you have to, like it or not': 你去也得去，不去也得去.
L4-12.E4. X就是了 at the end means 'just do it, that's all': 有什么想法，说就是了 'If you have an idea, just say it'.
L4-12.E5. 还 + noun + 呢 mocks something that does not live up to its name: 还大饭店呢，菜这么难吃 'Call this a big restaurant? The food is so bad'.
L4-12.E6. Verb + 一X是一X means 'one at a time, with no plan': 过一天是一天 'live one day at a time'; 走一步是一步 'take it one step at a time'.

**Examples (new):**
L4-12.X1. 有什么好害怕的？  (Yǒu shénme hǎo hàipà de?)  — What is there to be afraid of?
L4-12.X2. 他是他，我是我。  (Tā shì tā, wǒ shì wǒ.)  — He is he and I am I.
L4-12.X3. 你去也得去，不去也得去。  (Nǐ qù yě děi qù, bú qù yě děi qù.)  — You have to go, like it or not.
L4-12.X4. 有什么想法，说就是了。  (Yǒu shénme xiǎngfǎ, shuō jiùshì le.)  — If you have an idea, just say it.
L4-12.X5. 还大饭店呢，菜这么难吃。  (Hái dà fàndiàn ne, cài zhème nán chī.)  — Some big restaurant this is: the food is so bad.
L4-12.X6. 我们只能走一步是一步。  (Wǒmen zhǐnéng zǒu yí bù shì yí bù.)  — We can only take it one step at a time.

**"Watch out" notes (new):**
L4-12.M1. These are casual spoken patterns; don't use them in formal writing.
L4-12.M2. 还…呢 is sarcastic: it criticises.

**Lesson exercises (new):**

   - wrong: 你去也去，不去也去。

## L4-13. Spoken patterns (2): 你吃你的, 买就买吧, 便宜是便宜  (new lesson)
New grammar points: 你X你的 (go ahead, don't mind me); 让你X你就X (just do as you are told); 说什么也得X (whatever happens); X就X吧 (fine, then); X是X，就是 / 可是… (便宜是便宜，就是…)

**Explanation (new part):**
L4-13.E1. 你 + verb + 你的(吧) means 'go ahead with yours, don't mind me': 我不饿，你吃你的吧 'I'm not hungry, go ahead and eat'.
L4-13.E2. 让你X你就X means 'just do as you are told': 老师让你多练，你就多练.
L4-13.E3. 说什么也得X means 'whatever happens, must': 他病了，我说什么也得去看看他 'He's ill; I must go and see him no matter what'. With 不 it means 'won't, whatever you say': 他说什么也不同意.
L4-13.E4. X就X吧 accepts something: 'fine, then': 买就买吧，也不太贵 'Fine, buy it then; it's not that expensive'.
L4-13.E5. X是X，就是 / 可是… admits one point and then adds a 'but': 这些衣服便宜是便宜，就是质量不太好 'These clothes are cheap all right, but the quality isn't great'.

**Examples (new):**
L4-13.X1. 我不饿，你吃你的吧。  (Wǒ bú è, nǐ chī nǐ de ba.)  — I'm not hungry, you go ahead and eat.
L4-13.X2. 老师让你多练，你就多练。  (Lǎoshī ràng nǐ duō liàn, nǐ jiù duō liàn.)  — If the teacher tells you to practise more, just practise more.
L4-13.X3. 他病了，我说什么也得去看看他。  (Tā bìng le, wǒ shuō shénme yě děi qù kànkan tā.)  — He's ill; I must go and see him, whatever happens.
L4-13.X4. 买就买吧，也不太贵。  (Mǎi jiù mǎi ba, yě bú tài guì.)  — Fine, buy it then; it's not that expensive.
L4-13.X5. 这些衣服便宜是便宜，就是质量不太好。  (Zhèxiē yīfu piányi shì piányi, jiùshì zhìliàng bú tài hǎo.)  — These clothes are cheap all right, but the quality isn't great.

**"Watch out" notes (new):**
L4-13.M1. In X是X the same word comes twice with nothing added: 便宜是便宜, not 便宜是很便宜.
