## L4-6. Frames: 在…方面, 为了…而… and 拿…来说  (new lesson)
New grammar points: 在…方面 (when it comes to); 为了…而… (for the sake of); 拿…来说 (take … for example)

**Explanation (new part):**
L4-6.E1. 在…方面 means 'when it comes to, in the area of': 在工作方面，他很有经验 'When it comes to work, he is very experienced'. It is a little more formal than 在…上.
L4-6.E2. 为了…而… means 'do something for the sake of …': 他为了家人而努力工作 'He works hard for his family'. 而 links the reason to the action and sounds written.
L4-6.E3. 拿…来说 means 'take … for example': 我的朋友都很忙，拿我姐姐来说，她每天工作十个小时 'My friends are all busy. Take my sister: she works ten hours a day'.

**Examples (new):**
L4-6.X1. 在工作方面，他很有经验。  (Zài gōngzuò fāngmiàn, tā hěn yǒu jīngyàn.)  — When it comes to work, he is very experienced.

**"Watch out" notes (new):**
L4-6.M1. 在…方面 needs 方面 at the end: 在工作方面.

## L4-7. 即使…也…, 就是…也… and 尽管…: even if, although  (new section in an existing lesson)

## L4-8. Four-word patterns: 一A一B, 无A无B and 有A有B  (new lesson)
New grammar points: 一A一B (一字一句, 一前一后); 无A无B (无儿无女); 有A有B (有说有笑)

**Explanation (new part):**
L4-8.E1. 一A一B puts 一 before two related words: 一字一句 'word by word'; 一前一后 'one in front, one behind'; 一大一小 'one big, one small'.
L4-8.E2. 无A无B means 'without either': 无儿无女 'with no children'.
L4-8.E3. 有A有B means 'with both, plenty of': 有说有笑 'chatting and laughing'; 有山有水 'with hills and water'; 有吃有喝 'plenty to eat and drink'.

**Examples (new):**
L4-8.X1. 她一字一句说得很清楚。  (Tā yí zì yí jù shuō de hěn qīngchu.)  — She says every word clearly.
L4-8.X2. 他们一前一后走进了教室。  (Tāmen yì qián yí hòu zǒu jìn le jiàoshì.)  — They walked into the classroom one behind the other.
L4-8.X3. 那位老人无儿无女。  (Nà wèi lǎorén wú ér wú nǚ.)  — That old man has no children.
L4-8.X4. 同学们有说有笑地走出了教室。  (Tóngxuémen yǒu shuō yǒu xiào de zǒu chū le jiàoshì.)  — The students walked out of the classroom chatting and laughing.

## L4-9. Handy phrases: 有的是, 说不定, 不怎么, 就是说  (new lesson)
New grammar points: 有的是 (plenty of); 说不定 (maybe); 不怎么 + verb / adjective (not much); (这)就是说 (that is to say)

**Explanation (new part):**
L4-9.E1. 有的是 means 'plenty of': 别着急，我们有的是时间 'Don't worry, we have plenty of time'. Don't mix it up with 有的…, 'some': 有的人 'some people'.
L4-9.E2. 说不定 means 'maybe, it could be': 他说不定已经到家了 'He may be home already'; 客人来不来还说不定 'It is not certain yet whether the guests will come'.
L4-9.E3. 不怎么 + verb or adjective means 'not much, not very': 我不怎么喜欢喝咖啡 'I don't much like coffee'; 这个菜不怎么辣 'This dish is not very spicy'. Compare 不怎么样 'not great'.
L4-9.E4. (这)就是说 means 'that is to say, in other words': 他没有直接拒绝，这就是说，你还有机会 'He didn't turn you down directly; that is to say, you still have a chance'.

**Examples (new):**
L4-9.X1. 别着急，我们有的是时间。  (Bié zháojí, wǒmen yǒude shì shíjiān.)  — Don't worry, we have plenty of time.
L4-9.X2. 他说不定已经到家了。  (Tā shuōbudìng yǐjīng dào jiā le.)  — He may be home already.
L4-9.X3. 我不怎么喜欢喝咖啡。  (Wǒ bù zěnme xǐhuan hē kāfēi.)  — I don't much like coffee.
L4-9.X4. 他没有直接拒绝，这就是说，你还有机会。  (Tā méiyǒu zhíjiē jùjué, zhè jiùshìshuō, nǐ hái yǒu jīhuì.)  — He didn't turn you down directly; that is to say, you still have a chance.

**"Watch out" notes (new):**
L4-9.M1. 不怎么 goes before a verb or adjective: 不怎么喜欢. On its own, say 不怎么样.

**Lesson exercises (new):**

## L4-10. 按照, 作为, 与…相比, 使, 表扬 and 选: according to, as, compared with, make  (new section in an existing lesson)
New grammar points: 表扬 / 批评 / 选 + person + verb (老师表扬我考得好, 选他当经理)

**Explanation (new part):**
L4-10.E1. Some verbs take a person and then a second verb about that person: 老师表扬我考得很好 'The teacher praised me for doing well in the exam'; 妈妈批评他回家太晚 'Mom told him off for coming home too late'; 大家选他当经理 'Everyone chose him as manager' (选 + person + 当 / 做 + role).

**Examples (new):**
L4-10.X1. 老师表扬我考得很好。  (Lǎoshī biǎoyáng wǒ kǎo de hěn hǎo.)  — The teacher praised me for doing well in the exam.
L4-10.X2. 大家选他当经理。  (Dàjiā xuǎn tā dāng jīnglǐ.)  — Everyone chose him as manager.

**"Watch out" notes (new):**
L4-10.M1. The person comes right after 表扬, 批评 or 选: 大家选他当经理, not 大家选当经理他.

**Lesson exercises (new):**
L4-10.Q1. **build** (put the words in order). 老师表扬我考得很好。  (Lǎoshī biǎoyáng wǒ kǎo de hěn hǎo.)
