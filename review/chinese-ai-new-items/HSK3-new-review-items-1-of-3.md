# HSK 3: new review items (219 review questions, 104 write-your-own prompts, 24 listening clips)


## A. Grammar review questions

Each item belongs to one grammar point. Types: **choose** (pick the sentence that says the English; option 1 is the right one), **fill** (the blank is a word), **build** (put the words in order; the sentence is the answer), **spot** (tap the extra word; the sentence with the extra word is wrong).


### g3-1  把 + object + verb  (lesson: 把: doing something to something)

A1. **choose**. English: Please turn on the light.
   - right: 请把灯打开。  (Qǐng bǎ dēng dǎkāi.)
   - wrong: 请灯把打开。
   - note shown to the learner: 把 comes before the thing, then the verb.
A2. **fill**. 他___水果洗干净了。  (Tā bǎ shuǐguǒ xǐ gānjìng le.)
   - English: He washed the fruit clean.
   - blank: 把
   - note shown to the learner: 把 + thing + verb + result.
A3. **build**. 我把书放在包里了。  (Wǒ bǎ shū fàng zài bāo lǐ le.)
   - English: I have put the book in the bag.
   - note shown to the learner: 把 + thing + verb + where.
A4. **build**. 请把这个送给他。  (Qǐng bǎ zhège sòng gěi tā.)
   - English: Please give this to him.
   - note shown to the learner: 把 + thing + verb.
A5. **spot**. sentence with the extra word: 他把饭被吃完了。
   - extra word: 被
   - correct sentence: 他把饭吃完了。  (Tā bǎ fàn chī wán le.)
   - English: He has finished the meal.
   - note shown to the learner: 把 and 被 do not go together in one sentence.
