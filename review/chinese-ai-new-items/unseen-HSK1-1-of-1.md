## More word examples

1. 我想喝一点儿水。  /  I want to drink a little water.
2. 请再说一点儿。  /  Please say a little more.
3. 这个菜有一点儿贵。  /  This dish is a bit expensive.
4. 这个菜有点儿热。  /  This dish is a bit hot.
5. 下午有点儿冷。  /  It is a bit cold in the afternoon.
6. 你在这儿，我很高兴。  /  I am glad you are here.
7. 这儿有一家商店。  /  There is a shop here.
8. 请坐这儿。  /  Please sit here.
9. 他在那儿。  /  He is over there.
10. 那儿有很多人。  /  There are a lot of people over there.
11. 你想去那儿吗？  /  Do you want to go there?
12. 你的朋友在哪儿？  /  Where is your friend?
13. 他住在哪儿？  /  Where does he live?
14. 你儿子在哪儿？  /  Where is your son?
15. 请问一下，学校在哪儿？  /  Excuse me, where is the school?
16. 我爱吃面条儿。  /  I love to eat noodles.
17. 那儿很好玩儿。  /  It is a lot of fun there.
18. 学汉语好玩儿吗？  /  Is learning Chinese fun?
19. 这个很好玩儿，你想玩吗？  /  This is a lot of fun. Do you want to play?
20. 这个饭店的面条儿很好吃。  /  The noodles at this restaurant are very tasty.
21. 你想吃米饭还是面条儿？  /  Do you want to eat rice or noodles?
