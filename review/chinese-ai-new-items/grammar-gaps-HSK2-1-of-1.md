## L2-1. 一…就…: as soon as  (new lesson)
New grammar points: 一…就… (as soon as)

**Explanation (new part):**
L2-1.E1. 一…就… means 'as soon as … , …': 他一下课就回家 'He goes home as soon as class is over'. 一 goes before the first action and 就 before the second.
L2-1.E2. If the two actions have different people doing them, each person comes first in their own half: 我一说，他就懂了 'As soon as I said it, he understood'.
L2-1.E3. It can also mean 'whenever': 他一累就头疼 'Whenever he is tired, he gets a headache'.

**Examples (new):**
L2-1.X1. 他一下课就回家。  (Tā yí xiàkè jiù huí jiā.)  — He goes home as soon as class is over.
L2-1.X2. 我一到家就给你打电话。  (Wǒ yí dào jiā jiù gěi nǐ dǎ diànhuà.)  — I will call you as soon as I get home.
L2-1.X3. 我一说，他就懂了。  (Wǒ yì shuō, tā jiù dǒng le.)  — As soon as I said it, he understood.
L2-1.X4. 他一累就头疼。  (Tā yí lèi jiù tóu téng.)  — Whenever he is tired, he gets a headache.

**"Watch out" notes (new):**
L2-1.M1. 一 comes after the person: 他一下课就回家, not 一他下课就回家.
L2-1.M2. Do not leave out 就: 我一到家就给你打电话.

**Lesson exercises (new):**

L2-1.Q2. **build** (put the words in order). 我一到家就给你打电话。  (Wǒ yí dào jiā jiù gěi nǐ dǎ diànhuà.)

   - right: 我一说，他就懂了。  (Wǒ yì shuō, tā jiù dǒng le.)
   - wrong: 一我说，他就懂了。
   - note shown to the learner: 一 comes after the person, not before.
L2-1.Q5. **fill**. 我___看就知道了。  (Wǒ yí kàn jiù zhīdào le.)

## L2-2. 已经…了, 都…了, 还没…呢 and 就: already, not yet, sooner than expected  (new section in an existing lesson)
New grammar points: 还没…呢 (not yet); 都…了 (already, so late)

**Explanation (new part):**
L2-2.E1. 都…了 also means 'already', with a feeling that it is later, older or more than you would think: 都十二点了，快睡觉吧 'It is already twelve, go to sleep'; 你都二十岁了 'You are already twenty!'. 都 goes before the time or the amount, and 了 goes at the end.
L2-2.E2. 还没…呢 means 'not yet': 他还没起床呢 'He has not got up yet'. Use 没, because it is about something that has not happened, and 呢 at the end makes it sound natural. The short answer is 还没呢 'Not yet'.

**Examples (new):**
L2-2.X1. 都十二点了，快睡觉吧。  (Dōu shí'èr diǎn le, kuài shuìjiào ba.)  — It is already twelve, go to sleep.
L2-2.X2. 他还没起床呢。  (Tā hái méi qǐchuáng ne.)  — He has not got up yet.

**"Watch out" notes (new):**
L2-2.M1. 都…了 needs the 了 at the end: 都十二点了, not 都十二点.
L2-2.M2. Do not use 了 together with 没: 我还没吃饭呢, not 我还没吃饭了呢.

**Lesson exercises (new):**
L2-2.Q1. **build** (put the words in order). 都这么晚了，你快回家吧。  (Dōu zhème wǎn le, nǐ kuài huí jiā ba.)

   - wrong: 电影已经没开始呢。
   - note shown to the learner: For "not yet" say 还没; 已经 is only for things that have happened.

## L2-3. 让, 叫, 请 and 别: asking, inviting and telling not to  (new section in an existing lesson)
New grammar points: 叫 / 请 + someone + verb

**Explanation (new part):**
L2-3.E1. 叫 and 请 work like 让, with the person right after them. 叫 + person + verb means 'tell someone to': 妈妈叫我去买水果 'Mom told me to go and buy fruit'. 请 + person + verb means 'invite' or 'politely ask': 我请你吃饭 'I am treating you to a meal'; 他请我去他家 'He invited me to his home'.

**Examples (new):**
L2-3.X1. 妈妈叫我去买水果。  (Māma jiào wǒ qù mǎi shuǐguǒ.)  — Mom told me to go and buy fruit.
L2-3.X2. 今天我请你吃饭。  (Jīntiān wǒ qǐng nǐ chī fàn.)  — Today I invite you to a meal (my treat).

**"Watch out" notes (new):**
L2-3.M1. The person comes right after 叫 or 请: 老师叫我们进来, not 老师叫进来我们.

**Lesson exercises (new):**
L2-3.Q1. **build** (put the words in order). 妈妈叫我去买水果。  (Māma jiào wǒ qù mǎi shuǐguǒ.)
   - English: Mom told me to go and buy fruit.
   - note shown to the learner: 叫 + person + what they should do.
L2-3.Q2. **choose**. Which sentence says: Today I am treating you to dinner?
   - right: 今天我请你吃晚饭。  (Jīntiān wǒ qǐng nǐ chī wǎnfàn.)
   - wrong: 今天我请吃晚饭你。
   - note shown to the learner: The person goes right after 请.

## L2-6. Split verbs: 帮忙, 睡觉, 说话  (new lesson)
New grammar points: split verbs (帮我一个忙, 说了一会儿话)

**Explanation (new part):**
L2-6.E1. Some two-character verbs are really a verb plus its object: 帮忙 (帮 'help' + 忙), 睡觉, 说话, 游泳, 跑步, 跳舞, 上课, 打电话. Other words can go between the two parts, so they are called split (separable) verbs.
L2-6.E2. 了 and 过 go after the first part: 我昨天睡了一个好觉 'I slept well last night'; 你游过泳吗？ 'Have you ever swum?'. How long or how much also goes in the middle: 我们说了一会儿话 'We chatted for a while'; 他打了一个小时的电话 'He was on the phone for an hour'.
L2-6.E3. The second part is already an object, so no other object can follow the whole verb. To say who you help, use 帮 alone or put the person in the middle: 我帮你 'I will help you'; 你能帮我一个忙吗？ 'Can you do me a favour?'.

**Examples (new):**

L2-6.X4. 他打了一个小时的电话。  (Tā dǎ le yí ge xiǎoshí de diànhuà.)  — He was on the phone for an hour.

**"Watch out" notes (new):**
L2-6.M1. No object after a split verb: say 我帮你 or 我帮你一个忙, not 我帮忙你.
L2-6.M2. 了 and how long go in the middle: 我们说了一会儿话, not 我们说话了一会儿.

## L2-7. Doubled adjectives and 多…啊!  (new lesson)
New grammar points: doubled adjectives (大大的, 慢慢地, 高高兴兴); 多 + adjective + 啊 (how …!)

**Explanation (new part):**
L2-7.E1. Saying an adjective twice makes a description more vivid: 大大的眼睛 'big eyes', 慢慢地走 'walk slowly'. One-character adjectives double as AA (大大, 高高, 慢慢, 早早); two-character ones double each character, AABB (高兴 → 高高兴兴, 漂亮 → 漂漂亮亮).
L2-7.E2. Before a noun a doubled adjective takes 的: 她有大大的眼睛 'She has big eyes'. Before a verb it takes 地: 他慢慢地走过来了 'He walked over slowly'; 孩子们高高兴兴地回家了 'The children went home happily'.
L2-7.E3. A doubled adjective already means 'quite, nicely', so do not put 很 or 非常 in front of it.
L2-7.E4. 多 + adjective + 啊 means 'how …!': 这里多漂亮啊！ 'How beautiful it is here!'; 今天多冷啊！ 'How cold it is today!'. It is an exclamation, not the question 多大？ 'how old?'.

**Examples (new):**
L2-7.X1. 她有大大的眼睛。  (Tā yǒu dàdà de yǎnjing.)  — She has big eyes.
L2-7.X2. 他慢慢地走过来了。  (Tā mànmàn de zǒu guòlái le.)  — He walked over slowly.
L2-7.X3. 孩子们高高兴兴地回家了。  (Háizimen gāogāoxìngxìng de huí jiā le.)  — The children went home happily.
L2-7.X4. 这里多漂亮啊！  (Zhèlǐ duō piàoliang a!)  — How beautiful it is here!

**"Watch out" notes (new):**
L2-7.M1. No 很 before a doubled adjective: 大大的眼睛, not 很大大的眼睛.
L2-7.M2. Two-character adjectives double each character: 高高兴兴地回家, not 高兴高兴地回家.

## L2-8. More comparing: 有 and 比 with 得  (new lesson)
New grammar points: A 有 B + adjective (as … as); 比 with 得 (她说得比我好)

**Explanation (new part):**
L2-8.E1. A 有 B + adjective means 'A is as … as B': 你有他高吗？ 'Are you as tall as him?'; 弟弟有我高了 'My younger brother is as tall as me now'. It is used most in questions. Its 'not as … as' form is the 没有 comparison you know: 我没有他高.
L2-8.E2. 有 + an amount means 'reach, be as much as': 你哥哥有三十岁吗？ 'Is your older brother thirty yet?'.
L2-8.E3. To compare how well two people do something, use 得 with 比. Both orders are right: 她比我说得好 and 她说得比我好 'She speaks better than me'. With an object, name it first: 哥哥汉语说得比我好 'My brother speaks Chinese better than me'.

**Examples (new):**
L2-8.X1. 你有他高吗？  (Nǐ yǒu tā gāo ma?)  — Are you as tall as him?
L2-8.X2. 弟弟有我高了。  (Dìdi yǒu wǒ gāo le.)  — My younger brother is as tall as me now.
L2-8.X3. 她比我说得好。  (Tā bǐ wǒ shuō de hǎo.)  — She speaks better than me.
L2-8.X4. 他跑得比我快。  (Tā pǎo de bǐ wǒ kuài.)  — He runs faster than me.

**"Watch out" notes (new):**
L2-8.M1. No 很 in these comparisons: 她说得比我好, not 她说得比我很好.
L2-8.M2. 得 goes right after the verb: 她说得比我好 or 她比我说得好, not 她说比我得好.

**Lesson exercises (new):**

## L2-9. Topic first: 她个子很高 and 什么的  (new lesson)
New grammar points: topic first (她个子很高, 这本书我看完了); 什么的 (and so on)

**Explanation (new part):**
L2-9.E1. Chinese often names the topic first and then says something about one part of it: 她个子很高 'She is tall' (her height is high); 我头疼 'I have a headache'; 他身体很好 'He is very healthy'.
L2-9.E2. The thing an action is done to can also come first, when it is what you are talking about: 这本书我看完了 'I have finished this book'; 水果都吃完了 'The fruit has all been eaten'.
L2-9.E3. 什么的 at the end of a list means 'and so on, and things like that': 我喜欢唱歌、跳舞什么的 'I like singing, dancing and things like that'. It sounds casual.
