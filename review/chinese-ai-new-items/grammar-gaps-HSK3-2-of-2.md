## L3-8. Handy phrases: 看上去, 一般来说, 不一会儿, 不怎么样  (new lesson)
New grammar points: 看上去 (looks); 一般来说 (generally speaking); 不一会儿 (before long); 不怎么样 (not great)

**Explanation (new part):**
L3-8.E1. 看上去 means 'looks, seems' (from how it looks), like 看起来: 他看上去很累 'He looks tired'; 这个问题看上去很难，其实很简单 'This problem looks hard, but it is actually simple'.
L3-8.E2. 一般来说 means 'generally speaking' and usually starts the sentence: 一般来说，早上锻炼比较好 'Generally speaking, it is better to exercise in the morning'.
L3-8.E3. 不一会儿 means 'before long, a moment later', and the next part usually has 就: 不一会儿，他就来了 'Before long, he arrived'.
L3-8.E4. 不怎么样 means 'not great, so-so', a soft way to say something is not good: 这家饭店的菜不怎么样 'The food at this restaurant is not great'.

**Examples (new):**
L3-8.X1. 他看上去很累。  (Tā kàn shàngqù hěn lèi.)  — He looks tired.
L3-8.X2. 一般来说，早上锻炼比较好。  (Yìbān lái shuō, zǎoshang duànliàn bǐjiào hǎo.)  — Generally speaking, it is better to exercise in the morning.
L3-8.X3. 不一会儿，他就来了。  (Bù yíhuìr, tā jiù lái le.)  — Before long, he arrived.
L3-8.X4. 这家饭店的菜不怎么样。  (Zhè jiā fàndiàn de cài bù zěnmeyàng.)  — The food at this restaurant is not great.

**"Watch out" notes (new):**
L3-8.M1. 看上去 is about how something looks; for your opinion use 我觉得.
L3-8.M2. 不一会儿 usually needs 就 in the next part: 不一会儿，他就来了.

**Lesson exercises (new):**

   - note shown to the learner: 不 comes before 一会儿.

## L3-9. Frames: 从…起, 在…上 and 对…来说  (new lesson)
New grammar points: 从…起 (from … on); 在…上 / 在…中; 对…来说 (for someone)

**Explanation (new part):**
L3-9.E1. 从 + time + 起 means 'from … on, starting …': 从明天起，我每天早上跑步 'From tomorrow on, I will run every morning'; 从那天起，我们就变成了好朋友 'From that day on, we became good friends'.
L3-9.E2. 在 + area + 上 means 'when it comes to, in terms of': 在学习上，老师给了我很多帮助 'With my studies, my teacher gave me a lot of help'. 在 + noun + 中 means 'in, during': 在生活中，我们会遇到很多问题 'In life, we meet many problems'.
L3-9.E3. 对 + person + 来说 means 'for someone, as far as they are concerned': 对我来说，这个机会非常重要 'For me, this chance is very important'.

**Examples (new):**
L3-9.X1. 从明天起，我每天早上跑步。  (Cóng míngtiān qǐ, wǒ měitiān zǎoshang pǎobù.)  — From tomorrow on, I will run every morning.
L3-9.X2. 在学习上，老师给了我很多帮助。  (Zài xuéxí shàng, lǎoshī gěi le wǒ hěn duō bāngzhù.)  — With my studies, my teacher gave me a lot of help.
L3-9.X3. 对我来说，这个机会非常重要。  (Duì wǒ lái shuō, zhège jīhuì fēicháng zhòngyào.)  — For me, this chance is very important.
L3-9.X4. 在生活中，我们会遇到很多问题。  (Zài shēnghuó zhōng, wǒmen huì yùdào hěn duō wèntí.)  — In life, we will meet many problems.

**"Watch out" notes (new):**
L3-9.M1. 对…来说 needs both parts: 对我来说, not 对我说 (that means 'say to me').
L3-9.M2. 从…起 needs 起 at the end of the time: 从明天起.

**Lesson exercises (new):**
L3-9.Q1. **build** (put the words in order). 从明天起，我每天早上跑步。  (Cóng míngtiān qǐ, wǒ měitiān zǎoshang pǎobù.)

   - English: For me, this chance is very important.
   - blank: 对
   - note shown to the learner: 对 + person + 来说.

## L3-10. Several describing words before a noun  (new lesson)
New grammar points: order of describing words before a noun

**Explanation (new part):**
L3-10.E1. When several words describe one noun, they all go before it. A common order is: who it belongs to or what was done to it (+ 的), then 这 / 那 + measure word, then the adjective, then the noun: 我昨天买的那条黑色的裤子 'the black trousers I bought yesterday'. The 这 / 那 + measure word part can also come first: 那条我昨天买的裤子.
L3-10.E2. Short adjectives such as 新, 大 and 小 join the noun without 的: 我的新手机 'my new phone'; 一个大蛋糕 'a big cake'.
L3-10.E3. Keep the measure word right after 这, 那 or the number: 那条黑色的裤子.

**Examples (new):**
L3-10.X1. 我昨天买的那条裤子在哪儿？  (Wǒ zuótiān mǎi de nà tiáo kùzi zài nǎr?)  — Where are the trousers I bought yesterday?
L3-10.X2. 这是我妈妈做的一个大蛋糕。  (Zhè shì wǒ māma zuò de yí ge dà dàngāo.)  — This is a big cake my mom made.

## L3-11. Results: 到, 住, 走 and 上  (new lesson)
New grammar points: results 到, 住, 走 (买到, 记住, 拿走); 上 after a verb (考上, 吃上, 爱上)

**Explanation (new part):**
L3-11.E1. 到 after a verb means you reached or got what you were after: 买到 'manage to buy', 找到 'find', 看到 'see', 听到 'hear'. 火车票你买到了没有？ 'Did you manage to get the train tickets?'; 我找到我的手机了 'I have found my phone'. The negative uses 没: 我没买到票 'I did not manage to get a ticket'.
L3-11.E2. 住 after a verb means it stays put: 记住 'remember (for good)'. 走 after a verb means away: 拿走 'take away', 飞走 'fly away'.
L3-11.E3. 上 after a verb can mean reaching a goal, often after effort or waiting: 考上大学 'get into university'; 我们终于吃上饭了 'We finally got to eat'; 住上新房子 'get to live in a new home'. 爱上 means 'fall in love with'.

**Examples (new):**
L3-11.X1. 火车票你买到了没有？  (Huǒchē piào nǐ mǎi dào le méiyǒu?)  — Did you manage to get the train tickets?
L3-11.X2. 请记住这个号码。  (Qǐng jì zhù zhège hàomǎ.)  — Please remember this number.
L3-11.X3. 谁把我的书拿走了？  (Shéi bǎ wǒ de shū ná zǒu le?)  — Who took my book away?
L3-11.X4. 他考上大学了。  (Tā kǎo shàng dàxué le.)  — He got into university.

**"Watch out" notes (new):**
L3-11.M1. For 'did not manage to' use 没 + verb + 到: 我没买到票, not 我不买到票.
L3-11.M2. For getting into a school use 考上: 考上大学.

**Lesson exercises (new):**
