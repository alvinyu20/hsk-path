## P10. HSK 5 · No hot water (p5-no-hot-water)
A woman calls her landlord about the hot water.

1. 女：喂，您好，我家从昨天晚上开始就一直没有热水。  (Wèi, nín hǎo, wǒ jiā cóng zuótiān wǎnshang kāishǐ jiù yìzhí méiyǒu rèshuǐ.)
   - Hello. There has been no hot water in my flat since last night.
2. 男：是不是没电了？你看看上面的灯亮不亮。  (Shì bú shì méi diàn le? Nǐ kànkan shàngmiàn de dēng liàng bú liàng.)

5. 女：那您什么时候能找人来修？天这么冷，没有热水实在太不方便了。  (Nà nín shénme shíhou néng zhǎo rén lái xiū? Tiān zhème lěng, méiyǒu rèshuǐ shízài tài bù fāngbiàn le.)
   - So when can you get someone to fix it? In this cold weather, having no hot water is really inconvenient.
6. 男：我下午就联系修理的师傅，不过他最早可能明天上午才能来。  (Wǒ xiàwǔ jiù liánxì xiūlǐ de shīfu, búguò tā zuì zǎo kěnéng míngtiān shàngwǔ cái néng lái.)
   - I will contact the repairman this afternoon, but the earliest he can come is probably tomorrow morning.
7. 女：明天上午我要上班，家里没人。  (Míngtiān shàngwǔ wǒ yào shàngbān, jiā lǐ méi rén.)
   - I am at work tomorrow morning, so nobody will be home.
8. 男：要不这样，你把钥匙放在楼下的邻居家，我陪师傅一起去。  (Yàobú zhèyàng, nǐ bǎ yàoshi fàng zài lóuxià de línjū jiā, wǒ péi shīfu yìqǐ qù.)
   - How about this: leave your key with the neighbour downstairs, and I will go with the repairman.
9. 女：可以。如果修不好，能不能换个新的？  (Kěyǐ. Rúguǒ xiū bù hǎo, néng bù néng huàn ge xīn de?)

   - If it does not get fixed, I will just buy a new one and pay for it myself. Don't you worry.

## P11. HSK 5 · Before the big race (p5-long-race)
A woman has hurt her foot while training for a race.

1. 男：你走路怎么这么慢？腿怎么了？  (Nǐ zǒulù zěnme zhème màn? Tuǐ zěnme le?)
   - Why are you walking so slowly? What happened to your leg?
2. 女：别提了，上周跑步的时候不小心把脚弄伤了。  (Bié tí le, shàngzhōu pǎobù de shíhou bù xiǎoxīn bǎ jiǎo nòng shāng le.)
   - Don't ask. I hurt my foot by accident while running last week.
3. 男：你还在为下个月那场四十二公里的比赛做准备？  (Nǐ hái zài wèi xià ge yuè nà chǎng sìshí'èr gōnglǐ de bǐsài zuò zhǔnbèi?)
   - Are you still getting ready for that 42-kilometre race next month?
4. 女：是啊，我每天早上跑十公里，周末跑二十公里，可能是练得太多了。  (Shì a, wǒ měitiān zǎoshang pǎo shí gōnglǐ, zhōumò pǎo èrshí gōnglǐ, kěnéng shì liàn de tài duō le.)

5. 男：医生怎么说？  (Yīshēng zěnme shuō?)
   - What did the doctor say?
6. 女：医生说问题不大，但是这两个星期最好别跑了，不然会越来越严重。  (Yīshēng shuō wèntí bú dà, dànshì zhè liǎng ge xīngqī zuìhǎo bié pǎo le, bùrán huì yuè lái yuè yánzhòng.)
   - The doctor said it is nothing serious, but I had better not run for the next two weeks, or it will get worse and worse.
7. 男：那比赛还能参加吗？  (Nà bǐsài hái néng cānjiā ma?)
   - So can you still take part in the race?
8. 女：我也不知道。如果休息两个星期，最后只剩十几天，肯定跑不出好成绩。  (Wǒ yě bù zhīdào. Rúguǒ xiūxi liǎng ge xīngqī, zuìhòu zhǐ shèng shí jǐ tiān, kěndìng pǎo bù chū hǎo chéngjì.)
   - I don't know. If I rest for two weeks, there will only be about ten days left, so I certainly won't get a good time.
9. 男：成绩不是最重要的，身体才是。这次就当是去试一试，能跑完就很好了。  (Chéngjì bú shì zuì zhòngyào de, shēntǐ cái shì. Zhè cì jiù dāng shì qù shì yi shì, néng pǎo wán jiù hěn hǎo le.)
   - Your time is not what matters most; your health is. Treat this one as a try. Finishing would be great.
10. 女：你说得有道理，我不应该太着急。  (Nǐ shuō de yǒu dàolǐ, wǒ bù yīnggāi tài zháojí.)
   - You have a point. I shouldn't be in such a hurry.
