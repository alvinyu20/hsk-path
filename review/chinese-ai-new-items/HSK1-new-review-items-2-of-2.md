Hello! I am building an HSK 1 study site (learners know HSK 1 to 0 and are now learning HSK 1). Below are the NEW practice items I just added: grammar review questions, "write your own sentence" prompts with a model answer, and listening clips. Please act as a native speaker and Chinese teacher and check them.
**Report only the problems**, no praise and no repeating good items. For each problem give: number / the item / what is wrong (simple Chinese plus one line of English) / a more natural version (using only HSK 1 to 1 words).
Please watch for: 1) unnatural or non-idiomatic wording; 2) grammar mistakes; 3) English that does not match the Chinese; 4) written style taught as spoken (or the reverse); 5) words too hard for HSK 1; 6) wrong pinyin (words with more than one reading, neutral tones, spaces between words); 7) in multiple-choice items, the "wrong" option must really be wrong (not another way that also works); 8) in "spot the extra word" items, the sentence without the word must be correct and the sentence with it must really be wrong; 9) the two "what to listen for" notes under each clip (tones, tone changes, readings, light syllables) must be accurate.

### g1-give  Giving and asking: 给 and 问  (lesson: Two actions in a row, and giving)

A166. **choose**. English: Mom gives me an apple.
   - right: 妈妈给我一个苹果。  (Māma gěi wǒ yí ge píngguǒ.)
   - wrong: 妈妈一个苹果给我。
   - note shown to the learner: 给 + person + thing.
A167. **fill**. 我问___一个问题。  (Wǒ wèn tā yí ge wèntí.)
   - English: I ask him a question.
   - blank: 他
   - note shown to the learner: 问 + person + question.
A168. **build**. 我给朋友打电话。  (Wǒ gěi péngyou dǎ diànhuà.)
   - English: I call my friend.
   - note shown to the learner: 给 + person + verb.
A169. **spot**. sentence with the extra word: 哥哥给我是两本书。
   - extra word: 是
   - correct sentence: 哥哥给我两本书。  (Gēge gěi wǒ liǎng běn shū.)
   - English: My older brother gives me two books.
   - note shown to the learner: Do not add 是 after 给 + person.

## C. Listening clips

Each clip is read aloud; the learner types what they hear. Two notes under it say what to listen for.

C1. 我家有四口人。  (Wǒ jiā yǒu sì kǒu rén.)
   - English: There are four people in my family.
   - note 1: 四口人 sì kǒu rén: a fall, a dip, then a rise.
   - note 2: 家 jiā is high and flat: 我家 wǒ jiā.
C2. 你妈妈在家吗？  (Nǐ māma zài jiā ma?)
   - English: Is your mom at home?
   - note 1: 你 nǐ is a third tone before the high tone 妈, so it only dips.
   - note 2: 妈妈 māma: the second 妈 is light and short.
C3. 我想喝一杯水。  (Wǒ xiǎng hē yì bēi shuǐ.)
   - English: I want to drink a glass of water.
   - note 1: 我想 wǒ xiǎng: two third tones in a row, so 我 sounds like wó.
   - note 2: 一 becomes yì before the high tone 杯: 一杯 yì bēi.
C4. 他不在家，他在学校。  (Tā bú zài jiā, tā zài xuéxiào.)
   - English: He is not at home; he is at school.
   - note 1: 不在 bú zài: 不 changes to bú before the fourth tone 在.
   - note 2: 学校 xuéxiào: a rise, then a fall.
C5. 这个包子很好吃。  (Zhège bāozi hěn hǎochī.)
   - English: This bun is very tasty.
   - note 1: 很好 hěn hǎo: two third tones, so 很 sounds like hén.
   - note 2: 这个 zhège: 个 is light and short.
C6. 我有两个哥哥。  (Wǒ yǒu liǎng ge gēge.)
   - English: I have two older brothers.
   - note 1: 我有 wǒ yǒu: two third tones in a row, so 我 sounds like wó.
   - note 2: 哥哥 gēge: the second 哥 is light and short.
C7. 你今天忙吗？  (Nǐ jīntiān máng ma?)
   - English: Are you busy today?
   - note 1: 你 nǐ is a third tone before the high tone 今, so it only dips.
   - note 2: 忙 máng rises, and 吗 ma is light.
C8. 我们明天去商店。  (Wǒmen míngtiān qù shāngdiàn.)
   - English: We are going to the shop tomorrow.
   - note 1: 我们 wǒmen: 们 is light and short.
   - note 2: 商店 shāngdiàn: high and flat, then a fall.
C9. 他今年十八岁。  (Tā jīnnián shíbā suì.)
   - English: He is eighteen this year.
   - note 1: 十八 shíbā: a rise, then high and flat.
   - note 2: 岁 suì falls: 十八岁 shíbā suì.
C10. 请你坐这儿。  (Qǐng nǐ zuò zhèr.)
   - English: Please sit here.
   - note 1: 请你 qǐng nǐ: two third tones in a row, so 请 sounds like qíng.
   - note 2: 这儿 zhèr is one beat: the 儿 merges into the syllable.
C11. 我昨天没去学校。  (Wǒ zuótiān méi qù xuéxiào.)
   - English: I did not go to school yesterday.
   - note 1: 没 méi rises: 没去 méi qù.
   - note 2: 昨天 zuótiān: a rise, then high and flat.
C12. 你喜欢吃面包吗？  (Nǐ xǐhuan chī miànbāo ma?)
   - English: Do you like eating bread?
   - note 1: 你喜欢 nǐ xǐhuan: two third tones in a row, so 你 sounds like ní.
   - note 2: 面包 miànbāo: a fall, then high and flat.
C13. 妈妈在做饭呢。  (Māma zài zuòfàn ne.)
   - English: Mom is cooking.
   - note 1: 呢 ne is light and short at the end.
   - note 2: 做饭 zuòfàn: both syllables fall.
C14. 这个杯子多少钱？  (Zhège bēizi duōshǎo qián?)
   - English: How much is this cup?
   - note 1: 多少 duōshǎo: high and flat, then a dip.
   - note 2: 钱 qián rises at the end.
C15. 我哥哥会开车。  (Wǒ gēge huì kāichē.)
   - English: My older brother can drive.
   - note 1: 我哥哥 wǒ gēge: 我 only dips before the high tone 哥.
   - note 2: 开车 kāichē: both syllables are high and flat.
C16. 你想在哪儿吃饭？  (Nǐ xiǎng zài nǎr chī fàn?)
   - English: Where do you want to eat?
   - note 1: 哪儿 nǎr is one beat, with the 儿 merged in.
   - note 2: 想在 xiǎng zài: 想 only dips before the falling tone 在.
C17. 他是我的老师，也是我的朋友。  (Tā shì wǒ de lǎoshī, yě shì wǒ de péngyou.)
   - English: He is my teacher, and also my friend.
   - note 1: 也是 yě shì: 也 is a third tone before a fall, so it only dips.
   - note 2: 朋友 péngyou: the last syllable is light.
C18. 我在商店买了两件衣服。  (Wǒ zài shāngdiàn mǎi le liǎng jiàn yīfu.)
   - English: I bought two pieces of clothing at the shop.
   - note 1: 了 le is light and quick after 买 mǎi.
   - note 2: 衣服 yīfu: the second syllable is light.
C19. 你昨天晚上几点睡觉？  (Nǐ zuótiān wǎnshang jǐ diǎn shuìjiào?)
   - English: What time did you go to sleep last night?
   - note 1: 几点 jǐ diǎn: two third tones, so 几 sounds like jí.
   - note 2: 晚上 wǎnshang: 上 is light and short.
C20. 今天太热了，我想喝水。  (Jīntiān tài rè le, wǒ xiǎng hē shuǐ.)
   - English: It is too hot today; I want to drink water.
   - note 1: 太热 tài rè: both syllables fall.
   - note 2: 了 le is light at the end of 太热了.
C21. 请问，你是中国人吗？  (Qǐngwèn, nǐ shì Zhōngguó rén ma?)
   - English: Excuse me, are you Chinese?
   - note 1: 中国人 Zhōngguó rén: high and flat, rising, then rising again.
   - note 2: 你是 nǐ shì: 你 only dips before the falling tone 是.
C22. 我们都喜欢看电影。  (Wǒmen dōu xǐhuan kàn diànyǐng.)
   - English: We all like watching films.
   - note 1: 都 dōu is high and flat; it means all and sits before the verb: 都喜欢.
   - note 2: 电影 diànyǐng: a fall, then a dip.
C23. 弟弟正在看电视。  (Dìdi zhèngzài kàn diànshì.)
   - English: My younger brother is watching TV.
   - note 1: 弟弟 dìdi: the second 弟 is light and short.
   - note 2: 正在 zhèngzài: both syllables fall.
C24. 你在做什么？  (Nǐ zài zuò shénme?)
   - English: What are you doing?
   - note 1: 做什么 zuò shénme: a fall, a rise, then a light 么.
   - note 2: 在 zài is a quick fall before the verb.
