## Speaking scripts

1. 您好，请问我可以帮您做什么？  /  Hello, what can I do to help you?
2. 好的。您还留着买东西时的小票吗？  /  Okay. Have you kept the receipt from when you bought it?
3. 我们可以给您换一个新的，也可以把钱退给您。您想怎么办？  /  We can give you a new one, or we can give you your money back. What would you like?
4. 好的，请您在这里签一下名，钱很快就会退给您。  /  Okay, please sign here, and the money will be returned to you soon.
5. 不用，卡里的钱超过一百块就不收费。  /  No. If there is over a hundred yuan on your card, there is no charge.
6. 太好了！你对哪种活动比较有兴趣？我们的活动有照顾老人、教孩子学习，还有整理大家捐来的旧书。  /  Great! Which kind of activity are you more interested in? We have looking after old people, teaching children, and sorting the old books people have donated.
