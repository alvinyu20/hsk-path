## C. Listening clips

Each clip is read aloud; the learner types what they hear. Two notes under it say what to listen for.

C1. 他在银行工作，每天都很忙。  (Tā zài yínháng gōngzuò, měitiān dōu hěn máng.)
   - English: He works in a bank and is very busy every day.
   - note 1: 银行 yínháng: both syllables rise; 行 is said háng here (a bank), not xíng.
   - note 2: 工作 gōngzuò: a high tone, then a fall.
C2. 我明天要把这本书还给他。  (Wǒ míngtiān yào bǎ zhè běn shū huángěi tā.)
   - English: Tomorrow I will return this book to him.
   - note 1: 还 huán: said huán here (give back), a rise, not hái.
   - note 2: 明天 míngtiān: a rise, then a high tone.
C3. 我们要重新开始，这次一定能成功。  (Wǒmen yào chóngxīn kāishǐ, zhè cì yídìng néng chénggōng.)
   - English: We must start again, and this time we will surely succeed.
   - note 1: 重新 chóngxīn: 重 is said chóng (again), a rise; then a high tone.
   - note 2: 成功 chénggōng: a rise, then a high tone.
C4. 她从小就爱好音乐，现在是一名老师。  (Tā cóngxiǎo jiù àihào yīnyuè, xiànzài shì yì míng lǎoshī.)
   - English: She has loved music since she was little, and now she is a teacher.
   - note 1: 爱好 àihào: 好 is said hào here (to like), so both syllables fall.
   - note 2: 一名 yì míng: 一 is said yì (falling) before the rising 名.
C5. 我想了解一下这个城市的历史。  (Wǒ xiǎng liǎojiě yíxià zhège chéngshì de lìshǐ.)
   - English: I want to learn a bit about the history of this city.
   - note 1: 了解 liǎojiě: two third tones in a row, so 了 rises (say liáojiě).
   - note 2: 历史 lìshǐ: a fall, then a dip.
C6. 这件衣服太长了，我想换一件。  (Zhè jiàn yīfu tài cháng le, wǒ xiǎng huàn yí jiàn.)
   - English: This piece of clothing is too long; I want to change it for another.
   - note 1: 长 cháng: said cháng (long), a rise.
   - note 2: 换一件 huàn yí jiàn: 一 is said yí (rising) before the falling 件.
C7. 小狗长得很快，现在已经很重了。  (Xiǎo gǒu zhǎng de hěn kuài, xiànzài yǐjīng hěn zhòng le.)
   - English: The puppy grows very fast and is already very heavy now.
   - note 1: 长 zhǎng: said zhǎng (to grow), a dip.
   - note 2: 重 zhòng: said zhòng (heavy), a fall.
C8. 请问，我怎么去火车站？  (Qǐngwèn, wǒ zěnme qù huǒchēzhàn?)
   - English: Excuse me, how do I get to the train station?
   - note 1: 请问 qǐngwèn: 请 only dips before the falling 问.
   - note 2: 怎么 zěnme: 么 is light and short.
C9. 今天的作业太多了，我要做到很晚。  (Jīntiān de zuòyè tài duō le, wǒ yào zuò dào hěn wǎn.)
   - English: There is too much homework today; I have to work until very late.
   - note 1: 作业 zuòyè: both syllables fall.
   - note 2: 很晚 hěn wǎn: two third tones in a row, so 很 rises (say hén wǎn).
C10. 我一般七点起床，八点出门。  (Wǒ yìbān qī diǎn qǐchuáng, bā diǎn chūmén.)
   - English: I usually get up at 7 and go out at 8.
   - note 1: 一般 yìbān: 一 is said yì (falling) before the high 般.
   - note 2: 起床 qǐchuáng: 起 only dips before the rising 床.
C11. 这个办法不错，我们可以试一试。  (Zhège bànfǎ búcuò, wǒmen kěyǐ shì yí shì.)
   - English: This way is not bad; we can try it.
   - note 1: 不错 búcuò: 不 is said bú before the falling 错.
   - note 2: 试一试 shì yi shì: 一 is light and short between the two verbs.
C12. 请大家安静，现在开始考试。  (Qǐng dàjiā ānjìng, xiànzài kāishǐ kǎoshì.)
   - English: Everyone, please be quiet; the exam starts now.
   - note 1: 安静 ānjìng: a high tone, then a fall.
   - note 2: 考试 kǎoshì: 考 only dips before the falling 试.
C13. 他的声音很小，我听不清楚。  (Tā de shēngyīn hěn xiǎo, wǒ tīng bù qīngchu.)
   - English: His voice is very quiet, and I do not hear it clearly.
   - note 1: 声音 shēngyīn: both syllables are high and flat.
   - note 2: 清楚 qīngchu: the second syllable is light and short.
C14. 我打算先去超市，然后回家做饭。  (Wǒ dǎsuàn xiān qù chāoshì, ránhòu huí jiā zuòfàn.)
   - English: I plan to go to the supermarket first, and then go home to cook.
   - note 1: 打算 dǎsuàn: 打 only dips before the falling 算.
   - note 2: 超市 chāoshì: a high tone, then a fall.
C15. 你的建议很有用，谢谢你。  (Nǐ de jiànyì hěn yǒuyòng, xièxie nǐ.)
   - English: Your suggestion is very useful; thank you.
   - note 1: 建议 jiànyì: both syllables fall.
   - note 2: 谢谢 xièxie: the second syllable is light and short.
C16. 这里的空气比以前好多了。  (Zhèlǐ de kōngqì bǐ yǐqián hǎoduō le.)
   - English: The air here is much better than before.
   - note 1: 空气 kōngqì: a high tone, then a fall.
   - note 2: 比以前 bǐ yǐqián: two third tones in a row, so 比 rises (say bí yǐqián).
C17. 医生说我应该多休息，少喝咖啡。  (Yīshēng shuō wǒ yīnggāi duō xiūxi, shǎo hē kāfēi.)
   - English: The doctor says I should rest more and drink less coffee.
   - note 1: 应该 yīnggāi: both syllables are high and flat.
   - note 2: 休息 xiūxi: the second syllable is light and short.
C18. 上个星期的旅行，我们玩得非常开心。  (Shàng ge xīngqī de lǚxíng, wǒmen wán de fēicháng kāixīn.)
   - English: On last week's trip, we had a great time.
   - note 1: 旅行 lǚxíng: 旅 only dips before the rising 行 (said xíng, not háng).
   - note 2: 开心 kāixīn: both syllables are high and flat.
C19. 他的房间很干净，东西也放得很好。  (Tā de fángjiān hěn gānjìng, dōngxi yě fàng de hěn hǎo.)
   - English: His room is very clean, and things are put away well too.
