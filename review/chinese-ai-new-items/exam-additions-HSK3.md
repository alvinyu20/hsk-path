Hello! These are new mock-exam questions (HSK 3) from my study site; you have not seen them. Learners know HSK 1 to 3.
Please act as a native speaker and Chinese teacher. **Report only problems**, no praise. For each problem give: number / the item / what is wrong (simple Chinese plus one line of English) / a better version (HSK 1 to 3 words only).
Please watch for: 1) unnatural wording; 2) grammar mistakes; 3) English that does not match the Chinese; 4) **whether the answer marked ✓ is the only right one**, or another option could also be right; 5) whether a true/false item is judged correctly; 6) for ordering items, whether another order is also correct; 7) words too hard for HSK 3 (judge by the new HSK 3.0 (2021) lists).
If there are no problems, answer "No problems found".

   女：累不累？  /  Are you tired?
   男：有点儿累，但是很高兴。  /  A bit tired, but very happy.
   问题 / question: 男的周末做什么了？  /  What did the man do at the weekend?
