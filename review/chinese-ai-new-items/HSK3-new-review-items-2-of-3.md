### g3-35  duration + 没 + verb + 了  (lesson: How long: 学了两年 and 三年没见了)

A146. **choose**. English: I have not taken a shower for 3 days.
   - right: 我三天没洗澡了。  (Wǒ sān tiān méi xǐzǎo le.)
   - wrong: 我没三天洗澡了。
   - note shown to the learner: duration comes before 没.
A147. **fill**. 他两年___回家了。  (Tā liǎng nián méi huí jiā le.)
   - English: He has not been home for 2 years.
   - blank: 没
   - note shown to the learner: duration + 没 + verb + 了.
A148. **build**. 我们一个星期没见面了。  (Wǒmen yí ge xīngqī méi jiànmiàn le.)
   - English: We have not met for a week.
   - note shown to the learner: duration + 没 + verb + 了.
A149. **spot**. sentence with the extra word: 我一个月才没看电影了。
   - extra word: 才
   - correct sentence: 我一个月没看电影了。  (Wǒ yí ge yuè méi kàn diànyǐng le.)
   - English: I have not seen a film for a month.
   - note shown to the learner: Do not add 才; 没 … 了 says how long something has not happened.
